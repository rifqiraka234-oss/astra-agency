# Panorama Studios — competitive map

**Researched:** 2026-09-14, from their public site (panoramastudios.nl, 138 pages).

## Headline

**They are not "a website builder." They are a full-service agency, and they own
Westland horticulture.** Their own tagline for the sector page is *"Eén loket voor
al je marketing behoeften"* — one desk for all your marketing needs, with an
in-house team of specialists and 17+ years in tuinbouwtechniek.

This materially changes our approach. Several things Raka wanted to put in the
proposal are squarely inside Panorama's core offering.

## What they actually sell — four studios

| Studio | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Communicatie Studio** | Brand naming, positioning, proposition, communication strategy and advice |
| **Design Studio** | Corporate identity, brand guides, packaging design, all brand assets |
| **Web Studio** | Webdesign, custom builds, **Umbraco CMS**, SEO structure, AVG/WCAG compliance, plus a **content studio** that will run your site content for you |
| **Online Marketing Studio** | Online marketing strategy, **social media**, **SEO & SEA**, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, apps |

Plus a dedicated **Leadinfo lead-generation** offering: visitor company
identification, **automated follow-up by email or into your own CRM**, retargeting
ads, and 70+ integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho).

## Their horticulture grip

Portfolio includes **Dutch Greenhouses, KUBO, Van der Hoeven, Lans, Nature's Pride,
KasGroeit, Combivliet, Noordam Plants, Delta Fresh, Superflora, Origin Fruit Group,
De Tuinderij, Versland**, and **Gemeente Westland** (twice, including a municipal
literacy campaign). They have sector landing pages for `/agf` and `/tuinbouwtechniek`
and a `/digivoucher-westland` page for regional subsidy work.

Several of those names — KUBO, Van der Hoeven, Lans — are the same tier of company
that pays Tomato World's partner fees. **Panorama and Tomato World share a customer
base.** That is why the site was built free or cheap, and it is why Ank said she
will not risk the friendship.

## Verdict: where we must NOT go

| Service | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Branding, identity, brand guide | 🔴 **Red** | Design Studio core. Head-on. |
| Social media strategy & content | 🔴 **Red** | Online Marketing Studio core. |
| SEO / SEA / Google & LinkedIn Ads | 🔴 **Red** | Explicit service. |
| Campaign email marketing, newsletters | 🔴 **Red** | Covered via Leadinfo + marketing automation. |
| CRM selection and connection | 🟠 **Amber** | They integrate CRMs already. Only touch it *downstream of a booking system we build*. |
| Website redesign / visual | 🟠 **Amber** | Web Studio core. Only as a joint engagement, with them leading visual. |
| Information architecture / UX restructure | 🟠 **Amber** | Defensible as a research-led service, but must be offered *with* them. |
| Copywriting and content production | 🔴 **Red** | They have a content studio. |

**Raka's instinct to widen into social, branding and email marketing goes straight
into their strongest studios.** Widening the menu is right; widening it in *that*
direction would make us a direct competitor in the exact sector they dominate, and
would likely end the deal via Ank.

## Where the white space actually is

Panorama sell **communication**. Nothing on their 138 pages offers:

- a booking or reservation engine
- capacity, scheduling, or availability logic
- a customer portal or admin panel
- internal workflow automation or back-office tooling
- systems integration beyond installing off-the-shelf tools
- data products or reporting built from a client's own operations
- **any AI capability whatsoever** — the word does not appear in their services

Astra's actual track record is precisely this: admin panels, storefronts, a
1000-product import automation, a payment app, a gym engagement app. **That is the
complement, not the overlap.**

## The positioning line

> **Panorama owns the brand and the front door. Astra builds the machine behind it.**

They make Tomato World look and sound right. We make Tomato World *run* — bookings,
capacity, partner value, the back office. Marketing agency and operations partner.
Different budget line, different buyer, no competitive collision.

This is also the honest pitch to Panorama themselves: we make their website more
valuable by adding function they do not sell, and we never touch their design,
their brand work, or their marketing retainer.

## One live defect worth using

`/en/guided-tours` currently shouts:

> **"WE ARE CURRENTLY IN OUR CROP ROTATION PERIOD NO GUIDED TOURS AVAILABLE UNTIL 1 SEPTEMBER 2026"**

Today is **14 September 2026**. The blackout notice is **two weeks stale and is
actively telling every visitor that tours are unavailable.** It is hardcoded page
copy, so someone has to remember to delete it.

This is the single best argument for the booking module, and it costs nothing to
raise: seasonal closures should be a date rule in a system, not a sentence someone
forgets to remove. Raise it gently and as a favour — it is a content slip, not a
Panorama build fault.
