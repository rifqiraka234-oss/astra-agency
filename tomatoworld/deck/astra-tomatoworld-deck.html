Five things a website can't fix

Because they aren't about the website at all.

01

Booking is all by hand

Every visit starts with one long form. No calendar to pick from, no way to pay online. Your team checks the guide, the language, the room and the season by hand, then sends an invoice or waits on a bank transfer. Across five languages and thousands of visitors, that's days of emails per group.

Falls on → Joyce & the reservations team

02

Your best leads walk out

Growers, tech companies and delegations tour the greenhouse every week. Nothing catches them or turns them into a partner chat, so the warmest leads for your best income line leave without a trace.

Feeds → new partners

03

Your best proof stays hidden

You're the field lab for live-data growing, 5G and robots, yet none of that live crop or sensor data shows up online. The most convincing thing you own never leaves the greenhouse.

Your edge → unused online

04

Partners never see their value

Thirty-plus partners like Koppert and Rijk Zwaan pay to be seen and to test in a live crop. But nobody shows them what they got, visits hosted, people reached, so every renewal rests on goodwill, not proof.

Protects → your biggest income line

05

Nothing remembers a visitor

No shared contact list, no automatic follow-up. A delegation that could come back, a school that could rebook, a visitor who could refer you, none get a nudge, because nothing remembers they were here.

Costs → repeat visits & referrals
