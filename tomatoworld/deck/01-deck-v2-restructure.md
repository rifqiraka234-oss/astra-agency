**Source:** `ASTRA_Tomatoworld_Direction_One_Pager.pdf`, July 2026
(Drive id `1p5oyxgEnnrGQqXSryKOcsR6LuW8jjQtx`), plus `preview-home.png` and
`preview-mobile.png` — the clickable concept Ank tried to click in the call.

**Why a v2:** the July version was written from public information, before we had
spoken to anyone. The 14 September call changed three things fundamentally. This is a
reshuffle, not a rewrite — the spine holds up well.

---

## What the July version got right (keep)

- **"One story. Three clear routes into Tomatoworld."** Strong, and it is the exact
  answer to Ank's complaint: *"Am I a visitor? Am I a partner? You know, I got lost."*
  **Keep this as the headline. Do not touch it.**
- **Experience it / Test it / Build it together.** Clean, goal-based, memorable.
  Ank will repeat these three words to Art. **Keep verbatim.**
- The numbered 01–04 structure and the visual language. Recognisable, and she has
  already seen it. Changing the format would cost us continuity.
- The honest closing caveat about validating before production. That line is part of
  why she trusted us.
- The clickable concept. **This is the single most valuable asset we have** and it is
  currently just two screenshots.

| Issue | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|

# Proposed v2 structure

**Format:** stay a one-pager, or extend to two pages maximum. Ank said she will print
it and take it to Art. **It must survive being printed in black and white.**

---

### HEADER — unchanged

> **TOMATOWORLD / DIGITAL DIRECTION · ASTRA AGENCY · SEPTEMBER 2026**
>
> **One story. Three clear routes into Tomatoworld.**

Subtitle, updated:

> A researched direction for the visitor experience, fieldlab and partner ecosystem,
> now shaped by what we learned in conversation with Ank.

*The subtitle change matters: it signals this version is built on their input, not our
assumptions.*

---

### 01 — What we heard *(replaces "What we did")*

This is the most important change in the document. **Lead with their words, not our
work.** It proves we listened, and it gives Ank the summary she needs for Art.

> - Coordinating tours is the sharpest daily cost. A request becomes an email
>   conversation that one person holds together, and every cancellation restarts it.
> - The website does not separate a visitor from a partner, so it asks people to work
>   out for themselves where to go.
> - Partners pay annually to be seen. That value is real, but it is described rather
>   than demonstrated.
> - Panorama Studios built the site and are trusted partners of the organisation.
>   Nothing here changes that.
> - Every step needs a number against it before Art and Joyce can decide.

---

### 02 — Our direction *(keep the three routes, add the fourth layer)*

Keep the existing three-route copy **exactly as written**. Then add:

> **And underneath the three routes: the system that runs them.**
>
> Clear routes only help if what happens next works. Today a tour request becomes
> manual coordination, seasonal closures are typed into the page by hand, and
> availability depends on which volunteer guide is free and which languages they
> speak. The same is true of space rental and specialist bookings, which are separate
> journeys on the site today.
>
> We would put a real booking process behind the routes, inside the site you already
> have.

*This is the pivot from "redesign" to "operations", without abandoning the work
already done.*

---

### 03 — Why this direction *(keep, add two findings)*

Keep the three existing reasons. Add:

> - The site runs on Umbraco, a modern content system. A booking process can sit
>   inside it without changing the look and feel, and without Panorama rebuilding
>   anything.
> - There are three separate bookable offers today, tours, space rental and specialist
>   bookings, each handled separately.

---

### 04 — Expected outcome *(keep, add the partner line)*

Keep all three existing outcomes. Add, and give it visual weight:

> - **Partner value made visible.** Visitor numbers, countries and hosted groups,
>   reported back to each partner annually, so a renewal conversation starts from
>   evidence rather than recollection.

*This is the only line in the document aimed at Art. Make it stand out.*

---

### 05 — NEW: Where to start

The section the July version had no way to include. **This is what makes the document
actionable rather than interesting.**

> **We would rather start small than ask for a large decision.**
>
> **First, three things at no cost.** Your guided tours page still says no tours are
> available until 1 September. The closure has passed, but the notice is written into
> the page. We will correct that, along with a cookie consent gap and a small search
> issue, whether or not anything else follows. We would route these through Panorama.
>
> **Then a two-week Discovery Sprint, at a fixed price.** You would receive a
> clickable prototype of the booking experience, a costed plan with firm prices, a
> measured picture of what coordination currently costs, and a technical approach
> agreed with Panorama Studios. **If you go no further, you keep all of it.**
>
> **After that, in pieces.** Every step below is separately priced and separately
> useful. Nothing requires committing to the next.

| Step | Indicative |
|---|---|
| Three fixes above | Free |
| Discovery Sprint, two weeks | €4,000 – €6,000 |
| Better tour request and automatic acknowledgement | from €1,500 |
| Live availability and automatic confirmations | from €4,000 |
| One partner value report, from existing records | from €2,000 |
| Tour presentation, rebuilt properly | from €1,200 |

> We are also checking whether the Westland Digivoucher could cover part of a first
> step. It is written for companies rather than foundations, so we are verifying
> whether a stichting qualifies before relying on it.

---

### 06 — NEW: How we work alongside Panorama Studios

**Short, plain, and unmissable.** Ank will show this page to Panorama. Write it for
them to read.

> Panorama Studios built your site and know your organisation. We are not proposing to
> replace any of that.
>
> They handle how Tomatoworld looks and sounds. We would build what runs underneath:
> booking, availability, partner reporting. Where our work becomes visible on the
> site, we would expect them to design it.
>
> A full redesign of your identity or website is their work, and we would say so.

---

### FOOTER — Who we are *(keep verbatim, one addition)*

Keep the existing ASTRA paragraph and "Grow. Optimise. Innovate." Add one line:

> We also work in branding, storytelling and social content. Where Panorama Studios
> already cover these for you, we would happily leave them there.

*Includes the brand and social offer without it reading as a land grab. If Ank
confirms Panorama are not retained for it, expand this in v3.*

Keep the closing caveat unchanged.
