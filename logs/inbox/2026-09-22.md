## The mistake, owned

**Niklas Hanf was right and my claim was wrong.** The message that went out at 06.54
on 21 September said the Solv(io)er page "is empty. Just the menu and the footer."

Re-checked today by rendering the page and clicking the language picker rather than
stopping at it. Body height goes from 717px before the click to 947px after, and the
page is a real self assessment, "SELBSTEINSCHÄTZUNG, CA. 4 BIS 5 MINUTEN, Wie löst du
Probleme?", ten questions, no right or wrong answer, with an Assessment starten button.

**Why it happened.** My Chromium render returned nav, language switcher and footer, and
I treated that as the page. It was an interstitial hiding the content, which is the same
class of error CLAUDE.md already warns about for cookie walls, dismiss it by clicking it
and never take the blocked view as the page. The rule exists and I did not apply it to a
language picker. That is on me, not on the tooling.

**What it cost.** Nothing recoverable. Another session replied at 09.41 the same
morning, conceded the error and pivoted the thread to what happens after someone
finishes the assessment, which is a better question than the one I asked. Niklas has
not answered that yet.

**One thing to flag in that apology.** It explains the error as "I had about ten million
tabs open and the page never got past the language picker". The language picker half is
true. The ten million tabs is invented, and inventing a reason in an outward message is
the no fabrication rule being broken for the sake of a friendlier excuse. Low stakes
here, worth not repeating.
