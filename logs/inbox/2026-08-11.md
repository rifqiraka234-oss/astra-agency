## How the Silent accepted list above was built
`search_campaign_leads` has no queryable connection-status field, so the
cross check was done by pulling all 513 leads across both campaigns with
`include: ["activities"]` (11 calls, limit 50 each) and checking each
lead's activity log for `linkedinInviteAccepted` with no personalized
follow-up (`linkedinSent` beyond the generic invite) and no
`linkedinReplied` since. That's a real gap in v0.1: it has no automated
second-touch step, so the personalized opener has always been a manual
send, and none of these 51 people were ever going to get one on their own.

This full scan is too expensive to repeat daily (see
`state/silent_accepted_queue.jsonl` and the note in `CLAUDE.md`), future
runs should only re-scan periodically (e.g. weekly, or once this batch is
cleared) rather than every day.

No openers have been drafted for the 51 above yet. The spec's quality bar
(genuine observation, a real structural gap, a named concept, no filler)
needs the same per-company research as the enrichment pipeline's Stage
1/2/4, so that's sized as its own batch job across future sessions.
