## The look, taken from the Astra PDF
Palette sampled from it. Navy 0D1B38, beige E6DDD8, steel blue 405B8D, near black 1D1D1E, off white FDFDFD.
Fonts embedded in that PDF are Solomon Sans (Fontfabric, commercial) for text and The Seasons (My Creative Land, commercial) for the serif headings. Not Sora or Cormorant.
Neither is on any free source, so none was downloaded. The PPTX names the real fonts, so it renders correctly on a machine that has them (Luna's and Raka's, where the original PPTX was made).
The preview PDF was rendered here with stand-ins, Figtree for Solomon Sans and DM Serif Display for The Seasons, picked by measured letter widths from the PDF's own embedded glyphs.
Google Slides can not hold these two fonts, so the deck stays a PowerPoint. The final PDF for Matthias should be exported from PowerPoint on a machine with the fonts.
Shapes and motifs from the PDF. Arch image frames, numbered circles, a vertical rule with a circle, beige cards on navy, full height photo panels, a bokeh strip.
