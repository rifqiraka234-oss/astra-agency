**Funding, NRW.** From IHK pages and one blog, and not yet checked against the NRW.BANK guideline.
- NRW.BANK.Impuls KI, new from 17 Aug 2026. A grant of 50 percent of eligible spend, up to 25,000 euros, with 5,000 euros of eligible
  spend as the minimum. It pays for preparing and trialling AI, not full production use. Open to SMEs based in NRW. A company can use
  it twice in two years. Wiehl is in NRW, so a trial of AI quote intake looks like a fit.
- MID Digitalisierung moved to NRW.BANK in early 2026 and, per the same sources, no longer pays for digitising internal processes.
  Max 15,000 euros at 50 percent for market ready digital products.
- Digital Jetzt closed, portal off since 31 Mar 2026. The BAFA consulting programme takes applications only until year end. KfW's ERP
  digitalisation loan is a loan, not a grant.
- Rules we don't know yet and must check before telling him anything. Whether the work may start before the application, whether the
  supplier has to be on an approved list, and whether an agency like Astra can be the supplier at all.
