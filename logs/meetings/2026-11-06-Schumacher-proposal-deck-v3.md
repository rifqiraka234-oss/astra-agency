# Schumacher proposal deck, rebuilt 6 Nov 2026 (third version). NOT SENT.

Raka's verdict on the 17 slide PowerPoint (v2): hard to understand, designs weak, no flow, "what are we recommending him". It was rebuilt from scratch.
The PPTX and preview PDF were delivered in the chat. Binary files are not committed here. The files sit in proposals/schumacher/ on the session machine only.

## What was wrong with v2
17 slides, small text, many boxes per slide, recommendation buried on slide 13, three different ways of showing the plan. The Astra PDF it was meant to follow uses one idea per slide, large serif headings and wide margins.

## The rule for the rebuild
One idea per slide. Body text 15 points or larger. A four step strip on every slide (1 What you told us, 2 What we found, 3 What we recommend, 4 Cost and next steps) so Matthias always knows where he is. The answer comes on slide 2, not at the end.

## The 13 slides
1 Cover. 2 The short version (all four steps, recommendation highlighted). 3 You told us four things. 4 Every request starts the same way (his form and the mixer PDF page, screenshots of 6 Nov). 5 Three places where quotes slip (buyers cannot find you, incomplete requests, nobody follows up, each tagged Phase 1 or 2).
6 We recommend you start with the calculator (staircase, phase 1 EUR 1,500, phases 2 and 3 priced later). 7 Fix the leaks first, then bring more buyers. 8 What the buyer sees (calculator screenshot, link to the demo). 9 What your engineers get (spec sheet screenshot). 10 Website and Beosys stay as they are (website, calculator, HubSpot). 11 Cost, 750 + 750 = 1,500, HubSpot licence extra. 12 Who you work with. 13 What happens next, Thu 12 Nov session.
