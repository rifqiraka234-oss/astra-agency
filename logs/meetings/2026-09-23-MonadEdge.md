He is a consultant selling operations and process work to the same SMEs Astra targets.
That makes him a **Build Squad or partnership** conversation, not a redesign prospect,
which is exactly what CLAUDE.md says about builders, consultants and technical founders.

The specific fit. He diagnoses process and operations problems for PMEs and then hands
over a recommendation. The internal tool that makes the recommendation real is the part
he cannot deliver himself. Astra has Dutch project management in front of a senior
Amwisesa build team with ten years of warehouse, retail, clinic and F&B systems behind
it, which is exactly the capability a solo operations consultant lacks.

Worth asking early. What happens after he hands over a process recommendation, who
builds the thing, and has he ever lost or turned down work because there was no build
capacity behind him.
