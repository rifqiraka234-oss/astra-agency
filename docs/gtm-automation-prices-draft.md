### Seller track. Sell more, with less manual work

**Who it's for.** AEs, SDRs, founders who still sell, and freelancers who sell their own work. You don't want a new job.
You want more meetings with the right people and less of your week spent copying, pasting and remembering.

**Why take it.** Reps lose 70% of their week to work that isn't selling (Salesforce State of Sales). This track gives that
time back with a small engine you run yourself in 2 to 3 hours a week.

| Level | What you learn | What you walk away with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Find | Your ICP, a ranked list, alerts that tell you who to contact now, and AI research you check yourself | A tiered list of 50 accounts, three live alerts, five accounts researched with a checked reason to reach out |
| 2 Reach | Messages about their problem, a sequence across LinkedIn and email, calls that end with a next step | A 3 step sequence live to at least 20 people, a call prep sheet, a follow up that goes out within 10 minutes |
| 3 Scale | Your scorecard, a weekly review, and AI that does your research and first drafts on every new account | A scorecard with 30 days of numbers against your starting point, and a research assistant running on your own list |

**Flag.** Seller is written today as one course of five modules (`docs/academy/gtm-seller.md`). Levels 1 and 2 map onto
modules 1 to 4. Level 3 needs new lessons for the AI research assistant part before it can be sold.

### Engineer track. Become a GTM engineer

**Who it's for.** People building a career in GTM engineering or RevOps. SDRs who want to own the tools, marketing ops
people, analysts, a founder's first hire, career switchers.

**Why take it.** GTM engineering and RevOps job postings rose 205% in 2025 against 2024 (Bloomberry). Companies are
hiring for a skill few people can show. Each level ends with a build you can put in front of an employer.

| Level | What you learn | What you walk away with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Operator | ICP, data and lists, enrichment and scoring in Clay, AI research that cites its sources, the message | A 200 account list, a scored and enriched engine, ten researched accounts, a passed practical test |
| 2 Builder | Sending domains and lemlist, n8n automations, CRM setup, lead to loyalty flows, dashboards | A full engine you built and ran live for two weeks, with a one page report of real numbers |
| 3 Architect | The whole revenue system on one page, AI agents in production, signal systems, running builds for clients | An architect capstone, an AI agent with a test set, and a portfolio for senior interviews |

### GTM Leader. Run a team that sells on a system

**Who it's for.** Heads of sales, sales directors and founders with a team of 3 to 30. You don't build the flows, you
decide what gets built and answer for the forecast.

**Why take it.** 84% of reps missed quota last year (Salesforce). A team that sells from memory can't be coached or
forecast. This course gives you the operating system.

**What you walk away with.** A sales bible your team wrote with you, an ICP and routing design, CRM rules with owners, a
dashboard and a forecast you can defend deal by deal, a win and loss review from real calls, and a 90 day plan that says
build, buy or hire. The 1:1 version includes a 90 minute workshop with your team.
