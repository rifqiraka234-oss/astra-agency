## The two arms

**A, SKETCH**, as it is today (`docs/opener-template.md`, top section).

```
Hi [name], [company] looks cool!

However, your [website, social media or the page] [what you saw]. [what it likely costs them].

I'd suggest [the project].

At Astra agency we make websites for brands like Unilever, AXA, Pertamina.

```

**B, SKETCHB**, SKETCH without the brand line and with the short ask (Raka's opening, 26 Oct).

```
Hi [name], [company] looks cool.

However, [what you saw]. [what it likely costs them].

I'd suggest [the project].

Shall I send you a sketch?
```

What B changes. No brand line, a shorter ask, a 95 word cap and a full stop instead of the "!". The 25 Oct plan also
changed the opening to "thanks for connecting" and a line in their own words. Raka took that out on 26 Oct.

B's rules, all checked by tools/check-drafts.py.
- Four blocks, 45 to 95 words.
- Block one is exactly `Hi [name], [company] looks cool.` with a full stop.
- Block two opens "However", sentences of 30 words max, and the cost worded as likely, probably or usually.
- Block three opens "I'd suggest ".
- Block four is `Shall I send you a sketch?` or `Shall I send you a sketch of [thing]?`, 14 words max.
- No "Unilever" line, and no "!" anywhere.

**Both arms keep everything else.** The full research gate, `worth:` at 2,500 euros or more (RULES 0E), `owner reply:`
(RULES 0F), the research failure checks (RULES 0G), the thread pulls, Raka's word before every send, one 👀 bump after
3 days, then a silent close.

## Who gets which

**The contactId decides, never a person.** `variant_for()` in tools/check-drafts.py takes sha256 of the contactId and
uses A for even, B for odd. Across all 541 contacts that's 266 A and 275 B. Of the 84 still waiting for a first message
on 25 Oct, 38 are A and 46 are B.

- From any drafts file dated 2026-10-26 or later, check-drafts fails a first message written in the wrong arm, and
  fails one without `variant: A` or `variant: B` in its gate.
- So nobody picks the leads that look likely to say yes for the version they like. That's the whole point of a test.
- **Outside the test.** The warm SKETCH (`Something I've been thinking about, [name]!`), the AI workflow "did you know"
  variant, and every reply, nudge, bump and closer. Those keep their own shapes.
- **Raka's angle calls are inside it.** When he names the angle ("website to get more members"), the angle stays his,
  only the shape follows the arm.

## What's measured

- **Main.** A yes within 7 days of the first message, to the sketch, a call or a look. A yes after the bump counts,
  because the bump is part of both arms.
- **Also.** Any reply within 7 days. Replies that correct a fact of ours. Hostile replies (stop, spam, annoyed).
  Meetings booked within 21 days.
- **Who labels.** A person reads every reply and labels it. A regex never decides what a yes is. Labels go in
  `state/ab_2026-10-26_labels.jsonl`, one row per contact.

```
{"contactId": "ctc_...", "label": "yes", "correction": false, "hostile": false, "note": "asked for the sketch"}
```

**Count it with `python3 tools/ab-count.py`.** It reads the test drafts, pulls every lead's whole thread from lemlist
with the control first, counts a lead only when its drafted message is actually in the thread, and prints every reply in
the 7 day window with its label or UNLABELLED. Proven 25 Oct against Sebastian's and Beckie's sent openers (both found)
and a B rewrite that was never sent (correctly left out).

## When it ends, and what decides it

- **Read it at 30 per arm with the 7 day window closed, or on 30 Nov, whichever comes first.**
- **The honest limit.** At these rates, 30 per arm gives about 3 yeses each side. Proving 9% against 16% statistically
  would take roughly 350 per arm, which is more first messages than we'll send this year. So the result is a direction
  for Raka to decide on, not proof.
- **The suggested reading.**
  - B ahead on yeses, with no more corrections or hostile replies than A, means B becomes the first message.
  - A ahead by two yeses or more means keep SKETCH and drop B.
  - Anything closer means run another 30 each before deciding.
- Raka makes the call. Whoever reads the result writes it at the bottom of this file, with the counts from
  ab-count.py and the date.
