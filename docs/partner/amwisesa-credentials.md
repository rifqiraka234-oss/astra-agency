## Read this before quoting anything from the PDF

1. **The deck is branded "amw" and never spells out "Amwisesa".** Raka gave it to us as the
   partner's credentials, and the Astra deck credits "JM Ventures x Amwisesa", so amw is
   Amwisesa. Name them as Amwisesa in anything we write.
2. **The text layer is scrambled.** The fonts are subset with a character shift, so copying
   text out of the PDF gives gibberish like `&OLHQW` for "Client". Everything below was read
   off the rendered pages and decoded, not copied. Page numbers are PDF page numbers.
3. **Much of this is agency work.** Several projects list an agency as the client (Arcade
   Indonesia for Unilever, Phibious for Gudang Garam, Media Wave for MWX). The brand is the
   end client, and the agency hired Amwisesa. Say "built for Unilever", never "Unilever hired
   us", and keep the disclosure line on everything.
4. **Some of it is old.** The Bango app shipped on BlackBerry and its awards are from 2015.
   The current clients (page 9 onwards) are MWX, Ruang Lavie, Klinik Kancil, AVS and Alfamidi.
   Nothing in the deck gives a date per project, so never write "recently" about a named one.
5. **It's their marketing.** Treat every line as a company claim (research master tier C),
   except the awards verified below.

## How to use it

- **The strongest three for credibility.** Unilever (48 brands on one platform, plus an app
  with a Best in Show), Pertamina (470 formulas offline, real engineering), the World Bank
  (national data collection that works offline). Raka's opener line "Unilever, AXA, Pertamina"
  is backed here, AXA by the page 3 logo and the page 57 client list.
- **Match the project to the lead.** Booking and scheduling for clinics, salons and services
  (Klinik Kancil, Ruang Lavie, clinic and spa system). Ops and stock for retail, F&B,
  franchise and logistics (the eight systems). Fintech and payments (GPay, CIMB Niaga).
  Offline field data (eHDW, Pertamina). Brand and marketing sites (Unilever, Alfamidi, Adtiq).
- **Always attach** "Delivered by our development partner, Amwisesa". Where an agency sat in
  between, "often through the brand's own agency" is the honest addition.
- **Never** date a project we can't date, quote the unverified 2016 or 2017 medals, call a
  page 57 name a current client, or put a photo from the deck of a real person next to a
  name we can't verify (the Danamon and Wardah screens show real people).
