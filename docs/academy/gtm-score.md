# The GTM Engineering Score

Free. 25 questions, about 7 minutes. Anyone who takes it gets a score from 0 to 100, a level, a short read on their
strengths and gaps, and the course that fits them next. It's the front door of the academy and the hook of the pitch.

---

## 1. Why it exists

| For the learner | For us |
|---|---|
| Finds out how good they really are, in plain words | A lead with an email, a role, a level and their gaps, before they've bought anything |
| Gets told exactly which course to take, so they don't buy the wrong one | The right product offered to the right person |
| Something to share ("I scored 62, GTM Builder") | Free reach on LinkedIn, every share links back |

---

## 2. The levels

| Score | Level | You can | Next step we recommend |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 29 | **Starter** | You know you want more pipeline, but the systems behind it are new to you | Level 1 Operator, or GTM Seller if you sell for a living |
| 30 to 54 | **Operator** | You can run an engine someone else built. ICP, lists, research, messages | Level 2 Builder |
| 55 to 79 | **Builder** | You can build the engine. Sending setup, automations, CRM, dashboards | Level 3 Architect |
| 80 to 100 | **Architect** | You design the whole revenue system, run AI agents safely and lead others | 1:1 with Raka, or work with Astra |

**The role question overrides the level.** If someone answers "I'm a sales manager" (question 2), the result always
recommends GTM Leader first, whatever their score, because that's what they'll use. If they answer "I sell" and score
under 55, it recommends GTM Seller first and Level 1 as the next step.

---

## 3. How it works on screen

1. A landing page with one line, "How good are you at GTM engineering? Find out in 7 minutes", and a start button.
2. Questions 1 to 3 (about you) aren't scored.
3. Questions 4 to 25 are scored, one per screen, with a progress bar.
4. Before the result, one screen asks for first name and email ("Where should we send your full result?"). The score
   and level show on screen straight away either way. The email gets the full breakdown and the course recommendation.
5. The result page shows the score, the level, a bar per area (section 5) and the recommended next step with a button.
6. A share button makes an image, "I'm a GTM Builder. 62/100. Take the GTM Engineering Score", in the Astra style.

Build it in the course platform's quiz tool if it supports scoring and tags, or in a form tool (Typeform or Tally type)
connected to the email list. Each answer adds points and a tag for the area, so the email can name the weakest area.

## 4. The questions

Questions 4 to 25 score points. Knowledge questions have one right answer. Experience questions score by how far
along someone is. Each question names its area (section 5). After the test, the email explains every knowledge answer.

### About you (not scored)

**1. What's your job right now?**
Sales (SDR, BDR, AE, founder who sells) · Sales manager or head of sales · Marketing or marketing ops · RevOps or sales
ops · GTM engineer or growth engineer · Data or analytics · Student or changing careers · Other

**2. Which describes you best?**
I sell and want more meetings · I lead a sales team · I want to become a GTM engineer · I already build GTM systems and
want to get better

**3. Which tools have you used in the last 6 months?** (tick all, not scored, used for the email)
A CRM · LinkedIn Sales Navigator · Clay · lemlist or another sequencer · n8n, Zapier or Make · Claude or ChatGPT ·
Gong or Leexi · Looker Studio or another dashboard tool

### ICP and lists (questions 4 to 7)

**4. What's an ICP?** (knowledge, 4 points)
A. A list of everyone who visited your website
B. A description of the companies most likely to buy from you and stay **(right)**
C. The person who signs the contract
D. Your monthly sales target

**5. You can only contact 50 companies this month. Which do you pick first?** (knowledge, 4 points)
A. The 50 biggest companies in your country
B. The 50 that look most like your best existing customers **(right)**
C. 50 at random, to be fair
D. The 50 with the most LinkedIn followers

**6. Have you written down your ICP with the reasons companies get ruled out?** (experience)
No (0) · In my head (1) · Written, without reasons to rule out (2) · Written, with tiers and reasons to rule out (4)

**7. Consumer businesses say yes to your meetings more than anyone, but none of them buy. What do you do?** (knowledge,
4 points)
A. Contact even more consumer businesses
B. Keep them in the ICP because the yes rate is high
C. Rule them out, or make them a lower tier, and focus on companies with a budget **(right)**
D. Lower your price to zero

### Data and enrichment (questions 8 to 11)

**8. What's a waterfall in enrichment?** (knowledge, 4 points)
A. Sending the same email three times
B. Trying one data source, and if it finds nothing, the next, and the next **(right)**
C. A chart that shows revenue going down
D. Importing all your contacts at once

**9. Before sending to an email address you found, you should** (knowledge, 4 points)
A. Send a test email to see if it bounces
B. Verify it with a checking tool **(right)**
C. Guess a second version just in case
D. Nothing, if the tool found it, it's right

**10. Have you built an enrichment flow that fills in missing data automatically?** (experience)
Never (0) · Followed a tutorial (1) · Built one for real work (3) · Built one with a waterfall and a cost per row (4)

**11. Under GDPR, when you email business contacts cold in the EU, what must you be able to do?** (knowledge, 4 points)
A. Nothing, GDPR doesn't apply to business emails
B. Explain why you have a real business reason, tell people where you got their data if they ask, and stop at once if
they ask **(right)**
C. Get a signed consent form before every email
D. Only email people with a company website

### Signals and research (questions 12 to 15)

**12. Which of these is a buying signal?** (knowledge, 4 points)
A. The company has a logo
B. The company just posted three job ads for the team you sell to **(right)**
C. The company was founded in 2010
D. The CEO likes sport

**13. An AI tool tells you a prospect "recently raised €5 million". What do you do before you mention it?** (knowledge,
4 points)
A. Mention it, AI tools are accurate
B. Open the original source and check it's the same company and still true **(right)**
C. Round it to €10 million to sound impressive
D. Ask the prospect if it's true

**14. How do you research an account before reaching out?** (experience)
I don't (0) · I look at their website (1) · I use a set routine with AI and check the facts (3) · I run a research
prompt or agent with a checking step, and log the sources (4)

**15. Do you feed what customers say in calls or posts back into your targeting and messages?** (experience)
No (0) · Sometimes, by memory (1) · Yes, I keep notes (2) · Yes, it's tagged and reviewed every month (4)

### Messages and sending (questions 16 to 19)

**16. Which first message is most likely to get a reply?** (knowledge, 4 points)
A. A long introduction of your company and all your services
B. A short message about one specific problem you noticed at their company, with a low effort question at the end
**(right)**
C. "Hi, do you have 15 minutes for a call this week?"
D. A subject line starting with "Re:" so it looks like a reply

**17. What are SPF, DKIM and DMARC?** (knowledge, 4 points)
A. Three sales methods
B. Settings on your domain that prove your emails really come from you, so they don't land in spam **(right)**
C. Types of CRM fields
D. LinkedIn features

**18. Why send cold email from a separate domain?** (knowledge, 4 points)
A. It's cheaper
B. So problems with cold email don't hurt the email of your main company domain **(right)**
C. Because LinkedIn requires it
D. So people can't reply

**19. Have you run a multichannel sequence (email plus LinkedIn) yourself?** (experience)
Never (0) · Sent messages by hand (1) · Ran a sequence in a tool (3) · Ran one on a warmed domain and A/B tested one change
at a time (4)

### CRM, automation and measuring (questions 20 to 23)

**20. Two records in your CRM are the same person. What's this called and why does it matter?** (knowledge, 4 points)
A. A backup, it's good to have two
B. A duplicate, it splits history and can make two sellers contact the same person **(right)**
C. A lead, it means interest is high
D. A sync, it's normal

**21. Have you connected tools with an automation (n8n, Zapier, Make or an API)?** (experience)
Never (0) · Copied a template (1) · Built one that runs every day (3) · Built several with error handling and logging (4)

**22. Your reply rate doubled the same week you changed both the list and the opener. What can you conclude?** (knowledge,
4 points)
A. The new opener doubled replies
B. The new list doubled replies
C. Something you changed helped, and you can't say which. Test one change at a time **(right)**
D. Reply rates don't matter

**23. Do you track a weekly scorecard from messages sent down to meetings and deals?** (experience)
No (0) · I check replies (1) · I track a few numbers (2) · Weekly scorecard from sent to won, and I act on it (4)

### Architecture and leading (questions 24 and 25)

**24. Have you designed how sales, marketing and customer success tools and data fit together for a whole team?**
(experience)
Never (0) · For myself (1) · For a team, partly (3) · For a team, documented, with one source of truth (4)

**25. Have you put an AI agent into real use with a checking step before anything is sent?** (experience)
Never (0) · Tried one (1) · Use one myself every week (3) · Run one for a team or client, with a test set and human
approval (4)

## 5. Scoring

| Area | Questions | Max points |
|---|---|---|
| ICP and lists | 4, 5, 6, 7 | 16 |
| Data and enrichment | 8, 9, 10, 11 | 16 |
| Signals and research | 12, 13, 14, 15 | 16 |
| Messages and sending | 16, 17, 18, 19 | 16 |
| CRM, automation and measuring | 20, 21, 22, 23 | 16 |
| Architecture and leading | 24, 25 | 8 |
| **Total** | 22 scored questions | **88** |

**Score out of 100** = points ÷ 88 × 100, rounded. Each area shows as a bar from 0 to 100% on the result page.

**A ceiling rule so the levels stay honest.** Knowledge alone can't make someone an Architect. If the score is 80 or
more but questions 21, 24 and 25 together score under 8, the level shows as Builder. If the score is 55 or more but
questions 10, 19 and 21 together score under 5, the level shows as Operator. The page says "you know the theory, the
next level is doing it".

The page shows the score, the level name, the six bars, and the text below. The email repeats it, names the weakest
area, explains every knowledge answer, and gives the course link.

**Starter (0 to 29).** "You're at the start, and that's the best place to learn this, before bad habits. In the first
lesson of Level 1 you'll research one real company and write a first message in 30 minutes." Button, Start Level 1
Operator. Sellers see GTM Seller first.

**Operator (30 to 54).** "You understand how a good engine works. What's missing is building one yourself. Your weakest
area is [area]." Button, Start Level 2 Builder.

**Builder (55 to 79).** "You can build. The next step is designing the whole system, running AI agents safely and
leading others through it." Button, Start Level 3 Architect.

**Architect (80 to 100).** "You're in the top level. If you want to sharpen it with Raka, or have Astra build alongside
your team, let's talk." Buttons, Book a call with Raka, and Work with Astra.

**Managers (any score).** "You lead the team, so start with GTM Leader. It gives you the operating model, the sales bible
and the dashboard, and your team can follow with GTM Seller." Button, Start GTM Leader.

## 7. Follow up emails after the score

| Day | Email |
|---|---|
| 0 | The full result, as above |
| 2 | Their weakest area, one free tip and one short video from the course on it |
| 5 | A worked example from our own engine (anonymised), linked to their level's course |
| 9 | "The first lesson of your level is free." Link to Lesson 1, the quick win |
| 14 | Last email. The course, the 1:1 option, and an invitation to retake the score in 3 months to see the change |

Everyone who scores goes into the email list with their score, level, role and weakest area, so offers can be matched.
They can unsubscribe in one click, and the privacy note on the landing page says what's kept and why.

---

## 8. Retakes

Learners retake the score at the end of each course. The jump in score ("from 34 to 71") goes on their certificate page
and, with their permission, into testimonials. It's the cleanest proof the course works.
