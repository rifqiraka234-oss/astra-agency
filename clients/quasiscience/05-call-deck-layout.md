# QuasiScience call deck layout (Thu 5 or Fri 6 Nov 2026)

Built 3 Nov 2026 from the Ivan call transcript, Gisella's emails of 27 and 30 Oct, and the earlier research. This is a layout, not the finished deck. Prices are blanks for Raka to fill.

## What the call must achieve

Leave the call with four things, in this order of importance.
1. **A trial agreed in principle.** Scope, length, what counts as a qualified meeting, how it is paid.
2. **One offer and one ICP chosen to start with.** Not six.
3. **The materials in hand,** or a date for them.
4. **The next date booked** (kickoff or review), with Marco's availability confirmed.

Partnership (introductions or delivering projects together) gets its own five minutes at the end or a separate call. Do not let it swallow the trial discussion.

## The insight the whole deck is built around

Gisella's 30 Oct email: they have been burned by providers "not actually delivering anything of value despite being provided with a lot of materials and clear target definitions". She also wrote that they "already have a clear idea of how to improve our sales and could use some help executing", and that managed outbound interests her more than strategy.

So her fear is **delivery**, not a missing strategy. The earlier plan (tell them the ICP is the problem, "the success is on you") answers a worry she doesn't have, and may sound like the lecture she has already had. The deck should make the work **visible and checkable from day one**, and treat her inputs as the brief, tested against the market in the first month.

### Slide 6, the offer choice (decision slide)

Frame it as "which first", and keep the evidence short. The research report holds the detail (`reports/QuasiScience ICP personas signals.md`).

| Offer | Why it could go first | Measured how |
|---|---|---|
| Renewables bid pricing | Small, public list of developers, buyers already pay advisers for bid strategy, dated auction calendar | Qualified opportunities, signal driven, low volume |
| KYC tool | Hardest regulatory clock (EU AML Regulation 10 July 2027), richest signals, but no public security certification | Connected calls and conversations, higher volume |
| Manufacturing digital twins | Machinery Regulation 20 Jan 2027 | Needs a verified case study first |
| Marketing mix and pricing | Large buyers, but free tools anchor price and proof is thin | Needs proof first |

**Before this slide goes on screen, verify the UK CfD AR8 dates on the GOV.UK timetable.** Raka and Ivan think bidding runs Nov to Feb. The research found applications closed 7 Aug 2026 and results are due 27 Nov 2026 to 17 Feb 2027. The pitch changes depending on which is right.

### Slide 8, the trial (what to put on it)

Her ask. A short commission based trial of 1 to 2 weeks, before any retainer.
Ivan's view. Two weeks shows nothing because ramp up takes about a month, and he can't commit to a meetings target until the ICP and list quality are known. He commits to **activity**, and the first month produces the data to set a meetings target.
Raka's earlier email to her promised "a fee per meeting booked".

Show the choice plainly and let her pick.

| Option | How it works | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| A. Capacity trial (Ivan's model) | 4 weeks. A committed number of connected calls (his floor is 200 a month, a connected call is 30 seconds or more), full inbox cover, weekly report. Exit after the month, no retainer. | Cheapest per month, but you pay for work done, not for outcomes. |
| B. Base plus bonus | Smaller base, a target of qualified meetings per month (Ivan's example is 4), a bonus for each one above target. | Shares the risk. Needs the qualified meeting defined first. |
| C. Per meeting only | A fee per qualified meeting booked, no base. | The most expensive way to buy meetings (the benchmark Raka gave was 900 euros a meeting at his employer). Priced higher because we carry all the risk. |

Built in to every option. A **checkpoint at the end of week 2** (activity and early data shown), a **decision at the end of week 4** (continue, change the ICP or message, or stop), and **no automatic retainer.** Say plainly why four weeks and not two, using their own history (providers that were cut early or didn't ramp).

**Fill in before the call.** Ivan's quote for the work, Astra's build fee, Astra's margin, currency (Raka's proposal used pounds, Ivan's numbers had no currency in the transcript), how a qualified meeting is defined, and who approves the first messages.

Ivan's numbers, from the transcript, for Raka to build the price from. The quote he mentioned for 200 connected calls a month with full inbox cover was about 2,500 if Astra provides the Lemlist, lists and enrichment. A full SDR day slot is roughly 3,800 to 4,500 before tax. He typically exceeds the floor (80 to 100 connected a week). The 200 figure is a floor he is happy to beat. His contracts run three months with an exit after the first or second month.

### Slide 9, honest expectations

Short list. Enterprise deals take months. Their buyers have no urgency, so the sequences run for months with monthly touches. Their deals are large (Raka's guess was 30k to 100k plus), so volume is low and quality matters. The first month produces data (connected rate, objections, which role answers), not closed deals. This is where we say "we measure activity and learning in month one, and set a meetings target from the data in month two".

## Questions to ask them live

1. What was promised and what was delivered with the earlier providers, and what could you see while it was running?
2. Your own priority offer, buyer roles and signals (the four materials), and who has the last word on the message?
3. What is a qualified meeting for you? Who qualifies it?
4. Which CRM do you use, and where should meeting data and recordings live?
5. What can we say on your behalf, and which sending domains and LinkedIn accounts can we use?
6. Who is on the calls with prospects, and when is Marco free for technical calls?
7. Is a trial of four weeks possible, and what would you need to see at week two?
8. Partnership. Are you thinking mainly of introductions to my clients, delivering projects together, or both?

## Likely objections and answers

| They say | Answer |
|---|---|
| "We wanted one to two weeks." | Week two is a checkpoint inside a four week trial. Two weeks alone shows only the ramp up. |
| "We've been burned before." | Everything is visible weekly (sequences, inboxes, call log, recordings), and you can stop after week four. |
| "Commission only." | Offer option B or C, and say why per meeting only costs more. |

| "What about cold calling rules?" | Check UK and EU rules per market with Ivan before the call. Unresearched. |
