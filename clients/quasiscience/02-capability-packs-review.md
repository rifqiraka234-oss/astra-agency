# QuasiScience capability packs, review

Two PDFs sent by Gisella on 27 Oct 2026 (25 and 22 pages), both marked "Private and confidential". Do not quote their figures publicly without asking.

## Verdict
Capability decks. They say what QuasiScience can build and show past work. They give no ICP, buyer titles, triggers, pricing or reason to act now, so they do not answer Gisella's own ask (who to target and how to word it). That gap is the argument for the strategy sprint.

## Contents
- Shared pages: about, a logo wall (about 25 names, no detail on which were paid projects), values, six capabilities, a five stage delivery process.
- Delivery process: requirements 1 to 2 weeks, R&D 1 to 3, build 3 to 8, testing 2 to 3, then ongoing. 7 to 16 weeks before support. So a long, high value sale.
- Manufacturing and engineering pack: FIPMEC bridge damper digital twin (about EUR 100k saved per test per damper), Ortus scope 3 emissions, FIPMEC materials data platform, Fogsphere safety monitoring, Ortus bid pricing (helped secure a EUR 400m investment), Kioko CAD and testing, Cranfield cloud HPC (about a third of the cost), Ortus document search (90 percent less search time), S&P 500 retailer email bots (thousands of hours a year).
- Finance pack: the KYC tool built with the University of Padova, Ortus project finance risk modelling, Ortus audit preparation (the same text as the document search case), Fogsphere SOC2 preparation and a data pipeline, the retailer automation case.
- All figures are company claims.

## Things noticed
1. The finance pack's sector page heading is about financial decisions but its text describes construction firms and site safety. Looks copied from another pack.
2. Ortus appears in three or four case studies, FIPMEC and Fogsphere in two or three each. One case is retitled across the two packs. Concentrated client base.
3. Testimonials are from founders and small firms (Ortus CFO, CEOs of Facture, Coyzy, Tribyou). The S&P 500 clients are not named.
4. The logo wall includes the Ministry of Defence (inference only: may link to Gisella's background).
5. Strongest cluster by volume of cases: renewables developers (Ortus). Bid pricing, emissions modelling and document search could form one first campaign.
