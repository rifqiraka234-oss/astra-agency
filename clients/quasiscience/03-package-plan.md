# Package plan for QuasiScience (Raka's thinking, 27 to 28 Oct 2026)

Status: working notes, not a proposal. Prices are placeholders inside the 500 to 50,000 euro band. Raka decides prices.

## Order of work
1. Strategy sprint, fixed fee, weeks 1 to 4. Strategy session and workshop. ICP per product and sector, unique selling points, pitch points, battle cards, messaging angles to test, cadence. Funnel analysis scheduled as a checkpoint at about day 90, once there is data.
2. Campaign building and running. One product, one sector, about 50 chosen accounts at a time. Build the account list and 3 to 4 contacts per account, write sequences for LinkedIn, email and calls, plan an A/B test of two angles, set up Lemlist with warmed sending domains, run weekly, handle replies, book meetings, report from the dashboard, learn and repeat.
3. Engine build. Lemlist, Clay and Claude workflows linked to their CRM. Signals. Outbound AI agent. Control room dashboard. X workflows included, extra ones priced each. Check what CRM and automation they already have first.
4. Outbound management. A partner (Ivan) for cold calls and email management. Not yet sourced or priced.
5. Fractional RevOps. Raka owns the pipeline process, weekly review with Gisella and Marco. Check his employment contract before offering himself personally, otherwise offer through Astra.

Rough placeholder shape: sprint 2,500 to 4,000 fixed. Engine 3,000 to 6,000 with X workflows. Dashboard 1,500 to 3,000. Outbound run monthly once Ivan is priced.

## Ideas to pull in
- Account based, about 100 to 150 chosen accounts, multi threaded, not 1,000 cold sends. Enterprise buying committees run 6 to 10 people.
- Signal engine scored with a threshold, enrich only when a signal fires.
- Network mapping: match Marco's, Gisella's and the advisors' connections to target accounts. Systematic ask for intros from existing clients.
- Their own posts as lead fuel: engagers at target accounts.
- Role based targeting from their testimonials: Engineering Director, Head of Pricing, Chief Strategy Officer, CFO.
- A small personalised sketch as the offer instead of "book a call".
- Auto generated pre call brief per booked meeting to protect Marco's time.
- Reseller route via Raka's own clients, as Gisella suggested.
