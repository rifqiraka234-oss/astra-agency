# QuasiScience, ideal customer profile (ICP)

Written 29 Oct 2026 from the deep research report (`reports/QuasiScience ICP personas signals.md`) and the five research notes behind it.
Labels, as in the report. **Sourced** has a URL. **Vendor claim** is QuasiScience or a seller describing itself. **Inference** is reasoning, not evidence. **Unverified** means nothing was opened. Size bands and buyer fears are inference throughout, because no sourced company counts, interviews or surveys exist for these sectors.

QuasiScience's sales decks are marked Private and confidential. Deck figures (the EUR 400m energy figure, the FIPMEC EUR 100,000 per test saving, the 5 and 18 percent MMO uplifts, the Cranfield one third cost, the S&P 500 retailer, the Padova KYC link, the Italian beta) stay out of outreach until Gisella gives written permission.

## One line

Companies with an expensive, uncertain decision (what to bid, what to test, who is risky, where to spend) and enough money to pay for a bespoke model, but not enough scale to build it in house or buy a seven figure software estate. Buyer is a senior commercial, engineering, compliance or marketing lead who can sign a project in the 500 to 50,000 euro band up to low six figures (inference on the upper range).

## ICP 1 in detail, renewables bid pricing (recommended first campaign)

- **Accounts.** About 50 UK and EU developers and IPPs. Build from the AR8 applicant and result pages if NESO or GOV.UK publishes them, otherwise from the AR7 results page (14 Jan 2026), then add German bidders after the 1 Nov and 1 Dec bid dates. Seed with people already in Marco's and Gisella's networks.
- **Buyer titles.** Head of Commercial, Origination, Market Modelling, or Director of Regulatory Affairs and Auctions (owns bid pricing). CFO, Head of Project Finance or Investment Director (returns and lenders).
- **Sourced context.**
  - AR8 freezes strike prices at AR7 levels (solar 75, onshore wind 92, offshore wind 113, floating 271 GBP per MWh, 2024 prices) and adds separate offshore clearing prices by connection zone. Source: CMS summary.
  - Solar capture factors fell sharply in April 2026 in core EU markets, negative price hours rose (PV Europe, citing Pexapark). One month, so it overstates the year.
  - Gate 2 grid connection offers: phase 2 by 31 Jan 2027 per Ofgem, one source says mid March 2027 (conflict).
- **Pains (inference).** Over shading and losing, or under bidding and holding an uneconomic contract. Models they can't inspect or rerun. No sourced cost of a pricing error was found.
- **Likely objections.** "Cornwall Insight or Baringa already do this." "Our spreadsheet works." Bid economics are commercially sensitive. A seven person supplier.
- **Message shape (inference).** One dated hook and one question about how they set a strike price today. Offer a small sketch run on public AR7 results, not "machine learning".
- **Triggers.** AR8 result (win or lose), AR9 preparation, Gate 2 offers, German offshore redesign (a cabinet stage bill, not yet law).
- **Where they spend time.** RenewableUK Global Offshore Wind, 16 to 17 June 2027, ExCeL London. Intersolar Europe, 8 to 10 June 2027, Munich. Trade press is inference.

## Who is NOT the ICP (inference, and Astra's own rules)

- Individuals, one person firms, and firms too small to fund a bespoke project.
- Large banks that demand ISO 27001 or SOC 2 on day one, until QuasiScience has it (or a dated plan, such as Cyber Essentials).
- Anyone who needs a named reference or audited result QuasiScience can't yet show.
- Buyers whose real alternative is a free tool and who have no budget for proof (MMO at the low end).
- Agencies, recruiters and coaches, per Astra's own targeting lessons.
