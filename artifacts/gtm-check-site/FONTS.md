# Fonts and palette for the GTM Engine Check

Read from the Astra brand deck in Canva (DAG8WBvhY7s), exported to PDF and parsed on 7 Nov 2026.

Palette, exactly as the deck uses it. Navy #0d1b38, off white #fdfdfd, ink #1d1d1e, sand #e6ddd8, slate #405b8d,
light grey #d8d7d7. The page uses only these six plus see through tints of them.

Fonts in the deck, by amount of text. Solomon Sans (body and bold heads), The Seasons (display, bold, regular,
italic), Cerebri Sans and Open Sauce One (a few lines each).

On this site
- Open Sauce One for everything, Raka's call on 7 Nov 2026. It is in the brand deck and open licensed (OFL), self hosted
  in fonts/ with its licence.
- Solomon Sans and The Seasons stay in Canva only. Using them online needs a Fontfabric webfont licence and an Adobe
  Fonts web project or a bought webfont.
