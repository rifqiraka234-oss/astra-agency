## 1. Which company types and size bands buy, which titles own budget, and what do they care about and fear?

### Takeaway
The best sourced anchor is Gartner's 2026 CMO survey of 401 marketing leaders, mostly at companies above $1bn revenue in North America, the UK and Europe, which shows budgets flat at 7.8 percent of revenue and 56 percent of CMOs saying they lack the budget to deliver strategy. Everything about specific titles, space planners and sub-sector fit is inference, because I found no sourced survey of who buys MMM, pricing or space tools in grocery, fashion, DIY, home, travel retail or pharma.

### Cited Findings
- Marketing budgets are "effectively flat, rising only slightly to 7.8% of company revenue in 2026 from 7.7% in 2025". **[SOURCED FACT]** — [Gartner press release, 11 May 2026 (syndicated copy)](https://salestechstar.com/predictive-ai-artificial-intelligence/gartner-2026-cmo-spend-survey-finds-cmos-allocate-15-3-of-marketing-budgets-to-ai-but-only-30-are-ready-to-scale-ai-capabilities/)
- 56 percent of CMOs say their marketing organisation lacks the budget to deliver the 2026 strategy; 54 percent report insufficient resources. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [same](https://salestechstar.com/predictive-ai-artificial-intelligence/gartner-2026-cmo-spend-survey-finds-cmos-allocate-15-3-of-marketing-budgets-to-ai-but-only-30-are-ready-to-scale-ai-capabilities/)
- CMOs allocate an average 15.3 percent of marketing budgets to AI; 70 percent say becoming an AI leader is a critical 2026 goal; only 30 percent report mature AI readiness; about 70 percent admit processes are not mature enough to scale AI. AI-ready organisations spend 21.3 percent of budget on AI and report budgets of 8.9 percent of revenue. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [same](https://salestechstar.com/predictive-ai-artificial-intelligence/gartner-2026-cmo-spend-survey-finds-cmos-allocate-15-3-of-marketing-budgets-to-ai-but-only-30-are-ready-to-scale-ai-capabilities/)
- Sample: 401 respondents, "the vast majority" above $1bn annual revenue, fieldwork January to March 2026, North America, UK and Europe. Half of CMOs report budgets of 6 percent of revenue or less (from the search summary of the same release). **[SOURCED FACT]** / **[UNVERIFIED]** for the median — [same](https://salestechstar.com/predictive-ai-artificial-intelligence/gartner-2026-cmo-spend-survey-finds-cmos-allocate-15-3-of-marketing-budgets-to-ai-but-only-30-are-ready-to-scale-ai-capabilities/)
- The Deloitte/Duke CMO Survey reported 9.0 percent of revenue in January 2026; the two surveys sample different companies. **[UNVERIFIED]** (search summary only) — [Gartner via search summary](https://gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-11-gartner-2026-cmo-spend-survey-finds-cmos-allocate-15-point-3-percent-of-marketing-budgets-to-ai-but-only-30-percent-are-ready-to-scale-ai-capabilities)
- Gartner's press release has no ROI, measurement or analytics statistics. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [same](https://salestechstar.com/predictive-ai-artificial-intelligence/gartner-2026-cmo-spend-survey-finds-cmos-allocate-15-3-of-marketing-budgets-to-ai-but-only-30-are-ready-to-scale-ai-capabilities/)
- Pharma commercial teams describe MMM as a "black box" and say channels are measured in isolation across HCP and patient audiences, with field reps, conferences, digital and portals fragmented. These are consultancy and vendor pieces. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** — [Tredence](https://www.tredence.com/whitepaper/optimizing-pharma-marketing-mix), [Axtria](https://insights.axtria.com/blog/orchestrating-omnichannel-measurement-to-enable-outcomes-driven-personalized-campaigns)
- IQVIA's white paper says companies "have failed to show a quantitative ROI impact" from AI and ML field engagement, despite qualitative value. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** (IQVIA sells commercial analytics) — [IQVIA white paper 2024](https://www.iqvia.com/-/media/iqvia/pdfs/us/white-paper/2024/iqvia-ffo-adoption-catch-22-white-paper-2024.pdf)

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** ICP size band: revenue above about $1bn (Gartner's sample base) in UK, Europe and US, because they run multi-channel, multi-market budgets large enough that a 1 to 5 percent allocation gain pays for a bespoke tool. I have no sourced lower bound.
- **[INFERENCE]** Budget owners by use case. MMO: CMO, VP Marketing Analytics or Head of Insight, with the CFO as sign-off. Pricing: Head of Pricing or Chief Commercial Officer. Space and layout: Head of Space Planning or Category Director. Strategy and scenario modelling: Chief Strategy Officer or CFO. Pharma: commercial excellence or commercial analytics head, brand leads, and a global marketing or omnichannel head. The anonymous QuasiScience testimonials use the same titles (Head of Pricing, Chief Strategy Officer), which is the only sourced hint about who QuasiScience has actually sold to.
- **[INFERENCE]** What they care about: proving marketing return to the CFO (budgets flat while 56 percent say they are short), defending share in a flat budget, AI readiness. What they fear: another black box, a model that cannot be explained to finance, and pharma compliance and data privacy limits on sharing HCP and patient data.
- **[INFERENCE]** The sub-sector split (grocery, fashion, DIY, home, travel retail, big consumer brands) is not supported by any source I found. Hypothesis to test: fashion and home have high markdown and seasonality exposure; grocery has the largest pricing and space volumes; consumer brands are the natural MMM buyers (they spend heavily on media); pharma is a smaller, more regulated MMM buyer with a different data setup.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on which sub-sectors (grocery, fashion, DIY, home, travel retail) buy MMM, pricing or space tools, or on adoption by size band. Needs a Gartner, Forrester or IDC paid report, or IAB/WARC member surveys.
- No sourced budget range for what an enterprise pays for any of these use cases at the buyer side (see question 5 for vendor-side prices).
- No sourced survey of Head of Pricing, Head of Space Planning or CFO priorities. Gartner's CMO survey is the only primary source found.
- Titles above are inference, not from an org-chart or job-ad study.

## 2. What are the pains and their sourced costs (media waste, privacy and cookie loss, pricing leakage, markdowns, space planning gaps, inflation and tariff volatility)?

### Takeaway
There is solid sourced evidence for media waste (ANA programmatic studies) and for the rise of MMM as a response to signal loss (EMARKETER). Pricing leverage and markdown figures exist but come from old or secondary sources and must be labelled as such. There is no strong sourced number for space planning gaps, and the "cookie loss" story has weakened because Chrome kept third party cookies.

### Cited Findings
- Media waste, programmatic only. ANA's 2023 study of $123m spend and 35.5bn impressions from 21 brands (Sept 2022 to Jan 2023) found only 36 cents of each dollar entering a DSP reached consumers. **[SOURCED FACT]** (narrow: open-web programmatic) — [ANA](https://ana.net/programmatic), [Marketing Brew](https://marketingbrew.com/stories/2023/12/05/ana-report-finds-that-only-around-a-third-of-ad-dollars-reach-intended-audiences)
- ANA and TAG TrustNet's 2024 benchmark put the figure at 43.9 percent of each $1,000 reaching consumers, up 7.9 points; Q3 2025 benchmark 47.1 percent. Different methodology from 2023, so not directly comparable. **[SOURCED FACT]** for the 2024 release, **[UNVERIFIED]** for 43.9 and 47.1 as quoted from search summaries — [ANA 2024 release](https://ana.net/content/show/id/pr-2024-12-programmatic), [Inside Radio](https://www.insideradio.com/free/study-less-than-half-money-spent-on-programmatic-ads-reaches-consumers/article_99daf3b6-bb84-11ef-b9f7-67408975f9b9.html)
- MMM demand. An EMARKETER and TransUnion survey (July 2025) found almost half (46.9 percent) of US marketers will invest more in MMM over the next year, and MMM was the top answer (27.6 percent) for most reliable measurement method. EMARKETER ties the resurgence to privacy regulation and signal loss. **[SOURCED FACT]** (survey figures taken from search summary) — [EMARKETER](https://www.emarketer.com/content/mmm--incrementality--other-measurement-trends-that-will-define-2026)
- A claim of MMM adoption rising from 9 percent (2023) to 26 percent, with 43 percent of adopters citing signal loss, appears on one marketing blog with vague sourcing. **[UNVERIFIED], do not use** — [digitalapplied](https://digitalapplied.com/marketing-attribution-statistics/)
- Cookie status. Google said on 22 April 2025 it would keep its existing cookie choice approach in Chrome with no standalone opt-out prompt, and on 17 October 2025 retired Privacy Sandbox APIs including Topics, Protected Audience, Attribution Reporting and Private Aggregation. Chrome's decision does not remove EU consent duties. **[SOURCED FACT]** (secondary reporting) — [Stape](https://stape.io/news/google-chrome-cookie-deprecation), [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/10/20/google-is-retiring-privacy-sandbox/), [CookieYes](https://www.cookieyes.com/knowledge-base/privacy-tech/is-google-ending-privacy-sandbox/)
- Pricing leverage. Secondary sources attribute to McKinsey (Global 1200 study) that a 1 percent price rise with constant demand lifts average operating profit by about 11 percent, and 8 percent for an S&P 1500 version. The underlying surveys date from the 1990s and assume volume holds. **[UNVERIFIED]** (primary McKinsey text not found; do not present as a retail margin leakage figure) — [IndustryWeek](https://www.industryweek.com/software-amp-systems/10-tips-better-pricing)
- Markdowns. One US study estimated annual markdown losses of about $300bn, roughly 12 percent of total retail sales (dated to 2018 by Toolio). **[UNVERIFIED]**, old — [Relex](https://relexsolutions.com/resources/markdown-optimization)
- Fashion markdowns "consume 20 to 50 percent of net sales at many fashion retailers" cited to McKinsey by a secondary blog. **[UNVERIFIED]** — [Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/average-dead-stock-markdown-rate-by-vertical)
- Inventory distortion. IHL Group and Blue Yonder are quoted at $1.77 trillion a year globally for overstock plus out of stock, about $562bn from overstock. **[UNVERIFIED]** (via Eightx, not IHL directly) — [Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/average-dead-stock-markdown-rate-by-vertical), [IHL](https://www.ihlservices.com/?p=40573)
- Space planning. An arXiv paper on floor space optimisation for a European grocery chain says standard space planning software is limited in linking space allocation to sales. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [arXiv 2011.04422](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.04422)
- A student MIT capstone on Walmart planograms models an example 6 percent uplift in a 2 to 10 percent range. **[SOURCED FACT]** about what the project reports, weak evidence — [MIT capstone](https://www.analyticscapstone.mit.edu/s/Walmart-Ella-Liu-Yijia-Yang.pdf)
- Retail cost pressure UK. EY-Parthenon reported seven UK listed retailers issued profit warnings in April to June 2025 versus three a year earlier, citing employer National Insurance and tariffs. **[SOURCED FACT]** (secondary) — [Grocery Gazette](https://www.grocerygazette.co.uk/2025/07/21/profit-warnings-increase-amid-hiked-nic-fees-and-tariffs/)
- US tariff volatility. The Supreme Court ruled 6 to 3 on 20 February 2026 that IEEPA does not authorise the reciprocal tariffs. A 10 percent Section 122 surcharge began 24 February and expired 24 July 2026, replaced the same day by Section 301 duties of 10 or 12.5 percent on about 60 economies. The Court of International Trade held Section 122 unconstitutional on 7 May 2026 (2 to 1), under appeal. On 4 March Judge Eaton ordered refunds to importers, and Costco and Kohl's suits are pending. Section 232 goods are excluded from the new Section 301 duties. **[SOURCED FACT]** (law firm and trade summaries, not checked against USTR or CBP notices) — [Allianz Trade](https://www.allianz-trade.com/en_global/news-insights/economic-insights/us-supreme-court-ieepa-tariffs.html), [UHY](https://uhy-us.com/insights/news/2026/july/tariff-reset-section-122-expires-as-section-301-duties-expand), [Steptoe](https://www.steptoe.com/en/news-publications/global-trade-and-investment-law-blog/from-ieepa-to-section-301-ustr-reimplements-trumps-global-tariff-regime.html)
- Advisers expect lasting caution on "inventory planning, pricing strategies, capital allocation, and advertising budgets". **[SOURCED FACT]** as an opinion — [Retail Insight Network](https://www.retail-insight-network.com/newsletters/us-retailers-address-impact-of-supreme-court-tariffs-decision)
- Ad market context. WARC Q3 2026 forecast (7 Oct 2026): global ad spend up 11.9 percent to $1.343tn in 2026, 8.4 percent to $1.46tn in 2027, 7.9 percent to $1.57tn in 2028; social, search and retail media are 66.4 percent of spend. Retail media $200.4bn in 2026 and $223.4bn in 2027. **[SOURCED FACT]** (secondary coverage of WARC) — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/digital-marketing-en/warc-global-ad-spend-2026-social-media-21-percent/), [Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/retail-media-spending-set-to-hit-223bn-by-2027-as-growth-eases-301128a)
- Recast's own example of cost of misallocation: misallocating 5 percent of $20,000 daily media spend equals $365,000. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** and arithmetic, not evidence — [Recast](https://getrecast.com/marketing-mix-modeling-software/)
- Pharma: Cognizant cites 10 to 40 percent marketing efficiency gains from an omnichannel commercial model, no methodology seen. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** — [Cognizant](https://www.cognizant.com/us/en/insights/insights-blog/how-pharma-companies-can-prototype-an-omnichannel-commercial-model-wf1116900)

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** Cost of doing nothing, illustrative arithmetic only: a retailer or brand with $5bn revenue at Gartner's 7.8 percent has about $390m of marketing budget (this includes more than media). Every 1 percent of that budget misallocated is about $3.9m a year. The ANA waste data covers open web programmatic only, so it must not be applied to total media.
- **[INFERENCE]** Sales framing for the MMO should not lean on "cookies are dying". Chrome kept third party cookies, so the sourced reason to move to MMM is signal loss and measurement credibility with finance, not a Chrome deadline.
- **[INFERENCE]** Tariff volatility is a stronger "pricing and scenario modelling" hook than a marketing hook: duty rates changed at least four times in 2026 (IEEPA, Section 122, court rulings, Section 301), and refunds are unresolved, so pricing and sourcing scenarios have to be re-run repeatedly. This fits stochastic scenario modelling.
- **[INFERENCE]** QuasiScience's "5 percent in first season" claim is measured against "an industry standard tool". Without knowing the baseline, the metric (sales? incremental sales?), and the test design, a finance buyer will not accept it. Buyers will ask for a holdout or geo test design.

### Gaps
- No primary McKinsey, BCG or Bain text confirming the pricing leverage figures or any retail pricing leakage percentage.
- No current, sourced markdown rate for grocery, fashion, DIY or home. The 12 percent and 20 to 50 percent figures are old or secondary.
- No independent sourced figure for the cost of poor space planning or planogram non-compliance (the "over half of categories fail the planogram" Cognizant claim came via SafetyCulture and is unchecked).
- No sourced figure for share of media spend wasted overall (all channels). ANA is programmatic only.
- No 2026 profit warning counts found (only 2025 EY data). EY's latest retail profit warning report is the place to look.
- No UK or EU tariff and inflation sources (ONS, Eurostat, BRC shop price index) gathered.
- BRC and business rates cost estimates appeared in search results but I could not tie them to a specific page, so I left them out.

## 3. Which market and regulatory events create timing in 2026 to 2028? Give dates.

### Takeaway
The firmest dated events are the EU AI Act omnibus (now law, high-risk dates moved to 2 Dec 2027 and 2 Aug 2028), the US tariff regime reset on 24 July 2026 with litigation open, and US pharma most favoured nation (MFN) pricing deals running to January 2029. The cookie deadline is gone. I found no sourced dates for retail results seasons or budget cycles, so those are inference.

### Cited Findings
- EU AI Act. The Digital Omnibus on AI was published in the Official Journal on 24 July 2026 and entered into force 27 July 2026. Stand-alone high-risk (Annex III) obligations move to **2 December 2027**; high-risk AI embedded in regulated products moves to **2 August 2028**. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/gbr/publication/the-eu-s-digital-omnibus-streamlined-ai-act-compliance-rules-are-now-in-force), [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/eu-digital-omnibus-on-ai-enters-into-force/1825722)
- Article 50 transparency still largely applies from **2 August 2026**; providers of generative AI placed on the market before that date have until **2 December 2026** for content labelling. Provisional deal was reached 6 May 2026 (Gibson Dunn) and confirmed by Member State representatives 13 May 2026. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/), [CMS](https://cms.law/en/gbr/publication/the-eu-s-digital-omnibus-streamlined-ai-act-compliance-rules-are-now-in-force)
- AI literacy duty under Article 4 retained but softened to supporting staff AI literacy. GPAI obligations applied from 2 August 2025. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/)
- The Regulation number (2026/1744) and a 8 July 2026 date appear in secondary results; sources differ on dates. **[UNVERIFIED]** — [Cuatrecasas](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/en/spain/intellectual-property/art/digital-omnibus-ai-has-been-published)
- Cookies. 22 April 2025 Chrome keeps third party cookies; 17 October 2025 Privacy Sandbox APIs retired. No Chrome cutoff date remains. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [Stape](https://stape.io/news/google-chrome-cookie-deprecation)
- US tariffs. 20 Feb 2026 Supreme Court ruling; 24 Feb Section 122 begins; 4 March refund order; 7 May CIT ruling on Section 122; 24 July Section 122 expires and Section 301 duties start; 31 July patented pharmaceutical articles under specified HTS headings become exempt from the new Section 301 duties; 22 Aug 2026 new 50 percent tariffs on designated Canadian HS codes (legal basis disputed between sources). **[SOURCED FACT]** (unverified against official notices) — [UHY](https://uhy-us.com/insights/news/2026/july/tariff-reset-section-122-expires-as-section-301-duties-expand), [Global-e](https://www.global-e.com/the-latest-tariff-updates/)
- Pharma MFN. As of late Aug to Sept 2026 the White House says 26 large drugmakers covering 90 percent of the branded US market agreed to MFN-aligned pricing; nine more midsize companies (including Teva, UCB, Astellas) were added in late August. A company signing both MFN and US onshoring agreements pays no tariff, through 20 January 2029. CMS has issued mandatory Medicare models GLOBE (Part B) and GUARD (Part D). Medicaid-wide MFN extended per a September 2026 White House release. **[SOURCED FACT]** (administration claims not independently verified) — [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/09/president-trump-extends-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-every-medicaid-program-in-america/), [PharmExec](https://pharmexec.com/view/trump-administration-adds-nine-drugmakers-most-favored-nation-program-report), [Hogan Lovells](https://insights.hoganlovells.com/report/lshc-horizons-2026/most-favored-nation-drug-pricing-models-make-global-waves-in-2026)
- Most Favored Patient Act (H.R. 7837) would test MFN pricing from 1 January 2029. **[SOURCED FACT]** (a bill, not law) — [policyrisk](https://policyrisk.com/legislation/HR7837)
- Gartner Marketing Symposium/Xpo took place 11 May 2026, the same day as the CMO Spend Survey release, which is when CMO budget conversations are most visible. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [Gartner newsroom](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-11-gartner-marketing-symposium-xpo-day-1-highlights)
- Ad market growth moderates after 2026: 11.9 percent in 2026 to 8.4 percent in 2027 and 7.9 percent in 2028. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/digital-marketing-en/warc-global-ad-spend-2026-social-media-21-percent/)
- QuasiScience beta open now with general release planned early 2027. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** from the brief, not on the homepage.

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** AI Act is a weak sales trigger for these tools. Pricing and recommendation engines are mostly not in the Annex III stand-alone high-risk list (which covers areas such as employment and essential services), so the 2027 date is mostly a governance and documentation argument, plus Article 4 AI literacy. A legal check is needed before QuasiScience says anything about classification. Useful point for EU buyers: with the high-risk deadline moved to 2 Dec 2027, the compliance panic has eased.
- **[INFERENCE]** Stronger 2026 to 2028 "why now" set: (1) flat budgets (7.8 percent) with 56 percent short of budget, so proving return is the job; (2) tariff and duty volatility that makes static price lists and static scenarios stale within months; (3) pharma MFN and tariff deals forcing pricing and launch sequencing rethinks in 2026 to 2029; (4) the beta to early 2027 general release window lines up with 2027 annual planning, so beta customers can show results by planning season.
- **[INFERENCE]** Budget cycle. Many large retailers and brands lock next year's media plans in Q3 and Q4, and many UK retailers have January or February year ends, so mid-year audit and planning windows differ by company. I have no source, so check each target's fiscal year end in its annual report.

### Gaps
- No sourced dates for UK and EU retailer results seasons. Each target's own financial calendar is the source.
- No sourced list of big format store programmes, openings or closures for 2026 to 2028.
- Official Journal text for the AI Omnibus not read directly. Article 50 details differ between sources (FPF vs Modulos).
- EU pharma package, IRA negotiation cycle for 2027 and UK pricing (VPAG) were not found. The search returned US MFN only.
- US Section 122 appeal outcome and refund process status as of 28 Oct 2026 unknown.
- UK Autumn Budget 2026 date and content not researched.
- Cookie related EU rules (ePrivacy, the digital omnibus consent changes) were not researched.

## 4. Which buying signals could be automated, and from what data sources?

### Takeaway
I could verify very little about specific feeds. The signal types below are inference, matched to sources whose existence I confirmed only for SEC EDGAR full text search. Each source needs access terms and licensing checked before building automation.

### Cited Findings
- SEC EDGAR full text search covers filings since 2001. Page mentions an FAQ that may describe programmatic access, which I did not read. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [SEC EDGAR search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/)
- Gartner's survey shows the topic phrases CMOs publicly discuss: AI readiness, budget shortfall, measurable business impact. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [Gartner release (syndicated)](https://salestechstar.com/predictive-ai-artificial-intelligence/gartner-2026-cmo-spend-survey-finds-cmos-allocate-15-3-of-marketing-budgets-to-ai-but-only-30-are-ready-to-scale-ai-capabilities/)
- UK profit warnings among listed retailers rose in 2025 (seven in Q2 2025), so profit warning trackers such as EY-Parthenon's are a published signal source. **[SOURCED FACT]** — [Grocery Gazette](https://www.grocerygazette.co.uk/2025/07/21/profit-warnings-increase-amid-hiked-nic-fees-and-tariffs/)

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** Signals and where to detect them (all need an access and terms check):
  - New CMO, Chief Strategy Officer, Head of Pricing, VP Marketing Analytics or Head of Insight: LinkedIn job-change alerts, company press pages, trade press appointments sections (Campaign, Retail Week, Grocer, Fierce Pharma), UK Companies House officer appointments for director level moves.
  - Job ads for marketing science, econometrics, pricing analyst, revenue growth management, space planning or category analyst roles: LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, company careers pages, aggregator APIs. A large team hiring "marketing science" is also a sign of in house MMM (Robyn/Meridian), which changes the pitch to augment not replace.
  - Profit warnings and trading statements: London Stock Exchange RNS, EDGAR 8-K and 10-K/10-Q for US, and EU exchange announcements.
  - Results mentions of marketing efficiency, pricing, margin, tariffs or markdowns: transcript and filing keyword search (EDGAR full text search for US; UK annual reports and results presentations for others).
  - Store roll outs and closures: results decks, planning applications, trade press.
  - Agency reviews: trade press (Campaign, Adweek, the Drum) and pitch consultancies' news.
  - Tariff exposure: 10-K risk factor language, UK/EU annual report risk language.
  - LinkedIn post themes (measurement, incrementality, MMM, retail media, pricing): social listening tools; check LinkedIn terms before scraping.
- **[INFERENCE]** Highest value combined trigger: new CMO or Head of Analytics in first 90 days plus a flat or reduced marketing budget plus a hiring ad for marketing science. Reasoning: new leaders re-examine measurement and agencies early, and they have a budget to defend (56 percent say it is short).
- **[INFERENCE]** For pharma, signals differ: new omnichannel or commercial excellence head, a product launch (new indication), MFN or pricing deal announcements, and patent expiry (loss of exclusivity) planning.
