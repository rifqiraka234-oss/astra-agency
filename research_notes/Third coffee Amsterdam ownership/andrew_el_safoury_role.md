# Andrew El Safoury: role in Third. (Third Grounds B.V.), his business network, and any link to El Encanto

Research date: 2026-09-25. Status labels: **CONFIRMED** means two or more independent underlying sources. Aggregators that republish the same KvK or BORME record count as one source. **PRIMARY** means an official register or gazette read directly. **SINGLE-SOURCE** means one underlying source only. **UNVERIFIED/INFERRED** is my reasoning, not a sourced fact. Privacy: this file records business roles only. Where a registered address looks residential, I say so and leave the address out.

Headline: the most important new finding is an **official Spanish gazette (BORME) record from 15 June 2023**. It names **"CODFRIED JUERGEN HEINRICH" and "EL SAFOURY ANDREW"** as joint administrators of the same Málaga real-estate and renovation company. Together with Deap Bouwvisie's own 2019 post, which hyperlinks "Adam Horeca groep" to Andrew's LinkedIn profile, this shows that Codfried (the visible controller of Third Grounds B.V.) and El Safoury have been business partners since at least 2019. It also shows they have co-directed a company since 2023. **Free sources still do not name the hidden second officer of Third Grounds B.V.** No link to El Encanto, "Ayad" or Scheldeplein 18 was found.
