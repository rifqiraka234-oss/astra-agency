# Third (Amsterdam coffee/bakery business): founders, owners and operators from press, social, review and people sources

Research date: 2026-09-25. Scope: press, social, reviews, people sources (company registries are deliberately out of scope and only mentioned as pointers for the registry researcher).

Confidence labels used: **CONFIRMED** (2+ independent sources), **SINGLE-SOURCE**, **UNVERIFIED/INFERRED**.

Headline for the report writer: the business is **"Third." ("Modern Bakery and Coffee")**, planned for **Wielingenstraat 22, 1078 KK Amsterdam** (Scheldebuurt/Rivierenbuurt, opposite the RAI). Its live website is **third.amsterdam**, not third.nl. The only person publicly tied to it is **Andrew El Safoury**. His LinkedIn profile headline, as indexed by search engines, reads "Andrew El Safoury - Third | Modern Bakery & Coffee". He is also the self-described founder of the staffing firm This Is Staff and was owner of the Sweetella dessert bar on Warmoesstraat. No press coverage, reviews or owner-signed replies were found, and as of 2026-09-25 the website still says "OPENING SOON". No link to "El Encanto" was found, but Restaurant El Encanto (Scheldeplein 18, 1078 GR) is in the same small neighbourhood.

## Q1. What is Third? (website, self-description, concept, location, status)

### Takeaway
Third. describes itself as a "Modern Bakery and Coffee": a small pastries bakery with specialty coffee at Wielingenstraat 22, 1078 KK Amsterdam. The name plays on "Third Wave" coffee and the "Third Place". It has one planned location. Its website (third.amsterdam, built July/August 2025) still showed "OPENING SOON" on 2026-09-25, and it names no founder or staff. The domain third.nl does not serve this business: it returns a broken WordPress error page. **CONFIRMED** that third.amsterdam and @third_nl are the business, from the site plus 3+ search indexes.

### Cited Findings
- The homepage `<title>` is "Modern Bakery and Coffee". The body text reads, verbatim: "OPENING SOON / meaning / the craft of / speciality coffee / “Third Wave” / feeling / as a social place beyond / home and work / “Third Place” / FOLLOW US ON / Third_nl / Wielingenstraat 22 / 1078 KK Amsterdam / Third.amsterdam" (fetched 2026-09-25). [third.amsterdam](https://third.amsterdam/)

### Inferences
- **INFERRED:** Third. was in pre-opening in the second half of 2025, running a neighbour survey ahead of a "lichte horeca" permit application. On 2026-09-25 the website still says "OPENING SOON" and has not changed since August 2025. I found no reviews or press confirming that it has opened (see Q3). It may not have opened yet, or it may have opened without updating the site. Treat launch status as unverified.
- **INFERRED:** The concept is a small bakery and pastry shop with specialty ("third wave") coffee and a light-horeca seating permit. There is no evidence that it roasts its own coffee (not a roastery). Number of locations: **one** (planned).
- **INFERRED:** The lemlist `companyDomain` "third.nl" is probably a data error or a stale domain. The business's working domain is third.amsterdam and its email is at @third.amsterdam. I could not establish who controls third.nl, or whether it was ever Third.'s domain.
- **INFERRED (web builder, not owner):** The WP user slug "racod" and a second survey-form recipient (a "rodney@" address at racod.nl) point to **RACOD**, a website design agency ("Website laten maken | RACOD Website's & Webshops"). Its testimonials refer to "Rodney van Racod" ([racod.nl](https://www.racod.nl/)). Rodney/RACOD should be treated as the site builder, not an owner. Nothing links him to ownership.

### Gaps
- The Wayback Machine could not be reached from this environment (connection reset, and WebFetch reported "unable to fetch from web.archive.org"). So the history of third.nl, and any earlier about or team pages, remains unchecked.
- The website has no about, team, contact or vacatures pages (the WP API lists only home, survey, survey-success, privacy, cookie and sample pages).
- The actual opening date, if it has opened, is unknown.

## Q2. Who is publicly named as founder, owner or operator? (press, LinkedIn, people sources)

### Takeaway
The only person publicly tied to Third. is **Andrew El Safoury**. His LinkedIn profile (nl.linkedin.com/in/andrew-el-safoury-031328143) is indexed with the title **"Andrew El Safoury - Third | Modern Bakery & Coffee"**, and "Modern Bakery & Coffee" matches Third.'s own site title exactly. This is **SINGLE-SOURCE**: one LinkedIn profile, seen only as search-result titles because direct fetches were blocked (HTTP 999/429). His exact title at Third (founder, owner or other) is **not visible**. No press article names any founder or owner.

### Cited Findings
- Search result title, verbatim: "Andrew El Safoury - Third | Modern Bakery & Coffee", URL nl.linkedin.com/in/andrew-el-safoury-031328143. It appeared in two separate searches ("El Safoury" Amsterdam Third; and Andrew El Safoury Third Modern Bakery & Coffee LinkedIn). [LinkedIn: Andrew El Safoury](https://nl.linkedin.com/in/andrew-el-safoury-031328143)
- An older index entry for the same profile ID shows the earlier headline, verbatim: "Andrew El Safoury - Founder & Managing Director bij This Is Staff | Wij bemiddelen professionals in verschillende branches! | Opzoek naar getraind personeel contact mij gerust." [LinkedIn (www) profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrew-el-safoury-031328143/)
- Caution: one search tool's AI summary (query `site:linkedin.com "Third" Amsterdam bakery coffee founder Wielingenstraat`) stated "'Third' is a new bakery and coffee concept run by founder Andrew El Safoury, and it's located at Wielingenstraat 22". **None of the result links it listed supported that sentence**, so treat it as **UNVERIFIED**. The word "founder" in particular is not confirmed by any quotable source. [search results included e.g. LinkedIn profiles of unrelated people](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lukasgeurts/)
- Circumstantial corroboration (network link). Third.'s website was built by RACOD (Rodney), and RACOD's homepage carries a testimonial from "Jurgen Codfried, CEO @ Deap Bouw Visie" ([racod.nl](https://www.racod.nl/)). - No Dutch or English press coverage naming Third.'s founders was found. Searches in Dutch and English (e.g. "Third Amsterdam Wielingenstraat koffie bakkerij geopend eigenaar", "Third. bakery coffee Wielingenstraat Amsterdam", "Third." Wielingenstraat reviews) returned only the website, Instagram and registry aggregators. Your Little Black Book's list of new coffee bars and bakeries (dated 2 September 2026) does **not** mention Third. [YLBB new coffee bars/bakeries](https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/en/nieuwe-koffiebars-bakeries-broodjeszaken-amsterdam/)

The reasons: he lists "Third | Modern Bakery & Coffee" as his current LinkedIn affiliation, the string matches the site title, he has a stated 10+ year horeca-concept background (Q4), and there is a network link through RACOD and Deap Bouw Visie to his earlier venture Sweetella. His exact role cannot be confirmed from people sources; the registry researcher should cross-check against the Third Grounds B.V. director record.
- **INFERRED:** No co-founder, head baker or manager is publicly named anywhere I could reach.

### Gaps
- The full LinkedIn profile (title at Third, start date, about text) could not be read: LinkedIn returned HTTP 999/429 and Instagram returned a 429 login wall.
- No press coverage was found. I did not run a separate search per outlet for Het Parool, AT5, NH Nieuws, Time Out, Sprudge, European Coffee Trip, Misset Horeca or Culy. The general Dutch and English searches surfaced nothing, which fits a business that had not yet opened or had only just opened.
