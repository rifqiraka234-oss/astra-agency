## Q1. Which "El Encanto" steak/grill restaurants exist (Amsterdam first, then NL, then abroad)?

### Takeaway
One "El Encanto" steakhouse exists in Amsterdam: **Restaurant El Encanto, Scheldeplein 18, 1078 GR Amsterdam (Rivierenbuurt/Scheldebuurt, Zuid)**. It is registered at KvK as 91130379, with the stated purpose of setting up a steakhouse. It is about 60 m from Third (Wielingenstraat 22, 1078 KK) and sits in the same perimeter block. It is almost certainly the El Encanto that matters for the Third cross-reference. The only other Dutch El Encanto steakhouse is on Bonaire, in the Caribbean Netherlands. The other Amsterdam "El Encanto" businesses are hair salons, not restaurants.
