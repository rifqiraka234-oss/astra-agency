# Codfried / Deap Bouwvisie behind "Third.", and the property and permit links between Wielingenstraat 22 and Scheldeplein 18 (El Encanto)

Researched 25 Sep 2026.

**Evidence labels**
- **CONFIRMED**: two or more independent sources. North Data, Company.info, Drimble and similar aggregators all republish KvK data, so together they count as ONE underlying source.
- **SINGLE-SOURCE**: one source only.
- **UNVERIFIED/INFERRED**: not directly sourced.

**Connection grades**: STRONG / MODERATE / WEAK / NONE.

**Privacy**: only business roles are recorded. One registered address that looks residential is described but not given. Mobile numbers, personal e-mails and personal details are left out.

**Main new findings** (not in the earlier notes):
1. Housing corporation **de Alliantie** is the landlord of Scheldeplein 18 (El Encanto) and lets flats throughout the block. That makes it the probable owner of the whole Scheldeplein 2-20 / Wielingenstraat 2-22 / Scheldestraat 56-116 block, probably including Wielingenstraat 22 (Third). This contradicts the earlier note that the two sites have "separate owners".
2. **Jurgen Codfried and Andrew El Safoury are named business partners in "31 Apartments"**, a Málaga (Spain) holiday-rental and property company. The source is 31 Apartments' own website.
3. The 2026 block-wide facade permit has its own case number (**Z2025-054524**). The Vervoerregio crane and scaffolding permit for the block runs **24 Aug 2026 to 31 May 2027**.
4. No source links Codfried, Deap, El Safoury or 31 Apartments to El Encanto, E. Ayad, or the Scheldeplein 18 permits. None of the permit notices names an applicant.

## Q1. What is Codfried's / Deap Bouwvisie's actual role in Third?

### Takeaway
Codfried's documented role is **statutory director of the operating company**. His personal holding, Deap Bouwvisie Holding B.V., is the "Algemeen directeur" of Third Grounds B.V., and the company has one more officer who is hidden. There is **no evidence that he or Deap is Third's landlord or developer**. The premises were let on the open market through a commercial letting agent, and the landlord is most likely de Alliantie (see Q3). His engineering firm has registered itself in postcode 1078KK, and one aggregator places it at Wielingenstraat 22. Deap's track record is design and permits for horeca fit-outs, including one for El Safoury's Sweetella. So Codfried is probably also running the fit-out and permits for Third in-house (INFERRED). The best description is: **co-principal (director) plus in-house building and permit lead, with El Safoury as the likely hospitality partner.**
