# CRM email layer and API / integration architecture (benchmarks: HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio; specifics: Gmail API, lemlist API; GDPR)

Researched 2026-11-06. Context: a fully custom CRM on Postgres for a small dev/design agency (Astra) that runs LinkedIn outreach through lemlist and email through Gmail on Google Workspace. Where a fact comes from this repo's own live-tested lemlist notes, the source is `docs/lemlist-field-index.md` (built by calling every endpoint, 2026-09-21 to 2026-10-22), which is a primary observation of our own account, not vendor docs.

**2025-2026 version/deprecation flags found during research**
- Gmail API now publishes a **daily billing threshold of 80,000,000 quota units per project**, with "full billing details later in 2026 with at least 90 days' notice", and per-method costs are higher than older docs (messages.get 20 units, 60 for full/raw). Page last updated 2026-10-16. — [Gmail API quota](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/reference/quota)
- HubSpot CRM docs now use **date-based path versions** (`/crm/objects/2026-09/contacts`, `/crm/objects/2026-09/{object}/search`, `/automation/sequences/2026-09/...`); older `/crm/v3/` paths still appear in some examples, deprecation not stated on the pages read. — [HubSpot contacts guide](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/objects/contacts/guide); [HubSpot search guide](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/search/guide)
- HubSpot **webhooks v4 is a journal you poll** (no `targetUrl`); v3 push webhooks are now under "legacy" docs. — [HubSpot webhooks](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api/webhooks); [Webhooks v3 (legacy)](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/legacy/webhooks/guide)
- lemlist: since **30 March 2026** unsubscribes emit `entityUnsubscribed` / `variableUnsubscribed` instead of `emailsUnsubscribed`; `skipped`, `emailsSendFailed`, `opportunitiesDone` webhook types removed. — [lemlist Add Webhook](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/webhooks/add-webhook)
- Attio webhook event names V1 (`entry.created` etc.) deprecated in favour of V2 (`list-entry.created` etc.). — [Attio webhooks](https://docs.attio.com/rest-api/guides/webhooks)
- Salesforce Einstein Activity Capture: Summer '25 added "Sync Email as Salesforce Activity" so captured email becomes native EmailMessage records (secondary sources only). — [asagarwal.com](https://www.asagarwal.com/?p=32826); [massmailer](https://massmailer.io/blog/sync-email-as-salesforce-activity/)

## 1. HubSpot email layer: connected inbox, logging vs tracking, auto-association, never-log, engagement fields, tracking, templates/snippets, sequences, subscription types

### Takeaway
HubSpot separates **logging** (saving a copy of the email to CRM timelines and associating it to contact/company/deal) from **tracking** (pixel opens and redirected clicks feeding engagement properties), gates both behind a "never log" list and a per-contact lawful basis, and runs **sequences** as a step list (automated emails threaded or new, manual email/call/LinkedIn/general tasks) with business-day delays and default auto-unenroll on reply or meeting booked. Send volumes are capped per seat (500/day Pro, 1,000/day Enterprise for sequences, rolling 24h, 3 emails/min in bulk enrollment) and by the mailbox provider.

### Cited Findings
**Sequences (structure and behaviour)**
- Step types: automated email (HubSpot sends; can start a new thread or reply to a previous sequence email), manual email task, call task, general task, Sales Navigator InMail task and connection-request task (LinkedIn steps are reminders, need the Sales Navigator integration). — [HubSpot: create and edit sequences](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/sequences/create-and-edit-sequences)
- "Each sequence is limited to 10 email templates", unlimited task reminders; delays in business days, an email can be delayed up to 90 business days; task delay 0 creates the task right after the previous step. — [same](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/sequences/create-and-edit-sequences)
- Settings: business-days-only on by default; send window (HubSpot picks time within window using past open data and time zone); tasks created at start of the account's calendar day in the enrolment time zone, due 5pm. — [same](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/sequences/create-and-edit-sequences)
- Default unenrollment: contact replies to any sequence email, or books a meeting via a meeting link in a step. Per trigger you can unenroll just that contact or **all contacts at the same company**. More triggers via workflows. — [same](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/sequences/create-and-edit-sequences)
- Personalization tokens usable in task titles and notes; snippets insertable into task notes; a template can be attached to a manual email task and opens in the composer from the task queue. — [same](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/sequences/create-and-edit-sequences)
- Sequences API (date-versioned `2026-09`): `GET .../serviceaccounts/sequences`, `POST /automation/sequences/2026-09/enrollments` with `sequenceId`, `contactId`, `senderEmail` (must be a connected inbox), `GET .../enrollments/contact/{contactId}`; scopes `automation.sequences.read`, `automation.sequences.enrollments.write`; user-level apps only; **1,000 enrollments per account inbox per day**. — [HubSpot Sequences API guide](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/2026-09-beta/automation/sequences/guide)

**Send limits**
- Sequences: Professional seat 500 emails/day, Enterprise 1,000/day; counts sends not enrollments; rolling 24h; bulk enrollment limited to "a maximum of three emails per minute". One-to-one provider limits listed by HubSpot: Gmail Free 350/day, Google Apps 1,000/day; Pro/Enterprise can set a custom daily limit; provider limits can't be overridden. — [HubSpot sales email send limits](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/connected-email/sales-email-send-limits)
- A HubSpot community post says the old 150/day enrollment cap was removed in favour of the 500/1,000 send caps. — [HubSpot blog: sequences limit change](https://blog.hubspot.com/customers/sequences-limit-change-blog-post) (via search summary; not opened)

**Logging, association, never-log**
- Never log list: emails to addresses/domains on it "will never save to your CRM. Email tracking activity will also not appear on the contact's timeline." — [HubSpot Office 365 add-in settings](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/connected-email/customize-your-hubspot-sales-office-365-add-in); [Chrome extension settings](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/connected-email/customize-your-hubspot-sales-chrome-extension-default-configuration-options) (via search summary)
- Super Admins can configure which associated records emails auto-log to (e.g. only associated deals); defaults apply to extension sends, sends from HubSpot, manual logs, BCC/forwarding addresses. Emails from shared inboxes (help desk/conversations replies) "aren't automatically associated". Page last updated 13 Oct 2026. — [HubSpot: configure automatic activity associations](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/object-settings/configure-automatic-activity-associations)
- Community answer (not official): an email to a contact is added to timelines of only the **five most recent open deals** (not closed won/lost); in 1:1 threads, association applies to contacts on the first email and later replies stay with those contacts. — [HubSpot Community](https://community.hubspot.com/t/automated-emails-not-posting-to-deals/130883); [HubSpot Ideas](https://community.hubspot.com/t5/HubSpot-Ideas/Automatically-associate-emails-to-open-deals-and-tickets/idi-p/480799) (search summaries)

**Open/click tracking and engagement fields**
- Opens: invisible 1-pixel image; fails on plain text, image blocking, filters. If no pixel load but a click or reply occurs, HubSpot infers the open retroactively (timestamp of first click/reply, only when opens go 0 to 1). — [HubSpot open and click tracking](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/connected-email/understand-hubspot-sales-email-open-and-click-tracking)
- Clicks: hyperlinked text rewritten to `*.hs-sales-engage*.com` redirect; one custom tracking domain per account; requires Sales Hub seat. — [same](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/connected-email/understand-hubspot-sales-email-open-and-click-tracking)
- Bot filtering: "will not generate open or click events for sales emails when the interaction comes from a non-human source"; self-opens from the sent folder can trigger notifications. — [same](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/connected-email/understand-hubspot-sales-email-open-and-click-tracking)
- Multiple recipients share one pixel, so opens can show as "Someone"; "Someone" events are excluded from reports. Engagement properties named: "Recent sales email open date", "Recent sales email clicked date", "Last engagement date"; not updated for contacts without lawful basis when privacy settings are on. — [same](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/connected-email/understand-hubspot-sales-email-open-and-click-tracking)

**Subscription types / lawful basis**
- Without data privacy settings on, unsubscribe links are not included by default in one-to-one and sequence emails; the link routes to the "One to One" subscription type. With privacy settings on, the contact's **legal basis to communicate** must be validated before a one-to-one send (a subscription or a one-time legal basis), and a separate **legal basis to process** is needed to store and to track the contact; it does not record consent to tracking. — [HubSpot: manage unsubscribe links for one-to-one emails](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/one-to-one-email/manage-unsubscribe-links-for-one-to-one-emails); [Email tracking consent guidelines](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/privacy-and-consent/email-tracking-consent-guidelines) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- The minimum HubSpot-parity email model for Astra is: `email_messages` (one row per RFC822 message, logged copy) separate from `email_events` (open/click/bounce/reply rows), with association to contact/company/deal decided by rules at log time (participants matched by address, company by domain, deal by "open deals for that contact/company", capped), plus a `never_log` table of addresses and domains checked before insert.
- Sequence engine needs: steps (email_auto / email_manual / task / linkedin_task), per-step delay in business days, `thread_mode` (new vs reply-to-previous), send window, per-sender daily cap on a rolling 24h window, per-minute throttle, and unenroll triggers (reply, meeting booked, bounce, unsubscribe) with an "unenroll whole company" option. Since Astra's LinkedIn steps live in lemlist, the CRM's own sequences can be email-only plus tasks, and lemlist events can feed the same unenroll logic.
- Treat "opened" as weak signal (see section 3 on MPP) and replies as the real engagement metric, matching HubSpot's own inferred-open-from-reply behaviour.

### Gaps
- Official HubSpot KB pages for the connected-inbox setup, never-log list and the precise default email association rules returned 404 or did not contain the defaults ("Associate activities with records" article not fetched); the five-open-deals rule is from community posts only.
- HubSpot snippet and template token syntax and limits were not opened.
- HubSpot's handling of inbound emails from connected inbox (auto-log replies only to tracked threads vs all mail) not confirmed from a primary source.

## 2. Salesforce: Einstein Activity Capture (EAC) / Inbox, Enhanced Email, EmailMessage

### Takeaway
Salesforce historically had two email models: **Enhanced Email** (email stored as the first-class `EmailMessage` object with `EmailMessageRelation` junction rows to Contact/Lead/User) and **EAC** (emails synced from Gmail/Outlook into a separate store shown on the timeline but not queryable). Summer '25 added an option to write EAC email as native EmailMessage records. The lesson for a custom CRM: store email as real rows you can query, with a participants junction table.

### Cited Findings
- EmailMessage is available for orgs using Email-to-Case or Enhanced Email ("automatically enabled for most customers"); `MessageIdentifier` is "The ID of the email message". Recipient ID lists link to `EmailMessageRelation` records, "the relationship between an email message and a Contact, Lead, or User record"; reply/forward require the sender address to exist as an EmailMessageRelation with `RelationType` of `FromAddress`. — [Salesforce EmailMessage reference (API 214)](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/atlas.en-us.214.0.api.meta/object_ref/sforce_api_objects_emailmessage.htm) (via search summary; current docs returned 403 to our fetcher)
- EAC "Captured" emails historically live in a separate data store, show on the Activity Timeline but cannot be reported on, queried with SOQL, used in Flows or exported in standard backup; default retention 6 months (24 months paid); deactivating EAC deletes captured activity from the timeline. — [Revenue Grid](https://revenuegrid.com/blog/einstein-activity-capture-limitations/) (vendor of an alternative, promotional)
- Summer '25 "Sync Email as Salesforce Activity" writes captured email as native EmailMessage and Task records usable by reports, APIs, triggers, Flow; applies going forward; backfill reportedly capped at 180 days and 50,000 emails per user; irreversible; consumes data storage; header-only capture option; attachments and opens/clicks not captured natively. — [asagarwal.com](https://www.asagarwal.com/?p=32826); [massmailer](https://massmailer.io/blog/sync-email-as-salesforce-activity/); [Salesforce Break](https://salesforcebreak.com/2026/03/04/einstein-activity-capture-guide/) (all secondary)
- Change Data Capture / high-volume platform events: storage not guaranteed beyond **72 hours**; `ReplayId` is opaque (store as bytes, never compute from it), not guaranteed unique across org migrations, use `EventUuid` for dedup; resubscribe with -1 (new only) or -2 (all stored). — [Salesforce Streaming API durability](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/atlas.en-us.260.0.api_streaming.meta/api_streaming/using_streaming_api_durability.htm) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- EAC's history is the cautionary tale: email that is visible but not queryable becomes useless for reporting and automation. Store every message in Postgres with a participants junction (`email_message_participants(message_id, role IN from/to/cc/bcc, address, contact_id NULL)`), mirroring EmailMessageRelation.
- Salesforce's header-only capture option suggests a useful GDPR lever: a per-mailbox or per-domain setting to store metadata only (no body).
- The CDC replay model (opaque cursor + event UUID for dedup + bounded retention window) is the same shape as Gmail's historyId (section 3) and should be the template for Astra's own outbound change feed.

### Gaps
- developer.salesforce.com and help.salesforce.com returned 403 to both WebFetch and curl, so current EmailMessage fields (ThreadIdentifier, IsClientManaged, etc.), the full RelationType picklist, External ID upsert (`PATCH /sobjects/{Object}/{ExternalIdField}/{value}`) and Bulk API 2.0 limits are **not sourced here**. Treat Salesforce details beyond the above as unverified.

## 3. Gmail API implementation (scopes and verification, incremental sync, push, threading, sending, quotas, Workspace delegation, deliverability)

### Takeaway
For an internal tool on Astra's own Workspace domain, mark the OAuth app **Internal** (or use a service account with domain-wide delegation): internal apps are exempt from OAuth verification and the 100-user cap. Use `gmail.readonly` (or `gmail.modify` if you need labels/read state) plus `gmail.send`; do a one-time full sync with `messages.list` + batched `messages.get`, then incremental sync with `history.list` from a stored `historyId`, driven by `users.watch` Pub/Sub notifications renewed daily, with a 404 on history meaning "full resync". Threading needs `threadId` on the send plus RFC 2822 `In-Reply-To`/`References` and a matching Subject.

### Cited Findings
**Scopes and classification**
- Restricted: `https://mail.google.com/`, `gmail.readonly`, `gmail.compose`, `gmail.insert`, `gmail.modify`, `gmail.metadata`, `gmail.settings.basic`, `gmail.settings.sharing`. Sensitive: `gmail.send` (plus add-on metadata/readonly scopes). Non-sensitive: `gmail.labels` and add-on action scopes. "If you store restricted scope data on servers (or transmit), then you must go through a security assessment." Google advises the narrowest scope. — [Gmail API scopes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/auth/scopes)
- Note: `gmail.metadata` (headers + labels, no bodies) is itself restricted, so it does not avoid restricted-scope rules. — [same](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/auth/scopes)

**Verification exemptions**
- Verification not needed for: personal use (<100 users, unverified screen and cap still apply); **internal use** ("only used by people in your Google Workspace or Cloud Identity organization", must be designated internal-only, may need admin approval); development/testing; admin-trusted apps added in the Admin console. — [Google Cloud: When is verification not needed](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13464323?hl=en)
- An Internal-marked app "will not be subject to the unverified app screen or the 100-user cap". — [OAuth API verification FAQs](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/9110914?hl=en-GB) (via search summary)
- Conflicting: Google's brand-verification page says an app targeting one Workspace org and installed domain-wide doesn't need brand verification, "But if your app utilizes restricted or sensitive scopes, app verification is required." — [Brand verification](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/production-readiness/brand-verification) (via search summary). The "when not needed" page does not carve restricted scopes out of the internal exemption. — [same](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13464323?hl=en)
- CASA security assessment for restricted scopes: annual recertification 12 months from the letter of assessment, Tier 1 self-assessment vs Tier 2 independent assessor, ~USD 500/year cited. — [singhamandeep.com](https://singhamandeep.com/gmail-api-restricted-scope-verification/); [Unipile](https://www.unipile.com/gmail-api-scopes-guide/) (third-party, unverified)
- Domain-wide delegation lets a service account impersonate users without per-user consent. — [Google Meet API auth guide](https://developers.google.com/workspace/meet/api/guides/authenticate-authorize) (via search summary)

**Incremental sync**
- Full sync: `messages.list` → batch `messages.get` (`format=full`/`raw` first time, `minimal` for cached) → store `historyId` of the most recent message. — [Gmail sync guide](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/guides/sync)
- Partial sync: `history.list` with `startHistoryId`; records include affected message IDs and change type (messages added, labels modified). History is "typically available for at least one week and often longer" but can be much shorter; an out-of-range `startHistoryId` returns **HTTP 404 → do a full sync**. — [same](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/guides/sync)

**Push notifications**
- Create a Pub/Sub topic + subscription (push webhook or pull), grant publish to `gmail-api-push@system.gserviceaccount.com` (domain-restricted sharing org policy may block this). `users.watch` with `topicName`, optional `labelIds` + `labelFilterBehavior: INCLUDE`. Returns `historyId` and `expiration`. — [Gmail push guide](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/guides/push)
- Must re-call `watch` "at least once every 7 days"; Google recommends **once per day**. Payload `message.data` is base64url JSON `{emailAddress, historyId}`; then call `history.list` from your last stored historyId. Max **one event per second per user**, excess dropped; acknowledge every message (HTTP 200 for push); unacked messages retried; add a periodic `history.list` fallback because notifications can be delayed or dropped; `users.stop` to end. — [same](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/guides/push)

**Threading and sending**
- `threadId`: to add a message or draft to a thread, "The requested `threadId` must be specified on the `Message` or `Draft.Message`", "The `References` and `In-Reply-To` headers must be set in compliance with RFC 2822", "The `Subject` headers must match." `raw` is "The entire email message in an RFC 2822 formatted and base64url encoded string." `internalDate` is epoch ms and determines inbox ordering. Methods: batchDelete, batchModify, delete, get, import, insert, list, modify, send, trash, untrash. — [users.messages resource](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/reference/rest/v1/users.messages)
- Send via `messages.send` with `raw`, or `drafts.send` for a draft. — [Gmail sending guide](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/guides/sending)

**Quotas (page last updated 2026-10-16)**
- 1,200,000 units/min per project; **6,000 units/min per user per project**; daily billing threshold 80,000,000 units/project ("Google will share full billing details later in 2026 with at least 90 days' notice"). Costs: `history.list` 2, `messages.list` 5, `messages.modify` 5, `messages.get` 20 (60 for full/raw/html), `threads.list` 10, `threads.get` 40 (120 full), `messages.batchModify` 50, `messages.send` 100, `drafts.send` 100, `watch` 100, `messages.attachments.get` 20. Max 500 recipients per message. — [Gmail API quota](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/reference/quota) (rechecked by direct curl the same session)

**Workspace sending limits**
- Per user, rolling 24h: 2,000 messages/day (1,500 mail merge, 500 trial); 10,000 total recipients; 3,000 external recipients; unique recipients 3,000 total / 2,000 external (500 external on trial). Per message: Gmail API 500 recipients, SMTP 100. Exceeding blocks sending for up to 24h. Trial limits rise only after USD 100 cumulative paid, up to 75 days. — [Gmail sending limits in Google Workspace](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/gmail/gmail-sending-limits-in-google-workspace)

**Deliverability and tracking**
- All senders (since 1 Feb 2024): SPF or DKIM, valid forward/reverse DNS, TLS, spam rate under 0.3%, RFC 5322 formatting. Bulk senders (5,000+/day to Gmail): SPF and DKIM, DMARC (p=none allowed), From-domain alignment, **one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) for marketing and subscribed messages**, spam rate below 0.10% and never 0.30%. — [Google email sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126)
- Apple Mail Privacy Protection prefetches images including the tracking pixel whether or not the email is read; vendor estimates put inflated opens at roughly half to 70-75% of reported opens; clicks are more reliable but B2B security scanners also click/open. — [Validity](https://www.validity.com/blog/case-closed-the-mystery-of-declining-email-open-rates/); [Litmus](https://www.litmus.com/blog/apple-mail-privacy-protection-for-marketers); [Postmark](https://postmarkapp.com/support/article/1257-open-tracking-and-apple-mail) (vendor sources; Apple publishes no mechanism details)

### Inferences
- **Recommended auth path for Astra:** a Google Cloud project owned by Astra's Workspace, OAuth consent screen set to **Internal**, each team member connects their own mailbox with scopes `gmail.readonly` + `gmail.send` (add `gmail.modify` only if the CRM must apply labels or mark read). This avoids the unverified-app screen and verification per the "when not needed" page. The CASA "store restricted data on servers" language sits in the verification context for public apps; given the brand-verification page conflict, confirm with Google before ever opening the app to client domains. Domain-wide delegation (service account) is the alternative when an admin wants to sync all mailboxes without each user clicking consent, but it grants mailbox-wide access across the domain, so scope it to named users in code.
- **Sync design per mailbox:** table `mail_sync_state(mailbox_id, history_id, watch_expiration, last_full_sync_at, last_partial_sync_at, status)`. Pub/Sub push → enqueue job keyed by mailbox (coalesce, since notifications only carry the latest historyId) → `history.list` from stored id → fetch new message ids with `messages.get(format=metadata)` first (20 units) and only fetch `full`/`raw` (60 units) for messages that pass the contact/never-log filter. Daily cron renews `watch`; an hourly cron runs `history.list` as the dropped-notification fallback; 404 triggers a bounded full resync (e.g. last 90 days).
- **Store both IDs:** Gmail `id`/`threadId` (mailbox-scoped, differ between the sender's and recipient's mailbox) and the RFC822 `Message-ID`, `In-Reply-To`, `References` headers (global). Dedupe across team mailboxes on `Message-ID` so an internal CC doesn't log twice; build CRM threads from Gmail `threadId` per mailbox and stitch across mailboxes by `References`.
- **Sending from the CRM:** compose MIME, set our own `Message-ID` before sending so the logged row can be written idempotently before the API call returns; for replies set `threadId`, `In-Reply-To`, `References`, identical Subject. At 100 units per send against 6,000/min/user, quota is not the constraint; Workspace's 2,000/day and 2,000 unique external/day are, and a sequencer should cap far lower for cold outreach.
- **Tracking:** Astra's volume is far under the 5,000/day bulk-sender line, but SPF, DKIM and DMARC with alignment cost nothing and should be on. Default open tracking off for cold 1:1 sends (pixel + MPP noise + it's a spam/consent signal); if click tracking is used, use a custom tracking domain aligned to the sending domain. Make reply detection (new inbound message in a thread we started) the primary engagement signal.

### Gaps
- No Google primary source found that states explicitly whether an **Internal** app using restricted Gmail scopes and storing data server-side still needs a CASA assessment; the two Google pages read are silent or conflicting.
- Max `raw` upload size for `messages.send` (commonly cited 35 MB) not confirmed on the pages read.
- Batch HTTP request size limits for `messages.get` not stated on the sync guide.

## 4. lemlist API: what can be pulled, webhooks, rate limits

### Takeaway
lemlist exposes campaigns, sequences, leads, contacts, companies, activities, inbox threads and tasks over REST at `api.lemlist.com/api` with Basic auth (empty user, API key as password), 20 requests per 2 seconds per key. Webhooks (`POST /api/hooks`) cover ~80 event types including `linkedinInviteAccepted`, `linkedinReplied`, `emailsReplied`, but authenticate only with a **plain shared `secret` in the JSON body** (no HMAC), document no retry policy, and auto-disable on 404/410. So webhooks should be the fast path and a polling reconciler the source of truth. Our own live tests show the activities endpoint is reliable for acceptances but under-reports sends.

### Cited Findings
**Auth and rate limits**
- Base `api.lemlist.com`; `Authorization: Basic` with empty login and API key as password. Rate limit **20 requests per 2 seconds**, on all routes, per API key; headers `Retry-After` (seconds), `X-RateLimit-Limit`, `X-RateLimit-Remaining`, `X-RateLimit-Reset` (a date string). — [lemlist rate limits](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/getting-started/rate-limits.md); [Delete Webhook (OpenAPI header)](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/webhooks/delete-webhook.md)
- Our environment: Bearer does not work, Basic does; a non-existent route answers **200 with the app's HTML**, so a non-JSON body means a wrong path, not an empty result. — `docs/lemlist-field-index.md` ("The API direct", 2026-10-22)

**What can be pulled (endpoint inventory)**
- Campaigns (get many, get, settings, stats, batch stats, reports, export leads v2, start/pause), sequences and steps, schedules, senders; leads (create in campaign, get by email/ID, update, variables, pause/resume, unsubscribe, mark interested/not interested, skip step, personalize step); contacts and companies (upsert by email/LinkedIn URL or domain/LinkedIn URL, get many by up to 100 ids, merge up to 10, delete cascading to leads/opportunities/lists/inbox/activities, notes, lists, CRM link); inbox (get many inboxes, get contact messages, send email with `replyToActivityId`, send LinkedIn/WhatsApp/SMS, drafts, labels); tasks (create, search, update, ignore); activities; CRM logs. — [lemlist llms.txt index](https://developer.lemlist.com/llms.txt)
- `GET /activities` requires `version=v2`; filters `type`, `campaignId`, `leadId`, `isFirst`, `minDate`/`maxDate` (or `startDate`/`endDate`, Unix seconds or ISO 8601), `offset`, `limit` default and max **100**. Activities carry `stepId` (stable, prefer over positional `sequenceStep`, older rows backfilled progressively) and new `journeyDepth`. — [Get Many Activities](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/activities/get-many-activities.md)
- `GET /inbox/{contactId}` with `userId`, `limit`, `skip`, `markAsRead` (default false); campaign messages carry `stepId`, `sequenceId`, `sequenceStep`. — [Get Contact Messages](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/inbox/get-contact-messages.md)
- Live behaviour in our account: thread route pages **10 per page, newest first**; text lives in two fields (`message` for what we sent via lemlist, `text` for LinkedIn-synced items including their replies and the connect note); `GET /api/inbox?userId=...` ignores `listId` and `search` and only lists threads with a reply; LinkedIn send is `POST /api/inbox/linkedin` with `sendUserId`, `contactId`, `message`. — `docs/lemlist-field-index.md`
- Live reliability: `type=linkedinInviteAccepted` returned 320 = `channelMetrics.linkedinInvitationAccepted` 320 (exact). `type=linkedinSent` returned 298 and missed known sends; 260 of 298 were connect notes (`emailTemplateName: "linkedinInvite"`), and messages sent by hand from the inbox carry `campaignId: null` and are **not attached to the lead's activity history**. A wrong `type` value returns `{}` not an error. — `docs/lemlist-field-index.md` sections 6.1-6.4 and "Per lead activity history misses inbox sends"

**Webhooks**
- `POST https://api.lemlist.com/api/hooks`, body `targetUrl` (required), `type` (optional; omitted = all events), `secret` (optional); query params `campaignId`, `isFirst`, `zapId`. Needs "Edit integrations" permission. Returns `_id` (`hoo_…`), `targetUrl`, `createdAt`, `type`. 409 when at cap or URL already registered. — [lemlist Add Webhook](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/webhooks/add-webhook)
- Event types include lead states (`contacted`, `hooked`, `attracted`, `warmed`, `interested`, `notInterested`); email (`emailsSent`, `emailsOpened`, `emailsClicked`, `emailsReplied`, `emailsBounced`, `emailsFailed`, `emailsInterested`, `emailsNotInterested`); unsubscribe (`entityUnsubscribed`, `variableUnsubscribed`, `emailsUnsubscribed`); LinkedIn (`linkedinSent`, `linkedinOpened`, `linkedinReplied`, `linkedinInterested`, `linkedinNotInterested`, `linkedinSendFailed`, `linkedinVisitDone`, `linkedinInviteDone`, `linkedinInviteFailed`, **`linkedinInviteAccepted`**, like/follow/endorse/voice note/withdraw variants); WhatsApp, SMS, Aircall, API, manual, `annotated` (tasks), workspace (`paused`, `resumed`, `stopped`, `campaignComplete`, `connectionIssue`, `sendLimitReached`, `customDomainErrors`, `lemwarmPaused`), enrichment, contact sourcing, `inboxLabelUpdated`, `signalRegistered`, `deliverabilityAlertTriggered`. — [same](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/webhooks/add-webhook)
- Payload mirrors the activity: `_id`, `type`, `teamId`, `createdAt`, `campaignId`, `sequenceId`, `sequenceStep`, `stepId`, `leadId`, `contactId`, plus `secret` if set; email events add `to`/`cc`/`bcc` and `isThirdPartyReply`. — [same](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/webhooks/add-webhook)
- Security: the secret is sent **as a field in the JSON body**, "a shared-value check, not a cryptographic signature"; stored encrypted, never returned, cannot be changed (rotate = delete + recreate). Retry behaviour not documented. Max **200 webhooks per workspace** (disabled ones count); `targetUrl` must be unique; webhook is **disabled** when destination returns 404/410 or host doesn't resolve. List via `GET /hooks` (shows `disabled`, `disabledReason`, `lastErrorStatus`); delete `DELETE /hooks/{hookId}`. — [same](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/webhooks/add-webhook); [Delete Webhook](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/webhooks/delete-webhook.md)

### Inferences
- **Ingestion design:** one webhook (all types) to an unguessable URL path, verify the body `secret` in constant time, write the raw payload to `inbound_events(source='lemlist', external_event_id=_id, payload jsonb, received_at)` with a unique index on `(source, external_event_id)` for idempotency, return 200 fast, process async. Never return 404/410 from that endpoint during deploys (lemlist disables the hook); return 503 instead.
- **Reconciler as source of truth:** because retries are undocumented and our tests show `linkedinSent` gaps and inbox sends missing from lead history, run a scheduled pull: `linkedinInviteAccepted` activities by `minDate` cursor (reliable), and per-contact `GET /inbox/{contactId}` for any contact with a new event, storing a `sync_cursors(source, stream, cursor_value)` row. Budget: 20 req/2s ≈ 600/min, honour `Retry-After`.
- **Mapping:** store lemlist `ctc_` contactId, `lea_` leadId, `cpn_` companyId, `cam_` campaignId and `act_` activity ids in an `external_ids(entity_type, entity_id, system, external_id)` table; join LinkedIn conversation rows on `contactLinkedinUrl`. Distinguish connect notes by `emailTemplateName = linkedinInvite`, not body length.
- Unenroll the CRM's own Gmail sequences on lemlist `linkedinReplied`/`emailsReplied`/`*Unsubscribed` events so one reply stops all channels.

### Gaps
- lemlist does not document webhook retry count, timeout, ordering, or delivery IP ranges; no HMAC signature exists.
- Whether `linkedinSent` webhooks fire for messages sent by hand from the lemlist inbox (which the activities endpoint omits) was not tested.

## 5. CRM API and integration design: HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio benchmarks and recommendations for a custom Postgres CRM

### Takeaway
The benchmarks converge on: object-centric REST (`/objects/{type}`), a POST search endpoint with OR-of-AND filter groups and cursor paging, batch endpoints capped at 100, upsert by a declared unique `idProperty`/external id, typed associations as first-class rows, and change notification either pushed (HMAC-signed, retried for ~3 days, at-least-once with an idempotency id) or pulled from a journal/replay cursor with bounded retention. For a small team, REST + Postgres + a Standard Webhooks style outbound layer is enough; GraphQL adds cost without a consumer that needs it.

### Cited Findings
**HubSpot**
- Rate limits, private apps: Free/Starter 100 per 10s and 250,000/day; Professional 190 per 10s and 625,000/day; Enterprise 190 per 10s and 1,000,000/day; API Limit Increase 250 per 10s (+1,000,000/day, max two). Public OAuth apps 110 per 10s per installing account. 429 on breach; error responses should stay under 5% of daily requests; daily limit resets at midnight account time. — [HubSpot API usage guidelines](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/developer-tooling/platform/usage-guidelines)
- Search: `POST /crm/objects/2026-09/{object}/search`; up to **5 filterGroups, 6 filters per group, 18 total**; filters ANDed within a group, groups ORed; operators `LT LTE GT GTE EQ NEQ BETWEEN IN NOT_IN HAS_PROPERTY NOT_HAS_PROPERTY CONTAINS_TOKEN NOT_CONTAINS_TOKEN`; one sort; `limit` max 200; `after` cursor; **10,000 result cap**; **5 requests/second per account**; new/updated records may take moments to appear; query up to 3,000 chars; association search via `associations.{objectType}`. — [HubSpot search guide](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/search/guide)
- Objects: batch operations limited to **100 records**; `POST .../contacts/batch/upsert` with `idProperty` = `email` or a custom unique-value property, value in `id`; partial upserts not supported when `idProperty` is email. Associations inline on create via `associations[{to:{id}, types:[{associationCategory, associationTypeId}]}]`. Email is the primary unique identifier; additional emails also unique. Merge via `objectIdToMerge` + `primaryObjectId`. DELETE moves to recycling bin (restorable). — [HubSpot contacts guide](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/objects/contacts/guide)
- Associations v4 batch read limited to 100 IDs per request from 6 Jan 2025. — [HubSpot changelog](https://developers.hubspot.com/changelog/bug-fix-batch-read-limit-for-hubspot-associations-api) (via search summary)
- Webhooks: v4 subscription types `OBJECT` (create, update, delete, merge, restore, snapshot), `ASSOCIATION` (added/removed), `APP_LIFECYCLE_EVENT`; events are written to a **journal the app polls** ("v4 endpoints do not include a `targetUrl` field"); Journal API 100 req/s, Subscriptions 50, Snapshots 10 per app. — [HubSpot webhooks](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api/webhooks)
- Legacy v3 push signature: `X-HubSpot-Signature-v3` = base64 HMAC-SHA256 over method + URI + body + timestamp, with `X-HubSpot-Request-Timestamp`; reject if older than 5 minutes. — [HubSpot request validation](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/apps/developer-platform/build-apps/authentication/request-validation); [v3 signature changelog](https://developers.hubspot.com/changelog/introducing-version-3-of-webhook-signatures) (via search summary)
- Third-party descriptions of v3 push delivery: up to 100 events per request, up to 10 retries (window cited as 24h by one source, 3 days by another), 5s timeout, 4xx except 429 not retried. — [Hookdeck guide](https://hookdeck.com/webhooks/platforms/guide-to-hubspot-webhooks-features-and-best-practices) (secondary, sources disagree)

**Salesforce**
- CDC/platform events: 72-hour retention, opaque `ReplayId`, dedupe on `EventUuid`, replay -1/-2. — [Salesforce Streaming durability](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/atlas.en-us.260.0.api_streaming.meta/api_streaming/using_streaming_api_durability.htm) (via search summary)

**Attio**
- Webhooks: payloads carry identifiers (`workspace_id`, `list_id`, `entry_id`, `attribute_id`, `parent_record_id`), i.e. thin events; retry up to **10 times with exponential backoff over ~3 days**, then marked degraded with an email; 5s timeout; 2xx = success; **at-least-once**, dedupe on `Idempotency-Key` header (same across retries); signature `Attio-Signature` = hex HMAC-SHA256 of raw body; delivery capped at 25 req/s per target URL; filters with `$and`/`$or`, `equals`/`not_equals`; uniqueness on URL + event + filter (409 on duplicate). — [Attio webhooks guide](https://docs.attio.com/rest-api/guides/webhooks)
- Attio API limits cited as 100 reads/s and 25 writes/s. — [ZoomInfo review of Attio API](https://pipeline.zoominfo.com/sales/attio-api) (third-party)

**Standard Webhooks spec (vendor-neutral)**
- Headers `webhook-id` (stable across retries, use as idempotency key), `webhook-timestamp` (seconds), `webhook-signature` (`v1,` + base64 HMAC-SHA256 over `id.timestamp.payload`, secret `whsec_` 24-64 random bytes; multiple space-separated signatures allow zero-downtime rotation; ed25519 `v1a` option). Constant-time compare; sign the exact bytes sent. Example retry schedule: immediate, 5s, 5m, 30m, 2h, 5h, 10h, 14h, 20h, 24h (last at ~75.5h); 410 disables endpoint; 429/502/504 throttle and honour `retry-after`; 3xx is failure; 15-30s timeout. Thin payloads are easier to evolve and audit; full payloads save calls. — [Standard Webhooks spec](https://github.com/standard-webhooks/standard-webhooks/blob/main/spec/standard-webhooks.md)

### Inferences
- **REST, not GraphQL.** Consumers are Astra's own UI, scripts (`tools/*.py`), Claude routines and a handful of connectors. Mirror HubSpot's shape: `GET/POST/PATCH /v1/objects/{type}`, `POST /v1/objects/{type}/search` (filter groups OR-of-AND, cursor `after`, limit ≤ 200), `POST /v1/objects/{type}/batch/{create|update|upsert}` (≤ 100, upsert by `idProperty` that must have a unique index), `PUT /v1/associations/{from}/{id}/{to}/{id}` with a typed `association_type`. Postgres handles search directly; no 10,000 cap or indexing lag needed.
- **API keys with scopes.** Table `api_keys(id, name, hashed_key, prefix, scopes text[], created_by, last_used_at, revoked_at)`; scopes like `contacts.read`, `contacts.write`, `email.send`, `webhooks.manage`; show the key once, store a hash.
- **Idempotency keys.** Accept `Idempotency-Key` on POSTs; `idempotency_keys(key, api_key_id, request_hash, response_status, response_body, created_at)` with 24h TTL, returning the stored response on replay. Same table pattern deduplicates inbound webhooks.
- **External ids.** `external_ids(entity_type, entity_id, system, external_id, external_url, synced_at)` with unique `(system, external_id)` and unique `(entity_type, entity_id, system)`: holds lemlist `ctc_/lea_/cpn_/cam_`, Gmail `threadId`/`Message-ID`, LinkedIn URL, Companies House/KVK numbers. Upserts from connectors resolve through it.
- **Sync state/cursors.** `sync_cursors(connector, account_id, stream, cursor jsonb, last_success_at, last_error, backoff_until)`; one row for Gmail historyId per mailbox, lemlist activities `minDate` per type, etc. Pattern mirrors Salesforce ReplayId and Gmail historyId: opaque cursor + bounded retention + "full resync on expiry".
- **Outbound webhooks.** Implement Standard Webhooks headers and signing, thin payloads (`{type, object_type, object_id, changed_properties, occurred_at}`), transactional outbox (`events` row written in the same Postgres transaction as the change), a worker that delivers to `webhook_endpoints(url, secret, event_types, status)` and logs `webhook_deliveries(attempt, status_code, next_attempt_at)` on the documented retry schedule, disabling on 410 or after the final attempt. Also expose `GET /v1/events?after=<cursor>` (HubSpot v4 journal style) so a consumer that missed webhooks can catch up; retain ≥ 7 days.
- **Inbound connectors.** One `inbound_events` table (source, external_event_id unique, payload, status, processed_at, error) receives lemlist webhooks and Gmail Pub/Sub pushes; workers are idempotent; a scheduled reconciler per connector re-pulls from the source of record.

### Gaps
- Official HubSpot v3 webhook retry count/window, batch size and timeout not confirmed from a primary page (third-party sources disagree: 24h vs 3 days).
- HubSpot associations v4 batch create limits and association type id meanings (e.g. 279) not confirmed.
- Salesforce REST External ID upsert, Bulk API 2.0 limits and Platform Events publish limits not sourced (Salesforce docs blocked our fetcher).
- Pipedrive and Twenty APIs were not researched within the call budget.

## 6. GDPR: storing email content and contact data in the EU (B2B lawful basis, objection, retention, erasure, records of processing)

### Takeaway
B2B prospect data is usually processed under **legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))**, which requires a documented three-part test (legitimate interest, necessity, balancing against reasonable expectations); Recital 47 says direct marketing "may" be a legitimate interest but it is not automatic. Art. 21 gives an absolute right to object to direct marketing, which must be flagged at the first communication, and sending rules are national (ePrivacy): Germany's UWG §7 is reported to require prior consent even for B2B email, while France and the Netherlands are reported as permissive with opt-out. The CRM therefore needs per-contact lawful basis, a suppression list that survives deletion, retention limits on email bodies, and an erasure path.

### Cited Findings
- EDPB Guidelines 1/2024 on Art. 6(1)(f): published 9 Oct 2024 as Version 1.0 for consultation (until 20 Nov 2024); final adoption status not shown on the page. — [EDPB guidelines page](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/documents/public-consultations/2024/guidelines-12024-processing-personal-data-based_en)
- Commentary: Recital 47 recognises direct marketing as a potential legitimate interest but the EDPB says it is not automatically sufficient; three cumulative steps (legitimate interest, necessity meaning "more than what is useful", balancing); reasonable expectations of the average data subject matter. — [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/blogs/sourcingatmorganlewis/2024/10/gdpr-when-can-data-controllers-rely-on-legitimate-interests-for-data-processing-new-guidelines-from-the-edpb) (law firm commentary)
- Art. 21(2): right to object "at any time" to processing for direct marketing, including profiling; 21(3): once objected, data "shall no longer be processed for such purposes"; 21(4): the right must be "explicitly brought to the attention of the data subject" "at the latest at the time of the first communication", "clearly and separately". — [GDPR Art. 21](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-21-gdpr/)
- Art. 30(1) record contents: controller/DPO details, purposes, categories of data subjects and data, recipients, third-country transfers, "where possible, the envisaged time limits for erasure", security measures. Art. 30(5): organisations under 250 staff are exempt **unless** processing is likely to result in a risk, is "not occasional", or includes special categories / criminal data. — [GDPR Art. 30](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-30-gdpr/)
- National sending rules (secondary, vendor blogs): Germany UWG §7 requires prior consent for commercial email even B2B, so legitimate interest may cover holding data but not sending; France (CNIL) allows B2B cold email related to the recipient's professional role with opt-out in every message; Netherlands described as permissive with opt-out; consumer email needs prior consent under ePrivacy Art. 13 everywhere. — [Overloop: B2B cold email Germany](https://overloop.com/blog/b2b-cold-email-germany-gdpr-compliance); [Prospeo: GDPR cold email B2B](https://prospeo.io/s/gdpr-cold-email-b2b); [lemlist GDPR help](https://help.lemlist.com/en/articles/8751523-gdpr-compliance) (none are primary legal texts)
- HubSpot models this as two separate per-contact fields: legal basis to process (needed to store and to track) and legal basis to communicate (subscription or one-time basis), and does not treat the processing basis as consent to tracking. — [HubSpot one-to-one unsubscribe links](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/one-to-one-email/manage-unsubscribe-links-for-one-to-one-emails) (via search summary)
- lemlist `Delete Contact` cascades to leads, opportunities, lists, inbox and activities, so erasure in lemlist is a single call per contact. — [lemlist llms.txt index](https://developer.lemlist.com/llms.txt)

### Inferences
- **Schema:** on `contacts`: `lawful_basis` (legitimate_interest / consent / contract), `lawful_basis_source` and `lia_id` (link to a stored Legitimate Interest Assessment), `country` (drives the ePrivacy rule, e.g. block automated email to DE unless consent), `objected_at`, `do_not_contact`. A separate `suppression_list(hashed_email, hashed_linkedin_url, reason, created_at)` keyed by a hash so an objection survives erasure of the contact row and blocks re-import from lemlist or enrichment.
- **Retention:** define per category in the Art. 30 record and enforce with a nightly job, e.g. full email bodies for non-converted prospects purged or reduced to headers after N months of inactivity (Salesforce's header-only capture and EAC's 6/24 month windows are precedents), while keeping the minimal fact "contacted on date X, objected on date Y" needed to honour Art. 21.
- **Erasure:** a single `erase_contact(id)` routine that deletes or anonymises the contact, its email bodies and participants rows, writes the suppression hash, calls lemlist `DELETE /contacts/{id}` and logs the request in an `privacy_requests` table. Gmail copies in the team's own mailboxes remain outside the CRM and need a documented manual step.
- **Art. 30:** Astra is far under 250 staff, but systematic outreach is arguably "not occasional", so keeping a short record of processing is the safe reading; it doubles as the retention spec.
- **Art. 21(4)** implies the first message to an EU prospect should carry an objection route; for LinkedIn 1:1 messages this is usually satisfied by a short "tell me and I won't contact you again" line plus honouring it in `suppression_list`.

### Gaps
- Final adopted text of EDPB Guidelines 1/2024 not retrieved; only consultation-version status and commentary.
- No primary source (UWG text, CNIL, Dutch AP) was opened for the national B2B email rules; the country split above relies on vendor blogs and should be checked before encoding it as a hard rule.
- No authoritative guidance found on how long B2B prospect email content may be retained; the retention periods above are design proposals, not legal findings.
- Whether LinkedIn messages via lemlist fall under ePrivacy "electronic mail" rules was not researched.
