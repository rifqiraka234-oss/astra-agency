# How RevOps agencies price workflow and automation builds and ongoing support

Research date: 2026-10-07. Every price below keeps its original currency (USD $, GBP £, EUR €). No FX conversion was applied. "$" pages that never name a currency are assumed USD and flagged where it matters.

Evidence labels used throughout:
- **[LIVE]** = the agency's own page, opened with a fetch on 2026-10-07 and quoted from the returned text.
- **[SECONDARY]** = a third party article or directory that reports the agency's price. Not confirmed on the agency's own page.
- **[ESTIMATE]** = a market range written by an agency or vendor (usually one that sells the service). Not a price list.
- **[SNIPPET]** = taken from a search result summary; the page itself was not opened or did not render. Lowest confidence.

## 1. What are RevPartners' published prices and models, and what does each tier include?

### Takeaway
RevPartners publishes a full rate card on revpartners.io/pricing: fixed "starting at" HubSpot implementations from $1,500 to $4,000 (14 to 30 days), a "RevOps As A Service" monthly retainer from $9,850 to $27,000 for 10 to 30 max hours per week on a 6 month initial term plus a $5,000 one time onboarding fee, an "Allbound Marketing" retainer from $12,900 to $24,000 for 60 to 120 hours per month, and a $25,000 one off Clay implementation. The tiers imply roughly $200 to $227 per hour.

### Cited Findings
**Published list prices [LIVE, revpartners.io/pricing, fetched 2026-10-07; page shows only "©2026", no effective date; "$" with no currency named]**
- HubSpot Implementations, headed "Launch HubSpot in 30 days or less": Sales "Starting at $1,500", Marketing "Starting at $3,000", Service Hub "Starting at $1,500", Bundle (Sales and Marketing) "Starting at $4,000". Each timeline "14-30 Days". Billing model for implementations not stated beyond "Starting at". — [RevPartners pricing](https://revpartners.io/pricing)
- RevOps As A Service, monthly recurring, "Initial Term" of "6 mo" on every tier — [RevPartners pricing](https://revpartners.io/pricing):
  - Bronze, 10 max hours/week, $9,850/month, bi-weekly meetings
  - Silver, 15 hours/week, $14,500/month, weekly meetings
  - Gold, 20 hours/week, $19,000/month, weekly meetings
  - Platinum, 25 hours/week, $23,000/month, 2x weekly meetings
  - Diamond, 30 hours/week, $27,000/month, 2x weekly meetings
- Onboarding fee: "One time fee" of "$5,000" listed in the RevOps section; the page does not say which tiers it applies to. — [RevPartners pricing](https://revpartners.io/pricing). GenFlows reads it as "$5,000 onboarding fee on every tier". — [GenFlows, 2 Sep 2026](https://www.genflows.com/blog/revops-agency-pricing-2026)
- Included in every RevOps tier: revenue focused configurations, system integrations, revenue and performance reporting, training and adoption. — [RevPartners pricing](https://revpartners.io/pricing)
- Allbound Marketing, monthly recurring, hours capped per month: Silver $12,900 (60 hrs), Gold $16,800 (80 hrs), Platinum $20,500 (100 hrs), Diamond $24,000 (120 hrs). Each includes SEO and website UX, CRM data enrichment, paid search and media, signal based outbound. No minimum term stated for Allbound. — [RevPartners pricing](https://revpartners.io/pricing)
- Clay Implementations: "One-Time Cost" of "$25,000", "Launch Timeline" of "90 days", weekly meetings, includes a HubSpot plus Clay integration and five playbooks of the client's choosing. — [RevPartners pricing](https://revpartners.io/pricing)

**Secondary confirmation**
- GenFlows, checking live pages on 2 Sep 2026, lists the same RevPartners figures (implementations from $1,500 / $3,000 / $4,000; retainers $9,850 to $27,000/mo; Clay $25,000; 6 month initial term), and calls the implementations 14 to 30 day "Fast Track" builds. Figures match the 2026-10-07 live page. — [GenFlows](https://www.genflows.com/blog/revops-agency-pricing-2026)
- G2's RevPartners pricing listing says RevPartners "has not provided pricing information" on G2, so the website is the only published source. — [G2 RevPartners pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/revpartners/pricing) [SNIPPET]

### Inferences
- Implied effective hourly rate if the client uses every capped hour (hours/week × 52/12): Bronze about $227/hr (43.3 hrs/month), Silver $223, Gold $219, Platinum $212, Diamond $208. Allbound: $215, $210, $205, $200 per hour. So RevPartners prices at roughly $200 to $230/hr with a small volume discount, and the true rate is higher whenever hours go unused (they are "Max" hours).
- Minimum first year commitment on the cheapest RevOps tier: 6 × $9,850 + $5,000 onboarding = $64,100 (assuming the onboarding fee applies to Bronze).
- The implementation prices ($1,500 to $4,000) are entry "starting at" figures and sit near HubSpot's own mandatory onboarding fees (see Q3), which suggests they are designed as a door opener into the retainer, not as a profit centre. This is a reading of the structure, not something the page says.

### Gaps
- The page does not state what an implementation actually includes (number of workflows, pipelines, integrations) or what pushes the price above "starting at".
- No published price for a single workflow build or an ad hoc request outside a retainer.
- Whether unused hours roll over, and the notice period after the 6 month initial term, are not stated.
- No date stamp on the prices; I could not establish when this rate card was introduced.

## 2. What do other RevOps agencies charge (Aptitude 8, HubSpot partners, Clutch listings, UK and EU consultancies)?

### Takeaway
Only a minority of RevOps shops publish prices (GenFlows found readable numbers on 8 of 33 domains it checked in Sep 2026). Where published, US monthly retainers sit between about $2,000 and $27,000, UK HubSpot/RevOps retainers start around £2,000 to £2,750, and EU (NL, IE) retainers start around €4,500 to €5,500. Aptitude 8 publishes nothing; its Clutch profile shows a $5,000+ minimum project and a $150 to $199/hr band.

## 3. Typical price ranges for a one off workflow build (single workflow vs full system), a monthly RevOps retainer, and hourly rates, in the US, UK and EU

### Takeaway
A single HubSpot workflow is a $50 to $1,000 job on Fiverr, a €2,500 to €5,000 "small project" at an Irish agency, and a "medium project" with a $5,700 median in a 2,000+ proposal marketplace dataset. A full CRM and automation system runs about $5,000 to $75,000 in the US, £8,000 to £60,000 in the UK and €10,000 to €40,000 in Ireland. Agency retainers run about $2,000 to $27,000/month (US), £800 to £20,000/month (UK) and €2,000 to €12,500+/month (EU). Hourly rates span $25 (Upwork median) to $400 (senior agency), with $100 to $150 the most accepted band in marketplace data and £513/day the UK contractor median.

### Inferences
- A defensible single workflow price ladder from the evidence: offshore/marketplace freelancer $50 to $1,000; UK/EU small agency or freelancer roughly £/€1,000 to €5,000 (block of 10 to 40 hours at £90 to €150/hr); US agency $2,000 to $6,000 (Profoundly median $5,700 for medium automation work; Media Junction 20 to 40 hour project minimums at $150 to $250/hr). This ladder is my synthesis, not a published standard.
- Published UK and EU nominal rates (£90 to £100/hr Bound, €94 to €150/hr Addmark, €100 to €250/hr About Inbound market band) sit below the US agency band ($185 to $310/hr), so a European shop quoting from a US guide would overprice.
- The cheapest published "real agency" retainer entry points cluster at $2,000 to $2,500/month (INSIDEA, MASH, OpsEthic advisory) and £2,000 to £2,750 (Bound), with SpotDev's £800/month maintenance estimate as the lowest UK figure.

### Gaps
- No source gives a clean per workflow price from an agency rate card; workflow pricing is always bundled into hour blocks, implementations or retainers.
- Profoundly does not state currency or regions, so its medians cannot be split US vs UK vs EU.
- No EU source outside NL and IE was found (no German, French or Nordic RevOps price lists opened).
- No Salesforce specific single workflow (Flow) build price was found with a verifiable source.

## 5. Is there published pricing aimed at very small businesses or solo founders?

### Takeaway
No RevOps agency found publishes a product explicitly for solo founders. The lowest published agency entry points are one off implementations at $1,500 (RevPartners Sales Hub, Pixcell Starter), HubSpot startup onboarding from $2,000 per hub (INSIDEA), UK block hours at £2,000 for 20 hours (Bound), and retainers from $2,000 to $2,500/month (INSIDEA, MASH). Below that, the market is freelancers on Fiverr and Upwork at $50 to $1,000 per workflow. Several sources tell very small companies not to buy a retainer at all.

### Inferences
- Between the freelancer ceiling (about $1,000 per workflow on Fiverr) and the agency floor (about $1,500 one off or $2,000/month) there is a visible gap in published pricing. A fixed price, small scope workflow product in the €500 to €1,500 range would sit in that gap with almost no published agency competition, though it competes directly with Upwork/Fiverr on price.
- The $5,000 minimum project size that many HubSpot RevOps partners self report on Clutch and the HubSpot directory effectively excludes solo founders from the established agency tier.
- Workflow automation in HubSpot needs a Professional seat (with $1,500 to $3,000 HubSpot onboarding unless waived), so for a solo founder the platform cost alone can exceed a small build fee. A small business offer probably needs to be tool agnostic (Zapier, Make, Pipedrive, lighter CRMs) to be affordable. This is inference, not a sourced finding.

### Gaps
- No RevOps agency was found with a published "solo founder" or "micro business" package.
- No UK or EU price list aimed specifically at small businesses was found beyond Bound's £2,000 block and SpotDev's £800/month maintenance estimate.
- Zapier/Make automation agency pricing (non CRM specific workflow shops) was outside this note's RevOps scope and was not researched here.
