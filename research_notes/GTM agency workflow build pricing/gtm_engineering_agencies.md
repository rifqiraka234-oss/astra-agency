# GTM engineering, Clay and "AI outbound" agencies: what they charge to build GTM workflows

How to read these notes. Every page cited as "fetched 2026-10-07" was opened on that date (curl or WebFetch) and the figure was read off the page itself. Evidence is labelled in three grades.
- PUBLISHED. A price on the agency's own site, fetched 2026-10-07.
- DIRECTORY. A tag the agency chose for itself in Clay's partner directory, read from the directory's own API on 2026-10-07 (186 partner records, every one pulled individually).
- REPORTED. A third party estimate (review site, competitor blog, vendor blog) or a practitioner anecdote. Several of these sources sell competing products (Salesforge, ColdIQ, Supered, Bitscale), so their figures are leads, not facts.
No currency conversions were applied. Figures are in the currency the source used.

## 3. What is included in a build, and what minimum contract lengths apply?

### Takeaway
A standard Clay outbound build includes ICP and TAM mapping, a Clay enrichment table (often waterfall email finding plus AI research columns), sending infrastructure (domains, inboxes, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, 14 to 21 day warmup), 3 to 5 step sequences with copy, CRM sync and weekly reporting, with LinkedIn as an upsell. Three months is the modal minimum (98 of 186 Clay partners), one month is common (59), and a few demand six or twelve months.

### Cited Findings
- closed:in, every tier includes "Dedicated sending infrastructure, Clay waterfall enrichment, multi-step sequence, weekly reports, reply handling, Close or HubSpot light sync" (EUR 4K Foundation, cold email only, standard ICP). ## 4. Do any offer a cheaper tier for solo founders or very small companies?

### Takeaway
Almost none of the Clay agencies do; the large names explicitly screen small clients out (Growth Engine X lists TAMs under 40K contacts and lifetime gross profit under $3,000 as "Usually not a fit"; Revenanas targets $15K+ deals and 5+ reps; Frontal wants a real sales team). The cheapest published options are Cleverly's LinkedIn plans at $397/mo, GTM Studios' £2,500 audit, closed:in's EUR 2K Clay only builds, B2B Boosted's £4,000 one off system, plus free tasters; below that sits a freelancer market at $250 to $1k per Clay table.

### Cited Findings
- Growth Engine X "Usually not a fit": "Small Target Markets (Under 40K Contacts)", "Lifetime Gross Profit Under $3,000", "No Sales System to Leverage the Leads We Generate"; application page targets "B2B companies with $1M+ revenue". Free test: "we'll launch a campaign for your business at no cost if we don't have a current case study to show you or if you've never done outbound before." [Growth Engine X](http://coldoutbound.com/)

- closed:in targets "ACV between EUR 5K and EUR 50K, selling to mid-market". [closed:in homepage](https://www.closedin.io/)

### Gaps
- No agency found publishes a named "solo founder" or "micro business" tier for a Clay build.
- Cleverly's own tier contents (prospects per month per plan) are not on its site; only an affiliate site and a review site describe them, inconsistently.
