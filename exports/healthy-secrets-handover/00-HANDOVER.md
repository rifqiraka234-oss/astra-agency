## 6. Things that are easy to get wrong (all verified, sources in the spec)

- **office@ is a shared inbox.** Leonie answered the invite from it. Greeting all three by name works.
- **Their DNS is at Wix but their email is Google Workspace.** Moving DNS without copying the MX and TXT records breaks
  office@. Full record list in `07-live-site-snapshot/README.md` and spec section 9.
- **On Wix, `/shop` is the tea category**, and `/shop-4` is the real shop. The redirect map in spec 7.1 handles it.
  The live canonical host is www.healthysecrets.at.
- **Their AGB (Nov 2025)** says consumer prices are exclusive of VAT (wrong for consumers), names "WIXPayment",
  links the EU ODR platform (closed 20 July 2025), and sells only to over 18s in household quantities. Every product
  page still links the 2021 terms of the old e.U. company. Nikolaus is a medical law lawyer, he fixes his own texts.
- **Health claims.** The prototype deliberately left out claims not on the EU register. The client owns every claim.
- **Austrian VAT** now has a 4.9% rate for some staple foods since 1 July 2026. The accountant sets the rate per product.
- **Klarna through Mollie** must use `captureMode: manual`, capture on shipping, the hold lasts 28 days. Mollie calls
  the webhook again on capture, refunds and chargebacks, so stock and receipts must only happen once.
- **The 30 day "was" price rule** (PrAG section 9a) uses the lowest price in the 30 days before the sale started.
- **Matcha at 44 €** while Chai and Moon Milk cost 25 € for the same size, maybe a typo, ask them.
- **Opening hours** of the café aren't published anywhere beyond "ab 10 Uhr".

## 9. House rules for anything sent to the client (from `08-agency-rules/`)

Read `08-agency-rules/RULES.md` and `CLAUDE.md` for the full set. The ones that matter here.
- Nothing goes to the client without Raka's explicit word, every time. An edit request is not a send.
- Before any message, reread the whole LinkedIn thread and check Gmail for anything new.
- In outward messages, no colons, no dashes of any kind, contractions used, plain warm English, no AI phrasing
  (`writing-standard-anti-ai.md`, `NO-AI-SLOP.md`). Never claim effort ("we spent the whole day").
- Never state anything as fact that wasn't checked at its source in the same session.
- Quotable price band 500 to 50,000 €. Prices are Raka's call.
- Every claim about the client's site or business needs a source, and anything about their absence of something needs
  a positive control.
