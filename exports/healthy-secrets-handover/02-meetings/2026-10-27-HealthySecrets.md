## Call kit (docs/gtm-plan.md section 5)

**The aim.** The three of them leave with one recommendation and a price range said out loud, and a decision date or a start
date in the calendar before the call ends.

**Questions, in order.**
1. "Now the club's open, what's the one thing you want the site to do?" Walk-ins, online orders, Leonie's consultations.
2. "How are people finding the café in its first week?" Instagram, the square, press.
3. "Who runs the current site, and what did it cost?" The anchor before any number.
4. "Who decides, all three of you together?" They're partners in an OG.
5. "Is there a date that matters?" A season, a launch, a press moment.

**The one recommendation.** The site as built, café first, with the shop and Leonie's booking on the same path. Anything
extra (the day planner, the English version) can follow.

**The price, Raka's call.** The opener's research valued the project at 6,000 euros or more. The public band is 500 to
50,000. Say the range on the call, the proposal follows within 24 hours, one page, half at the start.

**Watch.** They're a medical food brand, so health claims on the site must stay in the words they already use (the build
checked the EU claims register, see the handover). Three partners, so the decision may need a second call, book it before
hanging up.
