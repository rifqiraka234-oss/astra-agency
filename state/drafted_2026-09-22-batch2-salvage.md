## Daan Erisman, tuftuf. DRAFTED. The prior NO_STRONG_ANGLE is overruled with evidence.

**Why the earlier verdict said no.** It called the site "a deliberate one screen piece of
art direction" and noted the private events route is "a proper 21 field form posting to
admin-ajax, not a mailto, so the flow angle I expected does NOT exist". Both observations
are correct. The art direction IS deliberate, and I looked at it this time, a navy screen
with a coral script logo and "EVERY FRIDAY AND SATURDAY FROM 21:00 TILL LATE". Pitching them
a "proper website" would be telling someone their deliberate choice is wrong, which is the
Michael Barthel failure.

**But the form was read as a reason to stop, and reading it properly opens the angle.**
The "21 fields" is 19 visible inputs, and four of those are one radio group and three are
one checkbox group, so it is about twelve real questions. It is a well built brief, not
friction. What it asks for is the point.

| Field | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| `guest_range` | 40 to 100, 100 to 150, 150 to 200, 200+ |
| `event_date`, `start_time`, `end_time` | all required |
| `program[]` | Drinks, Walking dinner, Party |
| `budget` | placeholder reads **"For example €15,000"** |
| `privacy_consent` | required tick, "I agree that Tuftuf may use my details to respond to this request" |

**So private events are a serious revenue line, minimum forty guests, and their own example
budget is fifteen thousand euros.**

### The finding, verified nine ways with a positive control

**tuftuf.nl contains zero images. Not one photograph, anywhere on the site.**

Swept for `<img src>`, `data-src`, `data-lazy*`, `srcset`, CSS `url()`, `background-image`,
`<picture>`, `<video>` and `poster`. **Every one returned zero.** The same sweep on
hfmencap.org returns 23 `<img>` tags, so the method finds images when images are there. The
rendered screenshot agrees, it is type and SVG logos on a navy field.

**So somebody is being asked to commit a five figure budget to a room they cannot see.**
That is not an art direction argument. The club front can stay exactly as it is. The person
filling in that form is a different buyer with a different need.

### A second true finding, deliberately not used

**There is no privacy policy anywhere on the site**, while the form carries a *required*
consent tick asking permission to use the visitor's details. All 15 distinct hrefs on the
page were listed, none is a policy, and the four "privacy" strings are three ExactMetrics
scripts plus the checkbox itself. Google Analytics and Tag Manager also set three cookies
before any click with no banner. Positive control, the same href method finds
`/privacy-and-cookie-policy/` on hfmencap.org.

**Not used because it fails the tweak test.** A privacy statement is an afternoon for
whoever maintains the site. It is worth raising later as a favour, especially since the
consent tick currently points at nothing.


### Raka's edit, 2026-09-22. The pain was not felt and the value was too small.

**Why the first draft was weak, and both faults were mine.**

- **The pain was hypothetical.** "Somebody booking a room they've never seen" invites the
  obvious answer, they can look at the Instagram. And nobody loses a night's sleep over a
  mild hesitation.
- **The value was a brochure.** "A page that shows the room" is a design deliverable. It
  names no money and it prices itself low.

**What was already in hand and went unused.** Their homepage says **Friday and Saturday**,
and the form sells private hire to **two hundred guests at a fifteen thousand euro example
budget**. Put those two next to each other and the stake is obvious without modelling
anything, which the money rule forbids.

**The sharper pain is that the loss is invisible.** An event planner shortlisting Amsterdam
venues for a hundred and fifty people does not hesitate and does not enquire. They move on.
Tuftuf never enters the comparison and Daan never hears that it happened. A loss you cannot
see is worse than one you can.

**The credential now carries the argument instead of decorating it.** Eten Maar had
inventory that went off. A venue has nights that do. Same problem, and it is why we can
speak to it.
