## What moves it to 10k (Raka's calls)

1. **Schumacher is the 10k deal on its own.** He listed six things, and one of them is outbound, which is exactly the AI outreach
   flow. Price the whole engine in phases on 12 Nov, not only phase one. For example phase one, the calculator and the request
   flow into HubSpot. Phase two, the AI outreach flow to data centre cooling buyers. Phase three, the email to quote agent. Say
   all three numbers on the call and sign phase one that day.
2. **Don't wait on the grant.** NRW.BANK's MID-Digitalisierung pays 50% up to 15,000 euros, but it's a monthly lottery, the work
   can't start and no contract can be signed before the grant letter ("Sie dürfen vor Erhalt des Zuwendungsbescheids weder mit dem
   Vorhaben begonnen noch Vereinbarungen ... rechtskräftig abgeschlossen haben"), and since 1 Jan 2026 it only covers marketable
   digital products, not internal process work (https://www.nrwbank.de/mid-digitalisierung, read 6 Nov). So phase one is paid now,
   and a grant is only worth trying for a later customer facing piece. Ask NRW.BANK (0211 91741-4800) before promising him anything.
3. **Lisanne, one price for the whole list.** She asked for five builds and said she'd pay more. Five builds at 450 each is 2,250
   and still cheap. A single fixed price with a monthly fee is cleaner.
4. **Owen and Lupo, keep the 500 start but price the extras on the call.** The hints library, checkout and the full BWILD site are
   real money, and Lupo already holds a plan that says 2,500.
5. **A monthly line on every deal.** 50 to 150 a month for hosting, fixes and small changes. Six clients at 100 is 600 a month.
