Hello Favamole!
Favamole is a celebration of flavour, farming & the future. 

You can dip it, spread it, scoop it, swirl it. On toast, in wraps, over bowls or straight from the spoon.

Favamole is the fava bean dip from Dutch soil, we’re creamy, bold and delicious. We work with Dutch organic regenerative farmers to create a short supply chain and without air miles. Ideal for impact-driven menus, net-zero procurement & category stand-out.

Our Product is HPP-packed so we guarantee a 30 days shelf life, clean label, allergen-free and CSRD-proof.

Available in 150g Retail cups, 275g & 525g Foodservice units and as 5kg Bag in box
