About us

At The Garnish Collective, we believe great catering is about far more than exceptional food. It’s about creating experiences that bring people together and leave a lasting impression. Founded by MasterChef finalist Chris Hale, our team combines over 20 years of industry expertise with a genuine passion for hospitality. From intimate private dining experiences and weddings to corporate events, we deliver restaurant-quality food with seamless service and meticulous attention to detail. Over the years, we’ve had the privilege of catering for more than 500 weddings, working with leading organisations including Meta, Evri and the BBC, and serving distinguished guests, including members of the British royal family. Every event, no matter the size, receives the same commitment to quality, creativity and professionalism. Based at Hodroyd Hall in Barnsley and catering events across the UK, we’re proud to offer a personal approach that is both refined and refreshingly down to earth. Whether you’re planning a boardroom lunch, a product launch, a dream wedding or an unforgettable private celebration, our goal is simple: to create memorable moments through exceptional food and genuine hospitality. Let’s create something unforgettable together.
