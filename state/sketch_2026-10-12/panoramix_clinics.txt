IP Clinics

Our IP Clinics take place virtually, providing you with direct access to expert intellectual property advice, for a fixed fee of £375 plus VAT. Receive guidance on patents, copyight, trademarks, and other intellectual property matters for your business.

Whether you’re developing a new product, brand, or creative work, our intellectual property consultation will help you to understand your rights, identify opportunities for protection, and build a solid IP strategy to support long-term growth. We offer straightforward and honest advice, without any sales pressure.

Interested? Book your slot using the form on this page, or get in touch with our team to arrange your session.

What will be covered in our IP consultations

During your IP Clinic session, you can explore a wide range of intellectual property issues tailored to your needs. Whether you’re seeking guidance on patents, trademarks, designs, or copyrights, our specialists will help you understand your options for protection and enforcement. We also discuss IP strategy, brand development, licensing, confidentiality agreements, and managing IP within collaborations or investor relationships ensuring you leave with clear, practical next steps.

Do I need to provide anything prior to the IP Clinic?

No. While some context is useful, we will explore the IP aspects of your business and/or innovation during the session. Our team is highly experienced and skilled at quickly understanding the issues that matter to you.

What happens after the IP Clinic

You will be provided with an AI generated summary of the meeting and a detailed action plan. We will also make any introductions that might be useful to you. If you have follow on questions, we are happy to help.
