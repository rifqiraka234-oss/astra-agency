How Do Our Weddings Work?

Feasting / Banquet Style

Gather your guests around bountiful tables overflowing with seasonal dishes, shared platters, and bold flavours. Our feasting experiences are perfect for a lively, communal atmosphere, inviting everyone to dig in, pass plates, and celebrate together.

We create a relaxed, memorable meal that brings people together

After catering for hundreds of weddings, we are happy to work to your taste and deliver exactly what you want for your special day. Below are a few options of popular wedding catering styles that we can deliver.

Plated Dinner Style

For a classic, elegant touch, choose our plated dinner service. Each guest enjoys beautifully presented courses, carefully served by our professional team. Expect artful garnishes, thoughtful pairings, and seamless pacing throughout the evening. Perfect if you want a refined, sit-down celebration with stunning food as the centrepiece.
