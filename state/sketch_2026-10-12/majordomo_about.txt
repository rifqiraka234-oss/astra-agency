About Major Domo

Welcome to Major Domo, headquartered in Nice and serving homeowners from Monaco to Cannes. We are dedicated to providing bespoke management services for luxury houses and apartments, tailored specifically to meet the ultra high standards of our discerning clients. 

Major Domo excels in meticulous attention to detail and preservation of your estate's value and elegance. Our approach ensures each property receives individualized care, making us the trusted choice for second-home owners, busy real estate agencies, family offices and entrepreneurs. 

David Weyn, Founder

David Weyn is the driving force behind Major Domo's reputation for excellence. As a Frenchman with a career spanning over three decades in high-end hospitality, David's journey through luxury service began in renowned locales such as Paris, Lyon, Switzerland, and Scotland before advancing into the specialised world of super yacht management. His experience managing interiors and service on yachts for royalty and ultra-high-net-worth individuals equipped him with an eye for detail and an uncompromising standard of service. From Papua New Guinea to Palma de Mallorca, he’s spent nearly half his life exploring cultures around the globe.

David’s profound expertise extends to Michelin-star hospitality, where he honed his skills in creating exceptional guest experiences. He is also a recognized wine connoisseur and former wine bar owner, with extensive experience managing wine cellars and participating in the vibrant wine community. This blend of high-caliber experiences informs his leadership at Major Domo, where he is beloved for his dedication to excellence, easy-going nature and lasting relationships with clients.
