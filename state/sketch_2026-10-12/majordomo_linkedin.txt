The "Ghost Villa" Trap

A closed villa in the South of France is never truly off. For many of my clients, their Riviera property was a sanctuary for only a few weeks a year. For the other 40 weeks, it was a non-staffed, ghost villa.

The mistake? Assuming that closed shutters, a locked gate, and an alarm system equal safety. On the Côte d’Azur, the environment can be as aggressive as it is beautiful. Without a dedicated Majordomo, an unstaffed villa becomes a potential liability. I have stepped into properties to find surprises that could have been avoided with a professional presence:

The Humidity Spike: 
After a week of heavy rain in February, the internal humidity in a closed villa can soar. Without controlled ventilation, your bespoke woodwork warps and fine art begins to breathe moisture.

The "Dead Leg" Risk: 
In unstaffed homes, water that sits in pipes for months becomes a breeding ground for Legionella. We implement a rigorous flush-and-treat protocol so your first shower in June is safe.

The Pest Invasion: 
Rodents and insects don't wait for the high season. They look for the quietest house on the hill. Weekly perimeter and attic sweeps are non-negotiable.

The Administrative Lag: 
A missed Taxe d'Habitation notice or an overlooked letter from the mairie regarding local zoning changes can lead to months of legal headaches.

Major Domo specializes in the active absence. We act as your on-site eyes, ears, and hands, managing everything from sudden storm repairs to the arrival protocol (testing every lightbulb, cooling the cellar, and ensuring the pool chemistry is perfect 48 hours before you land).

Your villa should be a source of joy, not a mental burden 1,000 miles away.
Get in touch for a conversation about your villa.
