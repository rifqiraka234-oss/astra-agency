About The Garnish Collective

The Garnish Collective is a luxury events and catering company based in Yorkshire, delivering unforgettable experiences across the UK and beyond. Led by MasterChef finalist Chris Hale, our team has had the privilege of catering for royalty, world-class athletes, and A-list celebrities around the world. A testament to the standard upheld at every event.

Stacey Dockerty, Evri - Marketing & Events Manager
