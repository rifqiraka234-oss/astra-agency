About us

At Panoramix IP, we believe that protecting your innovations should be straightforward and tailored to you and your business needs. Our approachable team blends technical expertise with genuine understanding to deliver a full spectrum of intellectual property services, from full portfolio management and competitor analysis to trademark filing and dispute resolution. We support businesses of all sizes, whether you are an ambitious start-up, a growing SME, or a large multinational corporation, across a range of industries including healthcare, marketing, agriculture, retail, technology, and manufacturing. Every client benefits from personalised advice that is easy to follow, ensuring you always know exactly how your IP is being safeguarded. Uniquely, our dual-qualified attorneys are authorised to file directly in the UK, US, and European jurisdictions. This strategic advantage removes the need for subsidiary law firms in other regions, helping us keep costs competitive while maintaining the highest standards of service. Our expertise is backed by regulation from the Intellectual Property Regulation Board (IPReg) in the United Kingdom and the California State Bar in the United States of America. Whether you are just starting out and need to establish a robust foundation for your intellectual property, or you are already well established and want a reliable partner to help organise and protect your assets, our intellectual property specialists are here to guide you every step of the way. We are committed to building strong relationships founded on trust, clarity, and solutions that truly make sense for your business. Interested in getting started? Send us a message here on LinkedIn or visit our website below for more information.
