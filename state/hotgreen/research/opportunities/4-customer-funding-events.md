# HotGreen growth levers beyond the website: customer funding, financing, events and channels

Prepared 25 September 2026 for the Astra Agency proposal to HotGreen Solutions. Research only. Nobody was contacted, no form was submitted, nothing was posted.

**Access date.** Every URL in this file was accessed on 25 Sep 2026 unless a different date is stated. Where a page shows its own "checked" or "last changed" date, that date is given too.

**Labels.**
- **VERIFIED** means read on the primary source in this session (programme owner, legislation, official guidance, organiser's own site, company's own report).
- **SECONDARY** means trade press, consultant, aggregator, search summary, or a government body that is not the programme owner.
- **COMPANY CLAIM** means a company says it about itself on its own site.
- **INFERENCE** means our reading or arithmetic on verified inputs. It is not a published fact.
- **UNVERIFIED** means we could not confirm it from a primary source.

**Method notes.** Pages were read with curl (proxy CA bundle), headless Chromium where a page needed JavaScript, and WebFetch where both failed. PDFs were read with PyMuPDF. German, Dutch, French and Danish pages were read in the original and the key line is quoted and translated. WebSearch was used for discovery only and was exhausted part way through (session cap of 200 calls, shared with parallel agents), so some independent second checks rely on search summaries gathered earlier in the session.

## 0. Headline findings

1. **The UK has no capital grant for customer heat pumps, confirmed independently.** The gov.uk IETF page (updated 25 Jun 2026) says "IETF closed, July 2025 ... there would be no further extension of the IETF. No successor fund is planned." The older Industrial Heat Recovery Support programme closed in July 2020 and Scotland's SIETF calls are all closed. The UK case rests on tax (full expensing, 100% for companies), Climate Change Agreements to 2033, customer carbon prices and financing. [VERIFIED]
2. **The biggest new lever is EU wide and was published yesterday.** On 24 Sep 2026 the European Commission published final terms for the **IF26 Heat Auction**, €1bn, opening "early December 2026", a fixed premium per tonne of CO2 for five years. Heat pumps and food and beverage are both named. Heat pumps with COP of 1.5 or more get a 25% bid bonus. The 100 to 400°C basket (€600m) needs at least 3 MWth, which is six HotStack modules. Projects must be in the EEA, but "There is no limitation on the origin or type ... of members of the consortium", so a UK vendor can take part. [VERIFIED]
3. **In the first auction (IF25) not one food and beverage winner used a heat pump.** All 14 F&B projects selected (€58.8m) were electric boilers, resistance heating, a heat battery or a hybrid, and several were bid by utilities (Dalkia, Iberdrola Clientes, EDP) on behalf of the site. Only 7 of 65 winners in any sector were heat pumps. IF26's heat pump bonus is designed to change that. [VERIFIED data, INFERENCE on meaning]
4. **The Netherlands is the best matched market on paper.** SDE++ 2026 opens 27 Oct and closes 26 Nov 2026 with €8bn. It has an "industriële warmtepomp" category with a 500 kWth minimum (exactly one HotStack module), COP at least 2.3 and a halogen free refrigerant. The EIA tax deduction (40% of the investment) has a process heat pump code 221103 that accepts COP 1.5 at a lift of 80 K or more. VEKI is open until 28 Jan 2027 with up to €30m a project. [VERIFIED]
5. **Several windows close in the next 10 weeks.** Denmark's Erhvervspuljen closes by 30 Nov 2026 at the latest. Flanders' GREEN scheme "can still be requested until the end of 2026, after which it will be stopped", and it explicitly names ambient air industrial heat pumps for process heat. Germany's EEW Förderwettbewerb round runs to 31 Oct 2026 at the latest (secondary source). [VERIFIED, one SECONDARY]
6. **Rates that could sit inside a calculator are published.** Germany EEW Module 2 pays 40% of eligible cost for a non SME air source process heat pump (60% small, 50% medium), up to €20m. Ireland's EXEED (July 2026 guidelines) publishes a grant table by COP and delivery temperature, 30% to 40% for 90 to 120°C and above, up to €3m a project. Ireland's SSRH pays up to 40%, linked to COP. [VERIFIED]
7. **The US federal picture is thin.** 48C's $10bn is allocated and the July 2025 law stops revoked credits being recycled, DOE cancelled 24 demonstration awards in May 2025 (secondary sources name Kraft Heinz's $170m heat pump project among them), and the one clear federal help is permanent 100% bonus depreciation. California's Food Production Investment Program has a 2026 round expected in September to October 2026 with $5m. [VERIFIED plus SECONDARY]
8. **Financing as a service is the norm among competitors.** Clade (15 year heat as a service), Skyven ($300M EaaS facility), Kyoto (pay per steam) and Rondo with EDP for Heineken (backed by the EIB). The UK National Wealth Fund's minimum ticket is £25m, so it is only relevant to a pooled portfolio, not a single site. [COMPANY CLAIM, VERIFIED for NWF]
9. **The OEM named in HotGreen's own job ad now owns a competitor.** Copeland agreed to buy SPH Sustainable Process Heat in October 2025 and Spirax will sell SPH heat pumps through its steam sales channel. GEA sells its own heat pumps. Channel partners are also competitors. [VERIFIED]
10. **The buyers' deadlines are 2030 and heat is named as the hard part.** Heineken targets net zero Scope 1 and 2 by 2030 and says "Scope 1 remains a challenge as thermal energy solutions must be tailored". Unilever targets a 100% cut in Scope 1 and 2 by 2030 (77% done) and installed industrial heat pumps in 2025. CCEP targets minus 47% by 2030 and uses a €100/t shadow carbon price. [VERIFIED]

## 2. Rules that decide whether support actually lands

1. **IF26 does not stack.** No other public capex or opex support on the same project, and no electricity price relief under CISAF section 4.5 (which is how Germany's Industriestrompreis is approved). Prior public funding for feasibility or FEED studies is allowed. So a customer chooses between IF26 and, for example, BAFA EEW or SDE++. [VERIFIED]
2. **Apply before you commit.** VLAIO: the project may start only from the first day of the month after the application. EIA: report within three months of committing. BAFA's general "apply before project start" rule was not re read this session. [VERIFIED for VLAIO and EIA, UNVERIFIED for BAFA]
3. **Refrigerant.** SDE++ industrial heat pumps and EIA code 221103 need a halogen free refrigerant. SEAI discourages HFCs. [VERIFIED]
4. **Source of heat decides the German module.** Air source goes to Module 2 (up to 40% for a large company and it must run on renewable electricity). Waste heat source goes to Module 4 (25% plus up to 10 points for a large company). [VERIFIED rules, INFERENCE on product mapping]
5. **Size thresholds.** SDE++ needs 500 kWth, one HotStack module. IF26 medium temperature needs 3 MWth, six modules. VLAIO STRES needs €1.5m of investment, GREEN €50k. Flanders' heat call needs above 300 kWth. [VERIFIED thresholds, INFERENCE on module counts]
6. **A useful unit value for a calculator** [INFERENCE, arithmetic on the T&Cs]. The IF26 ceiling of €400/tCO2 times the auction's standard factor of 0.224 tCO2 per MWh of heat equals a maximum of €89.60 per MWh of heat produced, paid for five years. Bids are pay as bid and will clear lower. For the Dutch EIA, 40% deducted at the top 25.8% corporate rate (the rate RVO's own worked example uses) is worth about 10.3% of the eligible investment to a profitable company.

A one page explainer plus a pre filled technical annex (COP, capacity, metering) would help a Dutch, Belgian or Irish prospect, or its utility partner, bid with HotStack. 4. **A dated "funding windows" page or newsletter.** The next ten weeks hold four closings (Förderwettbewerb 31 Oct, SDE++ 26 Nov, Erhvervspuljen 30 Nov, GREEN end of year) and one opening (IF26). 7. **Pages for corporate energy teams.** One short page per buyer type mapping their 2030 targets, internal carbon price and funding routes onto HotStack, starting with the groups that already back HotGreen (CCEP, 100+ partners).
