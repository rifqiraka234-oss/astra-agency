02 / Your proof

Your proof already exists. It lives on other people’s sites.

On other people’s sites

01Coca-Cola Europacific Partners names HotGreen in its 2025 annual report as one of three startups it invested in that year.
02UK Research and Innovation lists your Innovate UK grant for a 50 kW thermal demonstration at a CCEP site, a twelve month project that ends in May 2027.
03Empirical Ventures, Tech.eu and Vestbee all cover the £1.2m raise.

On hotgreensolutions.com

01The homepage says “Some of our key funders and partners are” and then shows logos, with no names in the text.
02The raise, the grant and the demonstrator aren’t mentioned on any page.
03The HotStack specs sit inside one image on the Solutions page.

The savings figure changes with where you read it.

30%Your Solutions page“Reduce your energy bill by 30%”
40%Your LinkedIn page“reduce energy costs by 40% compared to competitors”
Up to 50%Empirical’s announcement“up to 50% energy savings”

They measure different things, and a reader sees three numbers. The yearly saving is €250k for a typical facility on the Solutions page and $250,000 per MW on LinkedIn.

The 100+ Accelerator’s own guidance says a website “should clearly present your technology, traction, and company information to help evaluators assess your solution”. That’s the standard we’d build to.
