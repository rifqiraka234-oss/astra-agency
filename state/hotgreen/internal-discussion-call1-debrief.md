# HotGreen, Raka and Josh debrief after call 1

Held after the 24 September call with Sanya. The exact date and time were not recorded. The full
transcript is at the bottom, word for word as Raka pasted it.

## Who said what, and how sure that is

Two people, no names in the transcript, so speakers are only named where the words themselves prove
it. Everything else is written as "we", because on every point that matters the other one agreed.

- **Raka, certain.** The Tomatoworld comparison, "grow, optimize", the lemlist and CRM
  recommendation, getting Luna in on positioning, the lemlist and Clay event "yesterday" and "I'm
  recording all the meetings", the offer to run their outbound, adding "another Claude project".
- **Josh, very likely.** "that's why I [asked this] question" (Josh asked the investors versus sales
  question on the call), "the infested part" (the same Dutch v as "infestors" on the call), the worry
  that recommending lemlist makes our own messages feel less special, the Framer AI point and being
  "hesitant" to send anything official, raising GDPR and enforcement, the regulation summary bot seen
  on a Claude livestream.

## What we decided

1. **Start cheap, get a foot in the door.** "Let's just start with something cheap ... I can already see
   it like, you know, potential like different bigger things." "I don't want to, like, burn their
   pockets too much." A small first price, possibly with "some revenue [share] ... Just a small part",
   which both of us said we were open to.
2. **Module 1 is investor ready plus the story.** "the biggest urgent thing is ... the website just
   doesn't feel Expensive ... it doesn't tell the story", "the look and feel so it becomes ... premium,
   feels legit", "when you look at the website, it doesn't say ... why would this be [worth an]
   investment", "let's make the goal of ... the 1st module to just be ... investor re[ady]", "the
   storytelling part and ... the [investor] part, I think those should be our priorities", "We're
   different ... the hero". Investor updates too, "a weekly or monthly report, like [what] George[ia]
   is [already] doing, then maybe ... also posted on the website".
3. **Everything else comes as modules on top.** "we can add different modules there. For example, the
   calculator module, the [this] module, that module."
4. **Module 2 is positioning, with Luna.** "I would also get Luna involved ... they're quite open to
   ... positioning."
5. **Do not build what they can do themselves.** "we should try to refrain building things that they
   can do themselves, but they can't do a lot of things."
6. **Be careful sending a finished design.** Josh's point, Framer now has an AI agent, "You can
   literally paste it an image and it does it for you", so "I'm a bit hesitant ... sending ...
   official[] something". Raka's answer, "someone needs to apply it". **This claim about Framer is
   being verified in the research.**
7. **Pitch outbound anyway.** "we should nevertheless pitch it ... we have experience with it."
8. **Next step.** Both drop all their ideas into Claude and turn them into a document, the same as for
   Tomatoworld. Raka sets up a Claude project for HotGreen.
9. **Focus on HotGreen over the other thing.** "do we still want to try out the F part" (not clear what
   the F part is), "I would rather focus on this".

## What we said AI cannot do for them

- **Setting up the inbound flow.** "it's very fucking hard to set it up."
- **Ideation and execution.** "they're probably ... focused with so many things ... we help them to
  advise and to execute."
- **Time.** Even what AI can do takes hours a six person team does not have.
- Raka's read from the lemlist and Clay event, "the technology is being ready, but ... the people
  need to be ready as well. And that's the gap that we need to chase."

## One thing for Astra, not HotGreen

"another ICP is that ... if they've got ... a funding or something, new business, you know, that is a
good [one]". Companies that just raised are a good Astra lead type. HotGreen itself fits it, a June
2026 round and a new commercial hire.
