## She is right. Two errors, both mine, both material

I read the **Statement of Financial Activities** and never opened the **notes behind
the two lines I built the whole message on.** The arithmetic closed perfectly, which is
exactly why it felt verified. The meaning was wrong.

**Error 1. The £255,378 is not money spent getting people to a donate page.**

Note 7, Expenditure on raising funds, year ended 31 March 2026.

| Line | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Governance costs | £7,175 |
| Staging fundraising events | £10,155 |
| **Advertising** | **£14,142** |
| Other fundraising costs | nil |
| Staff costs | £70,581 |
| Support costs | £153,325 |
| **Total** | **£255,378** |

Staff and support are £223,906 of it, **88 percent**. The advertising line is £14,142.
I treated the whole £255,378 as traffic spend pointed at one page. The honest figure for
anything resembling that is about **eighteen times smaller** than the one I used.

Their own note 1.5 already said so. "Costs of raising funds comprise the costs of
staging fundraising events, advertising and marketing and the associated support costs."

**Error 2. £258,203 of that income is legacies.**

Note 3, Income from donations and legacies, year ended 31 March 2026.

| Line | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Donations and gifts | £304,647 |
| **Legacies** | **£258,203** |
| Donated goods and services | £38,659 |
| Other | £216 |
| **Total** | **£601,725** |

Legacies are wills. They do not come through a donate page, and neither do donated goods
and services. I called the whole £601,725 donations and hung a web page argument off it.
Only £304,647 is donations and gifts, and even that includes cash, standing orders,
collections, the raffle and payroll giving.

**And the deficit framing was off too.** £76,600 against £1,185,857 of reserves, in a
year where legacies alone swung £258,203. That is not distress. "We are all good" is her
saying exactly that, and she is right.

## What went wrong in the process

**A summary line in a financial statement has a note behind it, and the note is the
source.** RULES.md check 2 says open the page that would disprove the claim. Note 3 and
note 7 ARE that page and I did not open either. I verified that the numbers added up,
which they do, and mistook that for verifying what they mean.

The rule this needs. **Never build a money claim on a top line without opening the note
behind it.** The arithmetic closing is not the check. It is what makes a wrong reading
feel safe.
