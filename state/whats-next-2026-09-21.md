## The one real finding. We broke a promise to Sholi Loewenthal.

On **2 August** he asked, "Did you look at ESRE?" On **11 August** we answered, "I have
not had the chance to properly look into ESRE yet but it is now on my list."

**That was six weeks ago and nobody ever looked.** It is the only outstanding promise in
the whole inbox and it has been sitting there since.

**So I looked, properly, today.** esremedia.co.uk, rendered and audited.

- **ESRE Media builds bespoke websites, applications and platforms.** The title tag is
  literally "Bespoke Websites, Software & Systems". The line is "Built Once. Evolves
  Forever." **He is in the same business we are**, which nobody on our side had realised.
- **His nav carries a Partners page.** That is an invitation, not an inference.
- He is the founder, with over twenty years across systems, community and research. ESRE
  is the consultancy arm of Sense Future and is named after his two sons, Esse and Ore.

**That kills the pitch and opens a better conversation.** Per the rules, builders and
technical founders want Build Squad, not a redesign. Selling him a website would be
absurd. The honest reply answers the question he actually asked, six weeks late, and
offers the partnership his own site advertises.

```
Sholi, I finally did what I said I would and went through ESRE properly. Six weeks late,
which is on me.

The thing that surprised me is that we're in the same business. Bespoke websites,
applications and platforms people actually own, which is almost word for word what Astra
does. I'd assumed ESRE was a media play from the name.

So I'll drop the pitch, because selling you a website would be ridiculous. The useful
version is the Partners page in your own nav. We do white label build for people in
exactly your seat, you keep the client and the relationship and we take the overflow when
a job outgrows one pair of hands. Works the other way too if you ever need hands here.

Worth twenty minutes on that instead?
```

**Not written into that message, and deliberately.** His own site has no cookie banner
while Google Analytics and Tag Manager load on arrival, no privacy policy link anywhere,
remote Google Fonts, and zero forms on the homepage with only a WhatsApp link. All true
and all verified. **Telling a fellow builder his own site has those problems, in the same
message where you ask him to partner, is the wrong move.** It is worth raising later once
there is a relationship, as a favour rather than a pitch.
