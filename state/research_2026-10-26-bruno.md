## The did you know fact

Design Business Association, Members' Forum November Summary, 5 Nov 2024, on the 2024 DBA Annual Survey Report (members only,
launched 31 Oct 2024), https://www.dba.org.uk/dba-members-forum-november-summary-3/ , "With 92% of DBA members regularly making
use of freelancers – the importance of matching your resource to work in the door is always a fine balance for agencies. If
future income is uncertain, be decisive; freelancers are an expensive but flexible resource." UK design businesses. Sample size
not on the public page (the 2025 edition had 122 members, https://www.dba.org.uk/2025-dba-at-work/). The 2025 summary
(https://www.dba.org.uk/dba-members-forum-november-summary-4/) says "Freelancer use is down 5% this year", without saying points
or percent, so 92% is the latest exact public figure. The 2023 public report (90%, and freelance web developer day rates up 10%
to £391) is older than two years and wasn't used.
