# Can we still get an opportunity on Michael, Sergey and Kate. 2026-09-22

Researched after Raka asked, honestly, whether the three unanswered replies are
salvageable. Short answer, one is dead, one is real but not a website sale, and one
is the strongest case in the whole session.

## Kate Phipps-Wiltshire, Hounds for Heroes. The real one.

Charity number **1134359**, found on their own site rather than guessed.

**A search snippet gave £475,814 income and £735,685 expenditure. That is the 2025
comparative, not the latest year, and quoting it would have been wrong.** The
Charity Commission register returns 403 to our fetcher, so the figures below come
from the charity's own published Annual Report and Accounts for the year ended
31 March 2026, downloaded from houndsforheroes.com. The PDF is a scan with no text
layer, so the Statement of Financial Activities was rendered at 120dpi and read.

Year ended 31 March 2026, their own printed figures.

| Line | 2026 | 2025 |

| Donations and legacies | **£601,725** | £399,577 |
| Charitable activities | £21,688 | £15,262 |
| Other trading activities | £6,298 | £2,211 |

| Fund balances at year end | £1,185,857 | £1,262,457 |

What that establishes, with no estimate anywhere in it.

1. **Donations and legacies are £601,725 of £670,978, so about 90 percent of all
   income.** The donate page is not a page, it is the business.
2. **They spent £255,378 on raising funds** in the year.
3. **Two consecutive years of deficit**, £(259,871) then £(76,600), and reserves down
   from £1,522,328 at April 2024 to £1,185,857 at March 2026, a fall of £336,471.

Against that, the verified state of the donate page from the 21 September research,
re checked before the send. One form, 21 inputs, body text instructing the donor to
scroll below the PayPal icon to pay by card, and the same page served at both /donate
and /node/160 with /node/160 sitting in the live menu. The site is still Drupal, 126
drupal references and 125 node/ paths on the homepage today.

**The so what ladder, and it gets off the ground floor easily.**

| Rung | Hounds for Heroes |
|---|---|
| The defect | The donate page tells the donor to scroll below the PayPal icon for card |
| So what | Donations are 90 percent of income and they take no government funding |
| So what | They paid £255,378 last year to deliver people to that page |
| **So what** | **A quarter of a million pounds of fundraising spend lands on a page that asks the donor to do the work, while the charity runs a second year of deficit and eats £336,471 of reserves** |

**The honest risks, both real.**

- **A charity in deficit has less money, not more.** The case is urgent and the budget
  is tight. This is not a £50k job and pretending otherwise wastes everyone's time.
- **"We are currently working on a new build" may mean an agency is already engaged.**
  The one question worth asking is whether the new build covers the donation flow or
  just the brochure site, because charities routinely rebuild the site and leave the
  donation platform untouched since it is a third party embed.
- **She has had two messages in 24 hours** and we already told her yes or no is
  completely fine. A third has to carry something she does not already have. Her own
  numbers set against her own donate page is that something. Repeating the offer is not.

## Sergey Shalunov, SotoCat. Real, but not a website sale.

**The original angle is gone.** sotocat.com returns 200 with 200,925 bytes today and
wp-login returns 302. The 500 and the missing MySQL extension are fixed.

**And the site is now good**, which is the honest finding. Pricing page with eight
published brackets, £2.50 per unit at 1 to 99 down to £1.65 at 1,000 plus, a worked
example of £297.60 a month for 124 units, a 90 day free trial, an interactive savings
calculator, Book a Demo, Request a Call, Create Account, a portal, apps, FAQ, guides
and a blog. There is no redesign to sell here and saying otherwise would be
manufactured pain.

Two things that are real.

1. **The team page claims "a top-tier team with numerous specialists in machine
   learning, frontend and cross-platform app development, backend development, and
   real estate industry expertise" and names exactly one person, Sergey.** No other
   name, no face. For an agency being asked to hand over its tenant request queue and
   pay per unit every month, that is the diligence surface. This is a credibility
   angle, so per the quantification rules it carries **no number**.
2. **He is running live customer discovery right now.** The team page footer carries
   "SotoCat is currently conducting independent landlord research. We are speaking with
   self-managed landlords, portfolio landlords and landlord organisations about tenancy
   admin, compliance, legal support and day-to-day rental issues" with a five to six
   minute survey, explicitly "not a sales form". The product today is priced per unit
   for agencies. The research is pointed at landlords, which is a different buyer.

That second one is the opportunity and it is a trigger event, not a defect. A repeat
founder mid discovery who finds something in that research has to build a second thing,
and Build Squad is the line that fits a technical founder. The playbook already says
builders, consultants and technical founders usually want Build Squad rather than a
redesign.

We also did him a genuine favour by telling him his site was down, which is what earns
the next message.

## Michael Barthel, No Leadershit. Honestly dead as a client.

He declined on 21 September with reasons, and the reasons are correct.

> my website is not made for conversion, it's more like a business ID. People here
> don't buy such kind of services on a Website. They buy from me as a person... Mine is
> made for sharpen my profile and give recruiters and "strangers" a better idea of who
> I am and what I stand for.

Three things make this a no rather than a not yet.

1. **He is job hunting.** He said on 7 September he is working on both a permanent role
   and freelance clients, and the site itself is aimed at recruiters by his own account.
2. **He already told us not to solve anything.** On 15 September, "No need to solve
   anything for me". We went ahead and offered a build anyway, which is why the decline
   reads as firm.
3. **He fails Astra's own qualification.** The deck's not a fit list is a lead with
   nobody internal to own the project and no appetite for the £5k to £50k band. A solo
   consultant looking for a salaried role is both.

**The one thing that is genuinely still open**, and it is his idea not ours, is the
white label relationship. On 15 September he wrote "I'll keep your agency model in the
back of my head as well. I can absolutely see situations where that could be a useful
combination." He designs operating systems and AI workflows, we build them. That is a
channel, not a client.

Its honest value this quarter is close to zero, because he has no clients to hand us,
which is the reason he is job hunting in the first place. It costs nothing to keep warm
and it should not absorb another minute of research.
