IIThe market

The province of Antwerp registered 7,919 weddings in 2024, the most in ten years.

Flanders registered 27,147, up 6.7% on 2023, and the city of Antwerp 2,496. You don't need a big share of that. A planner who takes ten couples a year, which is Flowerytales' own ceiling, needs one wedding in 792 in the province. Change the place and the number of couples and watch your share.

Another 4,390 marriages of people living in Belgium were registered abroad in 2024, 9% of Belgium's 48,589. Statbel counts weddings in embassies and consulates in Belgium in that line too. Destination weddings are on your form already.

VThe money

Couples planning now expect to spend about €31,750. A planner in Flanders starts at €350 and runs to five figures.

That's the average from House of Weddings' 2025 survey for engaged couples, up from €21,339 in 2022. Wedding Dreamworx tells couples to count on about 15% of the budget for planning. Flowerytales starts at €5,950 excl. VAT for planning and styling. Bring us a dream starts at €350 on mariages.net. You don't publish a price, and this plan doesn't set one. VIIThe new site

The site we sketched treats every couple as one of your first.

It opens on a wedding film and your own line, “For the moments you’ll never want to forget.” Then your launch offer, and your three colours as one couple’s day, something off white for the first idea, something blue for the planning you carry, something red for the day itself. It ends on a reply card that arrives already written, with their names, date, guests and the help they want. The wedding films are stand ins until your first couples are filmed, which is what your launch offer asks for.

  1. 01

    A wedding from the first frame

    A slow wedding film behind your wordmark and your own line, with one button, to be one of your first couples.

  2. 02

    Your launch offer, in your words

    “Will you be one of our first ‘I do’?” and your offer for 2027 and 2028, straight under the film.

  3. 03

    Something off white

    The first idea and the dress, behind a sheer veil that lifts as they scroll.

  4. 04

    Something blue

    Your 17 tabs as 17 lights. They pick the help they want and hand the lights to you.

  5. 05

    Something red

    The day itself, petals in your three colours, and your line, the details are mine, the moment is yours.

  6. 06

    You, in your words

    “Type A, so you don’t have to be.” Your years in events, and Jan Geysen’s words about Cinco Cero.

  7. 07

    The reply card

    Their names in red foil, the date, guests and help already written, sealed with your HH in red wax.

VIIIThe first couples

Six ways the first couples find you, all ending on the same reply card.

Your form already asks how people found you. These are the answers it offers, plus the launch offer itself, which is the one channel nobody else in Antwerp has this year.

  1. Instagram

    56% of the couples in House of Weddings' survey look for ideas there, and it's the first answer on your form.

    What we'd doPost the new site one colour at a time, off white, blue and red, and keep telling nights the way you told Cinco Cero, a sign, a setting, a night.

    The numberVisits from Instagram that reach the reply card

  2. Pinterest

    65.3% of the same couples use it, more than any other source. Your home page already speaks it, "a Pinterest board with 847 pins".

    What we'd doMake a board for every page of the new site and every event, each pin linking back to the date question.

    The numberSaves and clicks to the site

  3. Google

    The second answer on your form.

    What we'd doGive weddings in Antwerp and destination weddings a page each, and put Jan's words where couples check before they write.

    The numberSearches that land on a wedding page

  4. Fairs and House of Weddings

    House of Weddings, used by 65% of the couples it surveyed, lists only planners with its label. In exceptional cases it lets a newcomer in, when several label partners who've worked with her vouch for her. Good online reviews are a condition for everyone.

    What we'd doWalk Marry Me Mechelen this Sunday and Het Bruidsparadijs in January, decide on a stand for Trouw & Trends, and ask the suppliers you work with about the label.

    The numberReplies that answer Event

  5. Referrals

    125 guests saw Cinco Cero and 570+ the Pro League Awards. Bliss Weddings' only quote is from a couple who'd seen Lissa work at a family wedding.

    What we'd doPut a card in your red envelope for every guest list you handle, with the new site on it.

    The numberReplies that answer Referral

  6. The launch offer

    Your offer already trades a discount for the right to capture and share the celebration.
