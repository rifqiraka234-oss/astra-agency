## The idea in one line

Her own promise, "from your first idea to the final dance", run as the couple's own year. The visitor gives their date,
and the page counts back from it in the three colours she named for her brand on 17 June, "Something Off-White.
Something Blue. Something Red.", while one wedding is built down the page and arrives at the end as a letter in her red
envelope, sealed with her HH sticker.

## One wedding, carried down the page

A single state lives in the page, `{date, undecided year, place, guests, help, first couples, celebrating}`. Every
control writes it, every display reads it, the card in the hero envelope, the day count, the drawing of the days, the
guest field, the sticky bar, the letter's fields and its prefilled "Tell me everything", and the fallback mail. Nothing is
typed twice. The letter arrives already filled in.

## Media rules for this build

- Her own photos only, from honeyitshandled.com, read on 26 Oct. Elise's portraits are the six images her own pages
  label "Elise Laeven" in their alt text. Cinco Cero photos are named "Honey it's Handled_Cinco Cero" on her site. Pro
  League Awards photos carry "by Kaat DM" in their alt text and are credited.
- No Pro League, club or sponsor logo in anything shipped. Photos with the stage screen, the sponsor wall, trophies or
  table cards close up are left out or cropped.
- The wedding photos on /weddings (alt "Honey it's Handled_Wedding") are left out. Their origin isn't stated anywhere,
  and six of them are 735 or 736 px wide, the width Pinterest serves. Nothing on the page is captioned or implied as one
  of her weddings or her couples.
- Film is her own Cinco Cero aftermovie (the 1080p mp4 on her Cinco Cero page, now /cincocero), a 4.2 second loop of
  the disco ball and the dance floor with no third party labels, inside her HH, captioned "Cinco Cero, from the
  aftermovie" under the heading "A 50th birthday". Played only on screen, never under reduced motion or data saver.
  No stock film. A stock couple dancing would read as her wedding.
- Every scene writes CSS variables or classes only and has a still state without script and under reduced motion.

## What we don't know and don't claim

- Her prices. She publishes none, so the page has none.
- Where she steps in on a partial plan or for day coordination. Her pages give the idea, not the weeks. The split in the
  drawing is labelled as an example and the letter leaves it to the first conversation.
- What her role was at the Pro League Awards, beyond her page's own words. The page quotes her page and nothing more.
- Whether she can take a given date. The page never says a date is free.
