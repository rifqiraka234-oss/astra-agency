## The four lines, three visitors

**1. Who lands here.**
- **The couple.** Usually the bride first, engaged, in Antwerp or elsewhere in Flanders, marrying in 2027 or 2028, no
  planner yet. Found Elise on Instagram, Google, at an event or through a friend (the order of her own form's question).
- **The host.** Someone planning a milestone birthday, an anniversary, a dinner or a garden party, often after hearing
  about Cinco Cero.
- **The company.** An events, HR or marketing lead at a company or an agency, with an award night, a launch, a company
  dinner or a team event, or with a production that needs an experienced freelance event manager for a while.

**2. What they're thinking when they land, in order.**
- Couple. Is this a wedding planner, and could she do ours? When could she, and what's this launch offer? What would she
  actually do, all of it or part? She's new, has she handled anything big, and our number? Who is she, would we get on?
  How do we start?
- Host. Does she do parties, not only weddings? Can she do our date? What would she take off my hands? Has she done one
  like this? How do I start?
- Company. Has she run anything at our scale? Can she take the whole thing, the coordination, or join our team as a
  freelancer? When? How do we brief her?

**3. Their goal, in her words.**
- Couple, "fully present in every moment" of their own wedding (/weddings), with someone who has "already thought of it"
  (/about).
- Host, "you enjoy the moment" while "we take care of the details" (/events).
- Company, "a seamless and memorable awards experience" is the brief she quotes for her own night (/pro-league-awards).
  In our words, the night runs on time and nobody on their team spends it on the phone.

**4. The action, one per visitor.**
- Couple, the wedding enquiry, as one of her first couples if the date is in 2027 or 2028, with the date, here or abroad,
  the guests and the help already written in. "Tell me everything" (her button words on /contact).
- Host, the celebration enquiry, the occasion, date, guests and help written in. "Plan my celebration".
- Company, the company enquiry or hiring Elise as a freelance event manager. "Hire Elise for your event".
All three are her own form (her five "What are we celebrating?" options and "How did you find Honey, it's Handled?"),
filled in. The header carries Weddings, Events, About and one button, and the bar at the foot carries the current choice
and its action.

**The route rule (Fae Club v3).** The first screen carries the question, the three answers and "Tell me everything". The
bar comes in when the hero's own button leaves, leads with the choice (the date for a couple, the occasion for the host and
the company), and steps aside for every visible button that goes to the letter and for the letter itself. No half screen
without a way to the letter, checked by qa/guide-walk-v2.js on all three paths at 390 and 1440.

**Each path's own proof.** Couple, the launch offer and her years in events, then both nights. Host, Cinco Cero and Jan
Geysen's words first. Company, the Pro League Awards '26 and 570+ first, and the freelance offer as its own choice.

## What we don't know and don't claim

- Her prices. She publishes none, so the page has none, and her launch offer's discount isn't sized.
- Where she steps in on a partial plan or for coordination. The visitor picks the tabs, and the letter leaves the rest
  to the first conversation.
- Her role at the Pro League Awards beyond her page's own words, or who hired her. The page quotes her page and nothing more.
- Whether she's free on a date. The page never says a date is free.
- The tabs are things her pages say she handles, not a list of her services.
