## Why v2 missed

- **No wedding on the page.** The hero was a red HH on blank paper and a list of three options. There's no couple, no
  dress, no flowers, no candlelight, no first dance anywhere.
- **The mood is a nightclub.** Most of the page leans on her two non wedding nights, a football award gala and a
  Mexican themed 50th. On top of that, a mirror ball and "the night came alive", in red and near black.
- **Playful, not exclusive.** Sticker cut outs, chunky grotesk headlines and many busy sections.
- **Mechanical effects.** Doors, tabs and numbers. Clever, but not magical.
- **Why it happened.** She has no wedding photos of her own, so both builds avoided wedding imagery altogether. Fix that
  with licence checked mood film and photography (rules below). Her real nights move off the wedding home.

## What v3 is

**A luxury wedding planner's site that feels like being invited.** Romantic, light, cinematic, calm and expensive. The
wedding is the whole home page. Private and company events get their own short page, reached from the menu.

**The hero is film, full bleed.** Choose a slow, cinematic, licence checked wedding film: golden hour, candlelight, a
long table at dusk, a veil in the wind, a first dance in silhouette. Pick the best one you can find, Raka asked for it
by name. Put her wordmark in ivory and one of her lines over it ("For the moments you'll never want to forget." is on her
home page). One action, to be one of her first couples, plus a quiet "Planning an event?" link. The poster frame shows
under reduced motion and data saver.

**The spine is her own wedding rhyme.** Her brand reveal of 17 June riffs on "something old, something new, something
borrowed, something blue": "Something Off-White. Something Blue. Something Red." Three chapters, one couple's journey
with her.
- **Off white is the idea.** The first call, the dress, the first meeting, "From your first idea to the final dance".
- **Blue is the planning behind the scenes.** "I think ahead, so you don't have to." and her 17 tabs. Elegant here,
  never a dashboard.
- **Red is the day itself.** "The details are mine, the moment is yours." and the final dance.
Each chapter is full bleed film or photography, and a magical transition carries the visitor from one to the next.

**Exclusive comes from her own offer.** Her launch offer is for "a few special couples" to become "the very first Honey
it's Handled weddings", for 2027 and 2028. So the page treats the visitor as invited. The enquiry is an invitation card.
Ivory, letterpress with a deckled edge, her HH as a wax seal. The visitor types their names and date, and they appear in
script on the card as they type. That card arrives as the enquiry, already filled in. Never invent a number of places,
she says "a few".

**Magic, at least three moments nobody has seen.** Directions to beat:
- A sheer veil that the scroll lifts to reveal the next chapter (WebGL cloth or displacement).
- Petals or confetti in her red, blue and ivory that drift and part around the pointer.
- Warm candlelight, bokeh and light leaks that breathe.
- Foil script that catches the light as the pointer moves.
- The wax seal pressing onto the card when they send.
Restraint is what makes it look expensive. Few sections, lots of air, slow easing, sound off.

**Meet Elise, elegant.** Her own studio photos, large and editorial, with her words "I'm Elise, founder, planner and the
person behind Honey it's Handled" and "Type A, so you don't have to be." No sticker look on the wedding page.

**Proof with restraint.** Her 5 years managing events in Antwerp. Jan Geysen's line, word for word with his "!", as a
quiet quote. The Pro League Awards and Cinco Cero live on the Events page, where they belong.

**The flows.**
- Couple. The wedding enquiry, as one of her first couples. This is the home page's one action.
- Host or company. "Events" in the menu opens a short page with Cinco Cero, the Pro League Awards and the freelance
  event manager offer, each with its enquiry (Private Event, Corporate Event).
Run the guide walk on both pages at 390 and 1440.

## Type and colour

Exclusive wedding houses set a refined high contrast serif with a quiet sans, and her own wordmark is an italic serif,
so a serif is hers here (RULES section 5 allows the lead's own style). Avoid the banned "serif headline with an italic
second half" pattern and the other tells. Pull the fonts off at least five luxury wedding planners and couture houses
rendered today, pair with OFL fonts, and add a script for the couple's names on the card. Colour is ivory and powder
blue with her red as the accent. Not red and black.

## Media rules

- Stock film and photography only from sources whose licence allows commercial use without credit (Mixkit Free, Pexels,
  Unsplash). Read each licence on the item's own page, fetch each file singly and note it in the handover.
- Never caption or imply it as her wedding, her couple or her work. The handover says she'd replace it with her own
  footage from her first couples, which is exactly what her launch offer collects.
- No faces that read as identifiable real couples in an advert sense. Silhouettes, hands, details and back views are
  safest.
- No third party logos or brands in any frame. Her /weddings photos stay out (origin unknown).
- Her own photos (Elise, her stationery) as before. The Apple logo painted out of her photo in v2 is an edit of her photo,
  so prefer a photo that needs no edit.

## Everything else holds

RULES section 5 in full (the flow and the wow, the first build is the art build, expensive type, the banned tells). The
copy rules (no dashes, no colons, contractions, NO-AI-SLOP). Her lines copied from her site or her captions word for
word. The budget flag at the top of the handover. The full QA with planted faults.
