## The seed of the idea (yours to beat)

Her promise is "from your first idea to the final dance". Our message promised a couple's year with her, month by month.
The visitor most likely to land is a couple (often the bride) in Antwerp or Flanders marrying in 2027 or 2028, who
hasn't booked a planner, and the one action is the enquiry for a wedding, ideally as one of her first couples.
A strong spine would be the couple's own year. The hero asks their date ("When's your I do?") and the year counts back
from it, month by month, to the final dance, each month in her words and her pictures, while one state (date, local or
destination, guests, the kind of help, the launch offer) builds the enquiry, which arrives filled in. Her stationery
(stickers, cards, envelopes) and the honey in her name are material for the art. Write the four lines and the flow
table first (RULES section 5), then decide.
