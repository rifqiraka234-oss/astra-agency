# Honey It's Handled, the four lines (26 Oct 2026)

Written before any section was designed, RULES section 5. Her site was reread at 11.55 to 12.00 UTC on 26 Oct (all nine
sitemap pages in English and Dutch, raw HTML in the scratchpad honeyproto/raw, full page renders at 1440 in
honeyproto/shots, every part looked at), her Instagram through /embed/ at 12.03 UTC (137 followers, 6 posts, newest
15 Sep 2026). Raka's angle, "a more storytelling artsy website to get more bookings".

## 1. Who lands here

A couple in Antwerp or elsewhere in Flanders, usually the bride first, engaged and marrying in 2027 or 2028, who hasn't
booked a planner yet. She found Elise on Instagram (the first answer on Elise's own "How did you find Honey, it's
Handled?" list), on Google, at an event or through a friend. She's never hired a planner and doesn't know what one does
month to month.

Second visitor, someone planning a milestone birthday, a dinner or a company night, who saw Cinco Cero or the Pro League
Awards on the Events page.

## 2. What she's thinking when she lands, in the order she'd ask it

1. Is this a wedding planner, and could she do ours? We're getting married on our date, here or abroad.
2. What would she actually do for us, all those months between now and then?
3. Do we need everything planned, or only help with part of it, or only the day?
4. She's new. Has she handled anything big? Can she handle our number of guests?
5. Who is she? Would we get along with her?
6. What is this launch offer, and what's the catch?
7. How do we start, and what happens after we write?

What she doesn't ask the page for, because planners don't publish it and neither does Elise, is a price. The page
doesn't invent one.

## 3. Her goal, in Elise's words and hers

To be "fully present in every moment" of her own wedding (Elise, /weddings), with someone who has "already thought of
it" (Elise, /about), and to get through the year without carrying every detail herself.

## 4. The one action

Send Elise the wedding enquiry, as one of her first couples if the date is in 2027 or 2028, with the date, here or
abroad, the number of guests and the kind of help already in it. One primary call everywhere, "Tell me everything",
her own words from /contact.

Secondary, for the second visitor, the same letter with "Private Event" or "Corporate Event" chosen, reached from the
big nights section and the header.
