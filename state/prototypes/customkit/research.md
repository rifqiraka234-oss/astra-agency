## Art direction, decided before any HTML

**Concept sentence.** The tech pack, made public. A brand buyer's real decision is signing
off a spec sheet and a sample, so the site reads like the production documents CustomKit
already makes, garment renders on a gridded spec sheet, colourways as swatches, the
schedule as a production chart, and the nine reviews as signed off delivery notes.

Layers it controls (seven).
1. **UI demonstration.** The hero is a spec sheet holding one of CustomKit's own designer
   renders (Vortex), recoloured live by swatches. No staged AI photo of a blank shirt.
2. **Composition.** Alternates the kit room (their near black) with spec sheet paper, a
   cool white with a fine CAD grid, never cream.
3. **Colour.** Their own tokens, background hsl(220 12% 5%) and primary hsl(40 35% 55%),
   gold used as thread, the stitch line that joins sections and marks what is signed off.
4. **Interaction.** A colourway studio that recolours six real renders in the browser and
   carries the choice into the project brief.
5. **Copy voice.** Production words, brief, sample, sign off, run, ship. Short and plain.
6. **Proof.** Reviews set as delivery notes with the date and what was ordered, linked to
   Trustpilot. The chart is the graph Raka asked for, with CustomKit's own numbers.
7. **Section transitions.** A dashed stitch line in gold runs between sections.

**Adjectives with evidence.** Hands on ("Work directly with our founder", homepage and
brand design page, and two reviews name Chris, Paul and Zoe). Quick (six of nine reviews mention speed).
Premium (their own palette and "No compromises. No shortcuts." on why-us).
**Anti adjectives.** Cheap, factory catalogue, generic agency.

**Type.** Space Grotesk for display and Inter for reading, both already CustomKit's own
brand fonts (their CSS), self hosted as variable woff2 latin subsets. Kept because the
brief is their site at production level, not a rebrand. Not DM Sans (SotoCat), and no
serif, so no collision with the last three builds.

**Collision check against SotoCat, WisTree and Acquitas.** Palette differs (black and gold
against black, white and Apple blue). Hero differs (spec sheet with a live render against a
chat and ticket demo). Section rhythm differs (paper and kit room alternation). No mono
micro labels and no tiny uppercase eyebrow with a rule.
