The production process

One partner runs all four stages.

Go from idea to product to launch with one partner, and every garment carries your brand.

01

Design and development

Brief the team or use the design platform. We produce detailed tech packs and physical samples for your approval before any production commitment.

  • Tech packs and specification sheets
  • Physical samples in 5 to 7 working days
  • Changes made until the sample is right
02

Branding and labelling

Woven labels, printed tags, care labels and packaging made to your specification, so every touchpoint carries your brand.

  • Custom woven neck labels
  • Swing tags and care labels
  • Branded poly bags and shipping materials
03

Manufacturing

Cut and sew production in the fabrics and specification you approved, with quality control at every stage so every unit matches the sample.

  • Made through vetted manufacturing partners
  • Quality control, design and logistics run from the UK
  • Standard production in 4 to 6 weeks, rush available
04

Fulfilment

Stock is held and orders are shipped as they come in, direct to your customers under your brand, with inventory tracking included.

  • Individual, bulk or drop shipped orders
  • Tracked delivery to more than 30 countries
  • Real time inventory and order tracking

White label questions

Here's what brands ask before they brief us.

What's the minimum order?
White label runs start from 25 units and scale into the thousands, and every size of order goes through the same manufacturing process.
Can everything carry my own labels?
Yes. Every garment gets custom woven labels, printed tags, care labels and branded packaging, and swing tags, poly bags and shipping materials are available too.
What can you white label?
Football, rugby and cricket kits, basketball uniforms, training wear, hoodies, quarter zips, joggers, tees, base layers, jackets and accessories, all cut and sew in performance fabrics.
Where are the garments made?
CustomKit is a UK company that manages production through vetted manufacturing partners. Quality control, design and logistics are all handled from the UK.
How long does it take?
Sampling takes 5 to 7 working days, then standard production is 4 to 6 weeks from approved samples, including fabric sourcing, manufacturing, labelling, inspection and shipping. Rush production is available.
