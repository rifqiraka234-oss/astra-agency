Every run takes four steps from brief to box.

A run moves through design, development, launch and fulfilment, with a person managing every stage and nothing made until you've signed off the sample.

Start a project

01The process

A person manages every stage.

01

Design

Start with the design platform or brief our in house team. Upload artwork, pick fabrics and specify fits and finishes, down to collar construction, sleeve length and woven labels.

  • Online design platform with a live preview
  • In house designers for concepts made from scratch
  • 200+ performance fabric options
  • Pantone colour matching
02

Develop

Physical samples are made for your approval before any production commitment. You check fabric, fit and construction, and we keep refining until every specification is met.

  • Physical samples in 5 to 7 working days
  • Unlimited revisions at the sampling stage
  • Detailed tech packs and specification sheets
03

Launch

Sell through a branded store, a limited drop or straight wholesale. We provide the infrastructure and the customer relationship stays yours.

  • Branded ecommerce storefront
  • Limited edition drop management
  • Wholesale and business orders
04

Fulfil

Once orders are confirmed we pack them in your packaging and ship them with full tracking, so there's nothing operational left for you to do.

  • UK fulfilment centre
  • Custom branded packaging
  • Shipping to more than 30 countries
  • Real time order tracking and reporting

03Process questions

You'll always know what happens next.

What happens after I send a brief?
The production team reviews it within 24 hours, then we have a short call to scope quantities and timings, and you get a detailed production proposal with pricing and lead times within 48 hours.
I designed something myself. Can you make it?
Yes. Send the design, a sketch or even a description, and our studio rebuilds it as production ready files. No tech pack is needed to get started.
Do I have to approve a sample?
Yes, and that's the point of it. Nothing goes into production until you've reviewed the physical sample and signed it off.
Who manages my order?
You work directly with our founder and production team, with one clear point of contact from the first brief through to delivery.

Ready to start production?

Send a brief today and you'll have a proposal within 48 hours.
