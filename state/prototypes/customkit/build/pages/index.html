07Before you brief us

Brands ask these five questions first.

What's the smallest run you'll make?
It depends on the garment. Sublimated team kit starts at 10 garments, golf apparel at 15, white label runs and limited drops at 25, and a merchandise collection at 50. Your proposal confirms the exact number, so you can test a design with a small batch before committing to a full run.
How long does it take?
The production team reviews your brief within 24 hours and sends a detailed proposal within 48. Physical samples take 5 to 7 working days, and once you've signed them off the finished run is delivered in 4 to 6 weeks.
Who owns the designs?
You do. Your designs and brand assets stay your property, and every label, tag and package carries your brand, not ours.
Can you ship to my customers?
Yes. We hold stock at our UK fulfilment centre and ship individual orders, bulk orders or direct to your customers in more than 30 countries, with tracking and branded packaging.
Do I need a finished design?
No. Send us a sketch, a reference or a design you made yourself and our studio turns it into production ready files, or book the brand design service and we'll design the range with you.

Tell us what you want made.

Send a brief and the production team reviews it within 24 hours, then sends a proposal with pricing and lead times within 48.
