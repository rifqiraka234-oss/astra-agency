Drop and store questions

Plan the launch around these answers.

How long does a drop take to set up?
Concept to live drop typically takes 6 to 8 weeks, covering design development, sampling, production preparation and manufacturing. Faster timelines are possible for time sensitive campaigns.
Can I run a drop through my own website?
Yes. We can work with your existing ecommerce platform, set up a dedicated branded drop page, or run a hosted drop store for you. Fulfilment and shipping are handled whichever you choose.
What's the smallest drop you'll run?
Drops start from 25 units, made to the same standard as a large run.
Who owns the customer relationship?
You do. The store sits on your domain under your brand, and the customer data is yours.
