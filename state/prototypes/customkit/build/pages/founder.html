The founder

Chris Flood

Chris played football professionally and semi professionally, then spent more than a decade in elite sport leading partnerships and key accounts at adidas and TaylorMade, across clubs, federations and retail. He went on to run My Club Group as its CEO and founded C3 Sports Advisory, and today he leads CustomKit as its co founder and CEO.

Help organisations create products people are proud to wear while removing the complexity behind the scenes.

Why CustomKit exists

He kept seeing the same problem.

Brands, clubs and organisations wanted custom products, merchandise programmes and teamwear, but didn't have the infrastructure, the expertise or the operational support to make them happen.

CustomKit puts design, manufacturing, ecommerce and fulfilment under one roof, so you can launch and scale a product programme without building the operation yourself.
