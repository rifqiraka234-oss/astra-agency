VBuilt

The new site, built around one run.

One run carries down the page, from first light into the night and out to the next dawn, in your colours, your films and your mark. The runner picks sunrise or night, sets the time and the city, sees their dark runs come out as stars, and the reservation arrives with their run already on it. Reserve for €1 is on the first screen, in a bar through the page, and again at the end of the headlights. In the orange chapter the bar offers the Adventure Vest instead.

  1. 01

    Your slashes, your film

    Your three slashes strike down the screen with your golden film inside them, and as you scroll they open until the film fills it. Your own Instagram question, sunrise or night, sets the run, with Reserve for €1 right under it.

  2. 02

    Their year, as stars

    The sun sets behind the ridge from your film and the stars come out. Every star is one of the runner's own runs that starts in the dark in the next year, worked out from the sun over their city, never typed. Tap one and it gives its date.

  3. 03

    Headlights behind you

    A car comes up the road behind the runner. Your slashes and the panel flare at 140 metres, the BFU's distance for reflective material in dipped headlights, and the TCS's 112 metres to stop at 80 km/h on a wet road is marked.

  4. 04

    First light, and the bib prints

    Email and €1, and a race bib prints with their number, run time and city, with 30% off stamped on it. In the prototype nothing is charged or sent.

  5. 05

    The vest for today

    The Adventure Vest in your render's orange. As each spec comes up, the render plays the shot that shows it, then €98, Davide's review and a cart.

  6. 06

    Built out there

    Your bear opens onto your Swiss Peaks film as you scroll, then Lupo's own words, your photos and the Strava club.

VIUnderneath

What runs underneath.

We'd keep what already works for you and put it in one place.

bwildoutdoors.com, built from this prototype, in English and Italian like today.
02The €1 reservation
LaunchKit keeps running it, with your payments, emails and ads, and it moves onto your own domain, which LaunchKit offers. A runner never changes site.
03The Adventure Vest
Sold through Stripe Checkout, with your shipping and the 30 day returns as now.
04The countdown
Reads one launch date you set. On the day, Reserve for €1 becomes Back it on Kickstarter.
05The dark run counter
Works out sunrise and sunset in the visitor's browser. Nothing is stored and there's nothing to update.

A first visit today meets 10% off in a popup and 30% for €1 in the bar. Until launch, the new site leads with the €1.
