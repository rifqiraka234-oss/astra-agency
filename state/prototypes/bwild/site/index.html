The Luminous Vest

Urban to trail, without the safety gear look.

  • Seen from every angle

    360° reflective. It reflects light from every direction, so drivers and cyclists see you clearly in low light.

  • Breathable and light

    Ultralight, breathable fabrics keep the air moving, so you stay cool and comfortable.

  • No bounce

    Adjustable straps keep it locked in. Move freely and forget it’s there.

  • Water on board

    Soft flasks sit close at hand without costing you any freedom of movement.

  • Everything in reach

    Phone, keys and gels in quick grab pockets up front and a zip pocket that keeps them safe.

  • City to trail

    A sleek vest for city streets and wild trails.

Before you reserve.

What’s the €1 for?

It reserves your 30% off. Leave your email for early access, reserve for €1, then pledge on Kickstarter at launch.

When does the Luminous Vest launch?

On Kickstarter. Everyone who leaves an email gets early access.

What size should I choose?

One size. The straps adjust to fit most runners, lean to broad, 84 to 120 cm around the chest. Still unsure? Write to us, we’re real humans behind the screen.

What comes with the Adventure Vest?

The vest, two 500 ml soft flasks and a BWILD pouch for essentials, in premium packaging, all for €98.

Can I return it?

Yes. You have 30 days to return the vest unused, in its original condition.

Do you ship internationally?

Across the world. Europe takes 2 to 4 business days, outside Europe 5 to 10, and shipping is free for the launch.

What makes BWILD different?

Premium performance without the premium price tag, because the outdoors should be for everyone, not just those who can afford €200 gear.

Do you work with run clubs or creators?

Absolutely. If you’re building community or believe in our mission, write to lupo@bwildoutdoors.com.
