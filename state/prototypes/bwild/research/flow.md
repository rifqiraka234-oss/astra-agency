## The four lines

1. **Who lands here.** A runner in Europe, 25 to 45, who runs before or after work, city streets and weekend trails,
   and came from BWILD's Instagram (3,837 followers) or a friend's post. From November to February their usual run
   starts in the dark. Second visitor, a trail runner who wants a hydration vest now, the 98 euro Adventure Vest.
2. **What they're thinking, in order.** What is this? Does it actually light up, show me. Will I look like I'm wearing
   safety gear? Is it hot, does it bounce, what fits in it? When can I get it, what does it cost, what is this one euro
   thing? Who's behind it, is it real? And the vest I could buy today?
3. **Their goal.** Keep running through the dark months and be seen by the driver, without the hi vis look, and get the
   new vest at the best price.
4. **The one action.** Reserve the Luminous Vest for 1 euro to lock 30% off at the Kickstarter launch. Secondary, buy
   the Adventure Vest now for 98 euros.

## The route, the Fae Club v3 standard from the first build

- The first screen carries the price and the action, "Reserve for €1" and "30% off at launch", beside the first input.
- The bar appears the moment the hero's own button leaves, leads with the price ("€1 today · 30% off at launch"), and
  steps aside wherever another reserve button is on screen and at the form.
- The headlight scene ends on the action, the button under its last line.
- Headings tell the truth. No launch date is invented, the countdown says it's an example.
- The proof sits where the doubt is. "Join 1000+ runners" beside the one euro, the review beside the price.
- A phone menu reaches the shop, the story and the FAQ.

## What we don't know and don't claim

- The Luminous Vest's price and the Kickstarter date. Neither is published. The countdown date is an example, labelled
  on the page and in the handover.
- What the one euro deposit's refund terms are. LaunchKit's refund policy couldn't be opened (a challenge page in our
  browser), so the page says only what their presale page says.
- Who is in any photo. Nobody is named from a picture.
- "The world's brightest vest" and "1000+ runners" are their claims, quoted as theirs.
