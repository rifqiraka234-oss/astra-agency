What v1 got wrong against these, said plainly.
1. Their photos and films were small or missing. The hero was a typed clock and two discs, the brand's golden films sat
   in tiles. Every reference leads with the picture.
2. Data UI where a story should be. A slider, a chart with an axis, a ruler with ticks. Correct, and it reads as a tool.
3. One colour, black. Their own material is golden hour flares, pink dunes, alpine green, an orange render and blue night
   streets. v1 used the night only.
4. The mark was a logo in a header. Their bear and the three claw slashes were never the art.
5. Wide upright capitals everywhere. Their logo is a heavy italic, it moves.
