## Buttons and grounds

Square or 2 to 5 px corners on 12 of the 14 running sites that rendered a button, 44 to 58 px tall. Only On and Lusion
use pills. White or bone grounds, colour from photography, black for night chapters. Full bleed photo heroes on Norda,
Pas Normal, Mammut, Hyperlite, Ciele, Bandit.

## What the category already does with the dark

- **Ciele**, homepage today, "Darkness falls and cool runs follow. Our reflective capsule is crafted for those cool
  autumn low light runs, featuring extra reflective detailing, designed for visibility, dusk to dawn." A night photo
  lit by flash, the reflective logos burning white. That's the bar the Luminous Vest is sold against.
- **Raidlight**, "Le trail passe du côté obscur", a dark promo band.
- **Darkroom Engineering**, black ground, giant caps, mono labels, film in four places.
- **Apple Watch Ultra and AirPods Pro**, film pinned to scroll, 15 videos on one page, light and dark chapters.

None of the running sites lets the visitor do anything with the dark. Nobody shows the reflective working, or asks when
you run. That's the gap the signature moments take.
