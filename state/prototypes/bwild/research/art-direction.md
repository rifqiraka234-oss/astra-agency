# BWILD, art direction (26 Oct 2026)

## The one idea, from their own mark and their own promise

Their logo is BWILD with three claw slashes in front of it. The Luminous Vest's reflective back panel is those same
three slashes. Their promise is "glows day & night" and "BE VISIBLE AT NIGHT". So the page is a run through the clock,
and light is the thing that moves. The three slashes are the object that catches it, every time.

## Colour, a script not a palette

- **The sky is computed, never drawn.** The hero's background is the sky at the visitor's run time in their city
  today, from the sun's altitude (NOAA equations). Day is a pale bone blue, the golden hour warms the horizon, civil
  twilight goes ember at the bottom and steel at the top, night is ink with a few stars. That's how the generic AI
  gradient gets reinvented, every stop of it means a minute of the day.
- Ink `#08090B`, bone `#EFEDE7` for day chapters, reflect white `#FFFFFF` only where light hits a slash, ember sampled
  from their moon photo and their product film for the sun and the one euro, with a deeper text shade on bone.
- Photography carries the colour everywhere else, their Alps green, the dune orange, the snow.

## Type

Mona Sans and Fragment Mono, both OFL, licence files in build/fonts. Caps statements in the wide cut (width about 118,
weight about 620, tracking about minus 0.01em), body at width 100 weight 400, the big run time in the condensed cut
(width 75, weight 700). Labels in Fragment Mono caps at 11 to 12 px. Their logo is their PNG, never retyped.

## Grammar

Edge to edge chapters, no cards on a grey page, square buttons 48 to 56 px. Night chapters and day chapters hand over
through the sky, never a border. One idea per screen.

## The signature moments

1. **The sky clock.** "Sunrise or night?", their own poll. A giant run time to drag, the sky and the sun or moon disc
   (their orange moon photo is the reference) move with it.
2. **Headlights.** A pinned scene on a dark road. Scrolling brings two headlights from 300 m to 0. A distance readout in
   mono. At 140 m (BFU) the three slashes flare white with a bloom, at 112 m (TCS) a stopping line crosses the road. Their
   own night film plays inside the slashes once they're lit. On desktop the pointer is a torch that lights them too.
3. **A year of dark runs.** Daylight for the year as a band, the run time as a line to drag, the dark days lit in
   reflect white. The graph is a control, it writes the same run time as the hero.
4. **The Swiss Peaks inside the slashes.** Their 41 second Swiss Peaks film plays masked into three giant slashes, with
   "every hour in the dark" beside it.
5. **The bib.** Reserving prints a race bib, number, run time, city, 30% locked, and its reflective strip flares as it
   lands.

## Media

All theirs, from bwildoutdoors.com and presale.bwildoutdoors.com. Films play only on screen, never under reduced motion
or data saver, with a still frame as poster. Nobody captioned by name. No stock, no third party film.

## Banned list, checked before anyone sees it (RULES section 5)

No serif headline with an italic half, no dash kickers, no pill buttons with arrows, no cream and indigo, no glow
behind a card, no dark overlay on every photo, no count up stats, no rounded cards with soft shadows, no marquee, no
chips, no floating pill, no Montserrat, Poppins, Anton or Bebas.
