# BWILD v2, the art and the route (26 Oct 2026)

## The vibe, from their own material

"DARE TO LIVE". Courage over comfort, dreamers who do. Their film is golden backlight and lens flare, runners on cliffs
above the sea, pink dunes at dawn, alpine meadows, snow in a forest, and at night a reflective panel catching a
headlamp. Warm, wild, brave, free. Their mark is a polar bear walking over the W, and the three claw slashes are its
claws. The site follows the light of one run, first light, gold, dusk, night and the next dawn, and the reflective
glow is the thing that keeps a runner in it after dark.

## The four lines (unchanged from v1, research/flow.md)

Who lands, a runner in Europe who runs before or after work and came from Instagram. What they think, in order, what is
this, does it light up, will I look like safety gear, what does it cost, who's behind it, and the vest I can buy today.
Their goal, keep running through the dark months and be seen, without the hi vis look. The actions below.

## Three goals, one bar

1. **Reserve the Luminous Vest for €1** (primary). Hero, after the stars, after the flare, the reserve chapter, and the
   bar through every night chapter.
2. **Buy the Adventure Vest, €98** (secondary). Hero link, nav, the orange chapter, and the bar while the orange chapter
   is on screen ("Adventure Vest €98 · Add to cart").
3. **Run with BWILD** (third). Their Strava club and Instagram, in the story chapter and the footer.

The bar reads the chapter in view (data-goal) and offers that chapter's action. It steps aside whenever the action itself
is on screen. The header keeps Reserve €1 at all times.

## Colour script, one sky behind the page

A WebGL sky fixed behind the page, four colour stops, a sun or moon glow, drifting haze, stars and grain. Each chapter
sets its sky, the sky eases between them as you scroll. CSS gradients carry the same stops without script.

| Chapter | Sky |
|---|---|
| Hero, sunrise picked | dawn, deep plum top, apricot and gold at the horizon, sun glow low |
| Hero, night picked | night, ink to indigo, moon glow, stars |
| The stars | gold to ember to violet to night as you scroll, the sun sets behind their ridge |
| The flare, the vest | night |
| Reserve | pre dawn to the pale peach of their bear frame |
| Adventure Vest | their render's orange, painted by the chapter itself |
| Story and community | the peach dawn of their dunes frame, contour lines |
| Footer | night |

## Shapes and materials

- Their three claw slashes, as the window onto their films and as torn edges between chapters.
- Their bear, traced from the last frame of their brand film, as the window onto the Swiss Peaks film.
- Topographic contour lines, generated, thin, as the ground of the light chapters.
- A ridge line traced from the opening of their brand film, in three layers, for the sunset.
- Reflective material that flares, with a horizontal lens streak and sparkle.
- Paper for the bib, with two safety pins and a reflective strip.
- Film grain over everything.

## Type

Mona Sans Italic at width 118 and weight 860 for the short shouts, uppercase like their logo, because their mark leans and
runs. Mona Sans upright for reading. Fragment Mono only for the few figures that need it. Fewer labels than v1.

## The scenes, in order

1. **The claw (hero).** Three of their claw slashes strike down the screen and their golden brand film plays inside them.
   DARE TO LIVE. The Luminous Vest line, Reserve for €1 with 30% off and 1000+ runners, the Adventure Vest link, and the
   first input, "When do you run? Sunrise or night", which sets the sky and the run carried down the page. Scroll and
   the slashes open until the film fills the screen.
2. **Out there.** Their film full screen with their own words, "To choose courage over comfort. To chase meaning over
   medals."
3. **The stars.** The sky goes from gold to night behind their ridge while the sun sets, and the stars come out. Each star
   is one of the runner's own runs that starts in the dark in the next year, worked out from the sun for their city and
   time. Hover or tap a star for its date. A headlamp climbs the ridge. Their words, "Every climb, every hour in the dark,
   every shiver shaped what this vest became." The count, and Reserve for €1.
4. **The flare.** Night. Two headlights come up from far away as soft bokeh, and at 140 m (BFU) the vest's slashes and
   panel flare, a streak of light across them and sparkle on the panel. 112 m to stop at 80 km/h on a wet road (TCS).
   Then their six features as a strip of their own night and close up loops.
5. **Reserve.** Pre dawn into peach. Three steps, the countdown (example date, labelled), the form with the run on it,
   and the bib that prints on paper with their number, run and city.
6. **Orange.** The Adventure Vest in their render's orange. As each spec comes up, the render plays the shot that shows
   it, comfort fit, breathable mesh, straps, flasks, pouch, reflective strips. €98, add to cart, Davide's review.
7. **The bear.** Their Swiss Peaks film inside their bear. Scroll and the bear grows until the film fills the screen.
   Lupo's words, the 100 km and 6,000 m, then their photos on the contour ground and the Strava club and Instagram.
8. **Last doubts and the footer.** The FAQ in two columns, the quote they close every page on, the bear and the wordmark.
