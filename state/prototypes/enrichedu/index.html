What trusts are told to ask for

DfE wants every agency charge split into pay, on costs and fee.

“Agencies must provide you with a transparent breakdown of their charges on a per candidate basis.”

DfE, Guidance on supply staff mandate, read 9 October 2026.

The ceiling a trust compares against

Framework supplier fees stop between £32 and £55 a day, by role.

The caps cover the agency’s fee only. DfE says supply staff pay and conditions shouldn’t be affected, and that trusts booking through another route must make sure their costs are no higher than the framework’s. It also notes many agencies have agreed lower charges than these maximums.

How each line is worked out

NI and pension are worked out per pay period, so the days a week change the cost of a day.

Employer National Insurance

15% of the candidate’s earnings above the secondary threshold, £96 a week or £417 a month. For a candidate under 21 the employer pays nothing up to £967 a week.

Earnings in the pay periodThe part NI is charged on

Employer pension

The automatic enrolment minimum is 3% of qualifying earnings, the slice between £120 and £967 a week (£520 and £4,189 a month). A candidate aged from 22 to State Pension age earning £192 a week or more is enrolled automatically.

Earnings in the pay periodQualifying earnings

The same day rate costs more NI per day over a longer week

The threshold is shared across the days worked in the pay period, so a one day week carries less NI per day than a five day week. Bars use the pay and settings in the breakdown above.

Holiday pay at 12.07%

For irregular hours and part year workers, holiday pay can be rolled up at 12.07% of total pay and shown as its own line on each payslip. It’s 5.6 weeks of leave divided by the 46.4 weeks left to work.

46.4 working weeks5.6 weeks of leave

Two allowances aren’t netted off. Employment Allowance, £10,500 for 2026 to 27, comes off the agency’s whole NI bill for the year rather than one booking, and the Apprenticeship Levy line takes 0.5% of the candidate’s pay without the £15,000 allowance. If the agency postpones automatic enrolment, set the pension to not enrolled until postponement ends.
