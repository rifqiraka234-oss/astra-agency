{{CURL:navy}}Station 1

Forward plan. See the year before it happens.

The Long-term planner puts every meeting, milestone and deadline on one timeline, by topic or theme, so nothing arrives at committee by surprise.

The full picture

Appointments, milestones and schedules in one view, short term and long term.

Deadlines you see coming

Built in deadline tracking keeps the cycle on time.

Linked to your goals

Agenda items connect to policy files and sit under your strategic goals.

Shared openly

Make plans visible to colleagues, Members and the public when you choose.

{{I:clock}}28 clear days. A key decision can't be taken until its notice has been public for at least this long.

{{CURL:coral}}Station 2

Report. Written once, used all the way through.

Every item starts as a work file. It collects input, feedback and early drafts as it moves through the steps you've set, until it's ready for the agenda.

Start anywhere

Open an item with a report writer, the executive or the council, wherever your process begins.

A built in editor

Draft in Qualigraf and reuse the content in the agenda, the decision and the minutes.

Work together, safely

Colleagues share documents and updates in real time, with access and permissions you control.

Bring in outside voices

Share part of a file with residents, advisers or partners and invite their comments, if you want to.

{{CURL:teal}}Station 3

Sign off. Every approval on the record.

Set an approval route for each kind of document. Strict where it has to be, lighter where it can be, and matched to how your council works.

Routes per document type

Reports, motions and delegated decisions can each follow their own path.

A worklist for everyone

Each person sees what's waiting on them and by when.

Initial in the workflow

Sign off directly on the file, so it's ready for the next meeting.

Who did what, and when

A built in audit trail shows every change, so nobody duplicates work.

{{CURL:mustard}}Station 4

Agenda pack. Built from what's already there.

The front sheet, the reports and the appendices come together from the files already in Qualigraf.

Unlimited meeting types

Full council, cabinet, scrutiny, regulatory committees and their sub committees.

Everything linked

Motions, amendments, supporting documents and correspondence attach to the right item.

Public or confidential

Publish each document for the public or keep it confidential, item by item.

Alerts when it's out

Staff, Members and residents get a notification when the pack is published.

{{I:clock}}5 clear days. The agenda and reports have to be open to the public at least this long before the meeting. Check a date with our calculator.

{{CURL:coral}}Station 5

Meeting. Paperless, in the room or remote.

Members and officers join in person or remotely on the same app, with the whole pack, their notes and the vote in one place.

An app for every device

iOS, Windows and Android, simple for every level of technical ability.

Notes that stay yours

Text notes, quick scribbles, highlights and bookmarks. Private until a Member shares them.

Voting and speaker queues

Digital votes and speaker queues for physical and hybrid meetings.

Questions in the open

Technical questions asked in the app are visible to the group, so they're answered once.

{{CURL:plum}}Station 6

Decision. Recorded while the meeting's still on.

Minutes, decisions and actions are recorded and linked straight away, so progress stays visible and the detail doesn't get lost.

Linked to the item

Every decision and action sits with the agenda item and the file it came from.

AI Minutes, if you want it

A transcript, a summary per item and a speaker overview from the recording.

Human proofing on request

Our optional review service checks the final version for you.

Votes on the record

Voting results go onto each Member's profile and into the webcast.

{{I:clock}}As soon as reasonably practicable. Every executive decision needs a written record with its reasons and the options rejected.

{{CURL:teal}}Station 7

Publication. The full story behind every decision, in public.

Set your publishing preferences once. Agendas, reports, decisions and webcasts go to the right channels, on time, with speaker names and votes attached.

Automatic publishing

From your council website to your streaming channel, without copying files across.

Webcasts that follow the agenda

Speaker names, agenda items and voting results, live and in the archive.

Member profile pages

Voting record, motions presented and issues spoken on, for anyone to see.

Accessible to everyone

Published material built to WCAG 2.1 AA.

{{CURL:mustard}}Station 8

Archive. Institutional memory that looks after itself.

Files update as each body decides. Policy files gather under strategic goals, and every paper, vote and webcast stays findable for as long as you need it.

Files that build themselves

New decisions and documents join the right file automatically.

Search that finds it

Full text search and smart filters, with optional AI semantic search.

A searchable video archive

Every webcast linked to its items, documents and votes.

Records kept to standard

Records management to ISO 16175, with metadata kept for long term preservation.

Six years of papers. One search.

When a Member, a journalist or an auditor asks what was decided and why, the file already holds the report, the vote, the minutes and the webcast.
