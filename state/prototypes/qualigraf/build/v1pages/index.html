{{CURL:coral}}The committee cycle

One topic, eight stations, nothing lost between them.

A report starts life as a line in the forward plan and ends as part of the public record. In Qualigraf it's one file the whole way, so the paper trail builds itself as the item moves.

Plan the year, and every key decision in it.

The Long-term planner lays out council and committee meetings with their deadlines and milestones, so items land on the right agenda at the right time.

  • Plan agenda items across short and long timelines
  • Deadline tracking built in, so surprises stay rare
  • Share the plan with colleagues, Members and the public

{{I:clock}}28 clear days. The notice of a key decision has to be public at least this long before it's taken.

Reports start wherever your process starts.

Open an item with the report writer, the executive or the council. Draft it in the built in editor, and reuse the content as it moves from report to agenda to minutes.

  • Flexible starting points for every kind of item
  • A built in editor, with content reused as the item moves
  • Colleagues work on the same file, with access you control

Sign offs that don't live in someone's inbox.

Set an approval route for each document type, strict or light, to match how your council works. Everyone sees who did what, so nobody chases a report twice.

  • Approval routes per document type
  • A worklist of what's waiting on you, with deadlines
  • A full audit trail of every change

The meeting pack compiles itself.

Build the agenda front sheet, link each item to its report and appendices, and add motions, amendments and correspondence. Unlimited meeting types, from full council to a licensing sub committee.

  • Agenda items linked to reports and resolutions
  • Exempt items kept confidential, the rest published
  • Publication alerts when the pack goes out

{{I:clock}}5 clear days. Agenda and reports have to be open to the public at least this long before the meeting.

Members meet paperless, in the room or remotely.

Papers, notes and votes in one app for iOS, Windows and Android. Members annotate privately and share notes when they choose. Digital voting and speaker queues work in physical and hybrid meetings.

  • Notes, highlights and bookmarks on every page
  • Digital voting and speaker queues
  • Technical questions asked and answered in the app

Decisions and minutes, recorded while you meet.

Minutes, decisions and actions are recorded and linked to the agenda item they came from. Add AI Minutes for a transcript and a summary per item.

  • Decisions and actions linked to their item
  • Speakers recognised in the transcript
  • An editor for the final check before anything goes out

{{I:clock}}As soon as reasonably practicable. The written record of an executive decision.

Published once, everywhere it needs to be.

Set your publishing rules once and agendas, reports and decisions go to your website on time. Webcasts carry speaker names, agenda items and voting results, live and in the archive.

  • Automatic publication to your council website
  • Built in streaming, or YouTube, Zoom and Microsoft Teams
  • Published material built to WCAG 2.1 AA

The topic file keeps itself, for as long as you need it.

Every report, decision and action on a topic lands in one policy file, grouped under your strategic goals. When someone asks what was decided and why, the answer is one search away.

  • Files that update as each body decides
  • Full text search with smart filters
  • Optional AI semantic search for the most relevant results

{{CURL:teal}}Deadlines built in

The law sets the clock. Qualigraf keeps time.

Every committee cycle runs on the same statutory dates. Miss one and a decision can be challenged, however sound it was.

2014

A 74 page report, public the day before committee.

In Northumberland a noise assessment wasn't open to inspection five clear days before the planning meeting. It reached the public the day before. The High Court found breaches of the public's right to know, and the planning permission was quashed.

R (Joicey) v Northumberland County Council [2014] EWHC 3657

Is Qualigraf already used in the UK?

Qualigraf runs council decision making for more than 400 clients across Europe. Qualigraf UK started in 2025, and two UK councils are already implementing it.

Will it fit our constitution and committee structure?

Meeting types, agendas, approval routes and publication rules are all set up around your procedures, with unlimited meeting types. Our UK team has worked inside Democratic Services, so the walkthrough starts from how your council actually runs.

Can Members use it on their own devices?

Yes. There are apps for iOS, Windows and Android, and everything works in the browser. It's built to be simple for every level of technical ability.

How is our data kept safe?

Qualigraf is ISO 27001 certified and independently audited, holds Cyber Essentials, ISO 9001 and an ISAE 3000 Type 2 attestation, and keeps a built in audit trail. See security and quality.

Can we stream meetings on YouTube or Teams?

Yes. Use our built in streaming, or YouTube, Zoom or Microsoft Teams. Speaker names, agenda items and votes are captured either way.

Do we have to take the whole platform?

No. Paperless meetings, council information and AI Minutes also work on their own, and connect up later if you want the full platform.
