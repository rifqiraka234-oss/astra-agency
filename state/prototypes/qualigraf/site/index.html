03Five clear days

In 2014 a report arrived one day late. The decision didn't survive it.

Agenda and reports have to be open to the public at least five clear days before the meeting. Clear days leave out the day of publication, the day of the meeting, weekends and bank holidays.

Permission quashed

R (Joicey) v Northumberland County Council. A 74 page noise assessment wasn't open to inspection five clear days before the planning committee. It reached the public the day before. The High Court found breaches of the public's right to know, and the planning permission was quashed.

[2014] EWHC 3657. Clear days, R v Swansea City Council ex p Elitestone (1993). Local Government Act 1972, section 100B.

Three meetings. When's the last day to publish?

Weekends and England and Wales bank holidays don't count as clear days.

1A meeting on Thursday 15 October 2026

Clear days are Wed 14, Tue 13, Mon 12, Fri 9 and Thu 8. The weekend in between doesn't count, so the pack goes out on Wednesday 7 October.

2A meeting on Tuesday 5 January 2027

New Year's Day, the Boxing Day substitute on Monday 28 December and Christmas Day all drop out. Clear days are 4 January, 31, 30 and 29 December, then 24 December. Publish on Wednesday 23 December 2026.

3A meeting on Thursday 1 April 2027, vesting day

Good Friday and Easter Monday fall in the gap. Clear days are 31, 30, 25, 24 and 23 March, so the pack has to be public on Monday 22 March 2027.
