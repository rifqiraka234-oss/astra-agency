{{ITEM:03|Where reorganisation stands|Capsticks, September 2026}}

Surrey is going ahead. The rest of the programme is under review.

If you're in Surrey

Vesting day hasn't moved.

The shadow authorities need committee papers, a forward plan and public meeting pages running now, and the new councils need them on 1 April.

If your area is paused

Use the pause.

Your council carries on as it is for now. The review is a good moment to look at the system your committees run on, before any new timetable arrives.
