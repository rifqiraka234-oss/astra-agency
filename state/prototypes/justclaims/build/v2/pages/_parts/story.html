1 One late flight

One late flight starts five clocks.

Follow a single delayed flight through the new rules. Each dial runs at the scale of its own clock, from a four hour ring to a nine month one. Scroll to move the hand.

Clock 1 of 5. Art. 6(4)

The flight lands more than three hours late.

At that point the passenger can ask for compensation, EUR 250, 400 or 600 depending on the distance and the route. The airline had to tell them about the delay as soon as it could, and by the scheduled departure time at the latest.

Clock 2 of 5. Art. 7(4)

96 hours to send the claim notice.

When a passenger may have a right to compensation, the airline has 96 hours from the end of the journey to send the claim notice, which tells them about the right to compensation and gives clear instructions for claiming. It carries its date and time. If a passenger says nothing arrived, the airline has to show that it did.

{{LAW:notice-96h}}

Date and time on the message, and the burden of proof, both Art. 14(7).

{{SDIAL:2}}

Clock 3 of 5. Art. 7(9)

The passenger has nine months to ask.

A request for compensation has to come within nine months of the date of departure. The airline acknowledges each one immediately, on a durable medium. A third party can file for the passenger, and the airline may ask for evidence of a valid mandate.

{{LAW:file-9m}}

An example request, received on day 41.

{{SDIAL:3}}

Clock 4 of 5. Art. 7(9)

30 calendar days to pay or justify.

Within 30 calendar days of receiving the request the airline either pays, by electronic bank transfer unless the passenger agreed to another way, or gives a justification. If it relies on an extraordinary circumstance it names the item in the Annex and explains the causal link and the reasonable measures it took. The burden of proof sits with the airline.

{{LAW:reply-30d}}

Annex list, Art. 6c and Art. 7(9). Payment, Art. 7(7).

{{SDIAL:4}}

Clock 5 of 5. Art. 15a(2)

A complaint opens two more clocks.

If the airline doesn't pay, the passenger can complain. The airline confirms receipt within seven working days and gives a reasoned reply within one month or, in duly justified cases, says that a final reply will follow in under two months.

{{LAW:complaint-1m}}

Outer ring, one month drawn as 30 days. Inner ring, working days.
