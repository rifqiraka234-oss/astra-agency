1 What they saw

They saw the problem from the airline's side.

As lawyers for airlines, the founders handled passenger claims every day. Claims were rising, claim agencies were gaining ground, and the work ran on spreadsheets and clunky CRM systems.

If a flight is delayed, passengers are entitled to compensation. But getting the money is usually a tedious process, which is also tedious and expensive for an airline. Ulrich Steppler and Katja Brecke have seen this day by day as lawyers at a law firm representing more than 50 air carriers.

2 What they built

One place for the whole claim, instead of spreadsheets.

They wrote that the time-consuming handling of endless spreadsheets or clunky CRM systems will have no future. So they built a digital alternative that saves time and lowers costs.

  1. 1

    Automation

    A high degree of it, from the claim form through the checks.

  2. 2

    Communication

    Inside the airline and with the passenger, in one place.

  3. 3

    Payment

    Paying the passenger is part of the process.

  4. 4

    Reporting

    Detailed analytics, so fees and legal spending can be tracked.

The aim, in their words, is faster responses to passengers, better quality, and the peaks evened out. A network of highly specialised aviation law firms supports the process, or defends the airline in court, when and where it's needed.
