1 Eurowings Discover

A claim form on every departure.

Eurowings Discover's feedback form uses justclaims technology and has applied to every departure since 1 August 2023. The legal check of each passenger's claim is built into the software.

By implementing justclaims.eu we have the benefit of improving the customer experience by responding more quickly and efficiently to our passengers' claims.

2 TUI fly

TUI fly was the first.

In June 2019 Skift reported TUI fly working with tech vendors to automate its flight disruption claims. In December the Frankfurter Allgemeine METROPOL named it the first customer of CHS.
