2 How it counts

How it counts, and what it covers.

The regulation doesn't settle the first three, so the clock makes a choice and says which. Check them with counsel before relying on a date.

{{IC:clock}}

When the journey ends

Taken as the scheduled arrival plus the delay. Time of arrival is on block time, Art. 2(x).

{{IC:cal}}

Day 0

The day a request arrives counts as day 0, so the 30th day is the date of receipt plus 30 calendar days, to the end of that day.

{{IC:cal}}

Months

Nine months and twelve months are counted as calendar months.

{{IC:search}}

Scope

A worked example for a delay at arrival, not legal advice. Rescheduled flights and missed connections are measured differently, Art. 2(o) and Art. 6b(3).

{{IC:pay}}

The amount

EUR 250, 400 or 600 by distance, Art. 7(1), for more than three hours late at arrival, Art. 6(4). Art. 7(2) can halve it in some cases.
