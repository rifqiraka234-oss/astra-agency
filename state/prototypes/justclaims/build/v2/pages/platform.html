2 The checks run

Then the claim checks itself.

Automated checks look for double payments, check that EU 261 applies and work out the amount from the length of the route. This is a real claim screen, with sample data.

  1. The deadline is on the claim.Each claim shows the date and time it's due.
  2. The amount is worked out.3,183 km and 200 minutes late gives EUR 400. Art. 7(1) sets 250, 400 or 600 by distance, and Art. 6(4) needs more than three hours late at arrival. Art. 7(1), Art. 6(4)
  3. Each check shows its result.Duplicate booking, duplicate passenger, the passenger manifest and the operating carrier.
  4. The scope check names the rule.It reads "EU 261 applicable" for this flight.
3 Paid or answered

Paid, passed to a person, or answered.

A clear case is paid out automatically. Where the data doesn't match, a claim handler decides. Where there's no right to compensation, the claim is rejected and the check says why.

6 The new rules
