Extraordinary circumstances, and who has to prove them.

The Annex lists 19 circumstances that count as extraordinary. An airline that wants to rely on one still has to show that it caused the disruption and that nothing reasonable would have avoided it.

What the Annex does

The list isn't exhaustive. Art. 6c(1) says extraordinary circumstances include the events on it and aren't limited to them. An event on the list still has to meet every condition written into its own entry, and the 19 entries are set out below with those conditions.
