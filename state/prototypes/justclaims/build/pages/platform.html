What it takes off a claims team

Twelve things justclaims says it does for an airline.

From the platform's own description.

  • Repetitive work moves to the system

    Reject incoming claims for an entire flight automatically, manage automatic payouts, and communicate fully automated with your customers.

  • Shorter lead times

    justclaims puts the cut in the lead time to process a claim at up to 90 percent, and says a team can handle more claims in the same period.

  • Peaks and seasons

    Manage seasonal challenges and peaks in the claims handling process.

  • Consistent quality

    Fewer errors and a consistent service delivery, with a better day for the people handling claims.

  • Payments in real time

    A simple and transparent process, with payments made in real time.

  • Inside your customer journey

    The processes integrate into your digital customer journey, with zero down time.

  • Reporting a CFO can use

    Real time reports, spending, risk exposure and workload distribution for headcount planning, shaped to your needs and your CFO's.

  • Workflow history

    Review the internal claims handling process through its workflow history.

  • A counter to claim agencies

    Counteract claim agencies and set up new ways to compensate, such as offering part of the compensation as a voucher through a retail partner.

  • Many jurisdictions, one place

    Centralize claims handling globally while taking national legislation and other local factors into account.

  • Live in 6 to 12 weeks

    6 to 12 weeks to integrate into enterprise IT and train claim handlers.

  • Compliance

    Get and stay GDPR compliant on a secure, German engineered and hosted platform, with less fraud potential and more accurate claims handling.
