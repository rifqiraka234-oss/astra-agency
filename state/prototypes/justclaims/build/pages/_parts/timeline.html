The reform, in dates

Nearly fourteen years from the Economic and Social Committee's opinion to the day the rules apply.

Every date comes from the Official Journal text of Regulation (EU) 2026/2202, from its preamble, its footnotes and its articles. The 2 October 2026 date is on the Official Journal's own header.

  1. 12 Nov 2013The Economic and Social Committee's opinion is published.Cited in the preamble. OJ C 327.
  2. 5 Feb 2014Parliament's position is dated 5 February 2014.
  3. 29 Sep 2025The Council adopts its position at first reading.Published in the Official Journal on 30 Oct 2025.
  4. 21 Jan 2026Parliament adopts its position.
  5. 15 Jun 2026The Conciliation Committee approves the joint text.
  6. 7 Jul 2026Parliament adopts its legislative resolution.
  7. 13 Jul 2026The Council adopts its decision.
  8. 16 Sep 2026The regulation is signed in Strasbourg.
  9. 2 Oct 2026It is published in the Official Journal.L series, 2026/2202.
  10. 22 Oct 2026It enters into force.The twentieth day after publication, Art. 3 of Regulation 2026/2202.
  11. 23 Oct 2027It applies.The Commission also has to adopt the implementing acts for the rights label and for reimbursement by airports by this date. Art. 3a(8) and Art. 11(7) of Regulation 261/2004 as amended.
  12. 23 Oct 2031The Commission reports.On the Annex list and on how the regulation works, including the feasibility of further automation of requests for and payments of compensation for delays. Art. 17 of Regulation 261/2004 as amended.
