{{SIGN:01|Im Einsatz|On the record}}

Two airlines, in their own words and in the press.

  • Eurowings Discover

    Live on departures since 1 August 2023

    The new feedback form on the airline's site uses justclaims technology and applies to every departure since 1 August 2023. Through an automated customer dialogue the airline promises fast and amicable resolution of disputes over delays and cancellations, and the examination of each passenger's legal claim is built into the software.

    By implementing justclaims.eu we have the benefit of improving the customer experience by responding more quickly and efficiently to our passengers' claims.

    Sidney Hansmeier, Manager Customer Relations and Insights, Discover Airlines.

    More and more airlines understand that a positive error culture and transparent dispute resolution can be a win-win situation for carriers and their customers. Our software has been perfectly integrated into the processes of Eurowings Discover.

    Ulrich Steppler, co managing director of CHS Claims Handling Solutions, 23 August 2023.

  • TUI fly

    First customer, 2019

    Skift reported on 14 June 2019 that TUI Fly was working with tech vendors to automate and standardize the handling of flight disruption claims, and in December 2019 the Frankfurter Allgemeine METROPOL named TUI fly as the first customer of CHS.

    TUI Fly says it's the first airline to work with tech vendors to automate and standardize the process of flight disruption claims.

    Skift, 14 June 2019.

{{SIGN:02|Chronik|The record}}

From the first airline in 2019 to every Eurowings Discover departure in 2023.

Dates are as given in justclaims.eu releases and in the press named above.

  1. 1 Apr 2019A Frankfurt based start up goes live with justclaims.eu, a service for air carriers.
  2. 14 Jun 2019Skift reports TUI Fly as the first airline to work with tech vendors on automating disruption claims.
  3. Dec 2019Frankfurter Allgemeine METROPOL names TUI fly as the first customer.
  4. 2020justclaims.eu publishes a whitepaper with PwC, "Streamlining passenger claims handling for the post COVID 19 era".
  5. 22 Mar 2023justclaims announces that Eurowings Discover will use its automated processes for compensation claims, expected to be fully operational in the summer season.
  6. 1 Aug 2023The justclaims claim form applies to every Eurowings Discover departure.
