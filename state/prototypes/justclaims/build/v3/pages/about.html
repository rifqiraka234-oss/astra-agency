{{SIGN:01|Wer wir sind|Who is justclaims.eu}}

A digital alternative to spreadsheets and clunky CRM systems.

The number of air passenger claims is rising, claim agencies are gaining ground, and IATA forecasts that passenger demand will double by 2038. Handling endless spreadsheets or clunky CRM systems has no future in that.

justclaims.eu is a digital alternative that saves time and lowers costs. It automates much of the work, handles communication inside the airline and with the passenger, includes payment and reports on the results, so the claims process sits in one place.

The platform lets airlines answer passengers faster, raise quality and balance out fluctuations. A network of highly specialized aviation law firms supports the process or defends your interest in court, when and where necessary.
