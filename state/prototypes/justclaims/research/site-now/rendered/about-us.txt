Who is justclaims.eu?

​

justclaims.eu was founded by a team of aviation attorneys in order to offer airline clients an easy and encompassing tool for their claims handling. With the number of air passenger claims skyrocketing, claim agencies gaining the upper hand and IATA forecasting the doubling of passenger demand until 2038, the time-consuming handling of endless spreadsheets or clunky CRM systems will have no future.

​

justclaims.eu provides a digital end-to-end alternative to save time and to lower costs. With a high degree of automation, efficient internal and external communication, payment solutions, and detailed analytics and reporting, the claims handling process can be centralized and significantly simplified.

​

The platform enables airlines to improve response times to passengers, enhance quality, balance out fluctuations, and thus promote customer loyalty. A network of highly specialized aviation law firms supports the process or defends your interest in court – when and where necessary.

​

justclaims.eu’s users are able to track fees, monitor legal spending, reduce overhead, and stay competitive. Take us to the test and request a demo.
