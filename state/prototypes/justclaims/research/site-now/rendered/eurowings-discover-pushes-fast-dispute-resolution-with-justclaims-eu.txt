Eurowings Discover pushes fast dispute resolution with justclaims.eu





The Lufthansa subsidiary Eurowings Discover now offers its customers a fully automated complaints management system. The software from CHS Claims Handling Solutions has now been activated and enables efficient dispute resolution in the event of any irregularities in flight operations.




Frankfurt, 23 August 2023. Eurowings Discover has further optimised its customer service. With an automated customer dialogue, the Lufthansa subsidiary promises fast and amicable dispute resolution in the event of delays or the cancellation of a flight. 




The new feedback form on eurowings-discover.com is based on the technology of justclaims.eu, a software of the legal-tech provider CHS Claims Handling Solutions GmbH and applies to all departures since 1 August this year.




The examination of individual legal claims of passengers is integrated in the software as well as an efficient and customer-friendly dialogue. "The questionable business model of the claims agencies, which usually demand high commissions from customers for their activities, is finally rendered obsolete by the new service," says Katja H. Brecke, Managing Director of CHS Claims Handling Solutions.




"More and more airlines understand that a positive error culture and transparent dispute resolution can be a win-win situation for carriers and their customers. Our software has been perfectly integrated into the processes of Eurowings Discover," adds Ulrich Steppler, Co-Managing Director of CHS Claims Handling Solutions.




Steppler and Brecke are also partners at the Frankfurt-based commercial law firm Arnecke Sibeth Dabelstein, which has successfully established CHS Claims Handling Solutions as a spin-off in the airline market since 2017.




About Eurowings Discover




Eurowings Discover is the new holiday airline of the Lufthansa Group that flies travellers from Frankfurt am Main and Munich to the most beautiful local and long-haul destinations in the world. A tailor-made on-board product with numerous extras and the personal service make every flight a special experience. Travelers benefit from a consistent booking process and seamless transfers at the hubs in Frankfurt and Munich as well as at numerous destinations of the Lufthansa Group and its partner airlines worldwide. Eurowings Discover is a wholly owned subsidiary of Deutsche Lufthansa AG with headquarters in Frankfurt. Currently, the airline operates a fleet of 22 aircraft and employs more than 1,700 people. Flights can be booked on eurowings-discover.com, all booking channels of the Lufthansa Group and in travel agencies.




About justclaims.eu




justclaims.eu is a brand of CHS Claims Handling Solutions GmbH based in Frankfurt. The company is founded and funded by 30 legal experts. justclaims.eu takes a novel approach to the payment of air passenger claims: Founded and thought by renowned air law experts, justclaims.eu enables efficient handling of compensation claims for the mutual benefit of passengers and airlines.
