Quickly compensated



With more than 19,000 lawyers admitted to the Frankfurt bar, Frankfurt am Main is the German center of gravity when it comes to lawyers, international law firms and also legal tech companies.

Please read the short article and portrait on CHS Claims Handling Solutions and justclaims.eu respectively in Frankfurter Allgemeine METROPOL - Business in the Rhine-Main Area, Issue No 6 December 2019 / January 2020 of 11 December 2019.

"If a flight is delayed, passengers are entitled to compensation. But getting the money is usually a tedious process, which is also tedious and expensive for an airline. Ulrich Steppler and Katja Brecke have seen this day by day as lawyers at a law firm representing more than 50 air carriers. That's how the idea for justclaims came about. In 2017, the lawyers founded the CHS Claims Handling Solutions GmbH to build a platform for a simplified claims management for airlines. With the help of two digital agencies, they developed a software that is integrated into the website of the airlines. It contains a complaint form for the customers and in a few minutes, it  will be processed automatically, and the corresponding processes would be initiated. Soon, this should even only take seconds, says Steppler and promises a cost reduction by half. First customer of the start-up is Tuifly."
