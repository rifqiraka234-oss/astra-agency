3. Collection and use of data

​

At the moment you can only use our website for informational purposes. For the in-formational use of our website, it is not necessary for you to provide data. In this case, we only collect and use the data that your internet browser automatically transmits to us, such as for example:

​

date and time of access to one of our websites

our browser type

the browser settings

the operated system used

your last visited page

the amount of data transferred and the access status (file transferred, file not found, etc.) as well as

your IP-address.

​

We collect and use this data during an informational visit exclusively in a non-personalized form. This is done in order to enable the use of the websites you have accessed, for statistic purposes and to improve our internet offer. The IP-address is only stored for the duration of your visit, no person-related analysis takes place.

​

4. Use of cookies

​

We use the technology of cookies for our website. Cookies are small text files that are sent to your browser by our web server during your visit to our website, who stores it on your computer for later retrieval.

​

We only use so-called session cookies (also referred to as temporary cookies), i.e. those that are only stored temporarily for the duration of your use of one of our web-sites. The purpose of those cookies is the continuous identification of your computer during your visit to one of our websites and the switch to another of our websites and to ascertain the end of your visit.

​

The cookies are deleted as soon as you end your browser session.

​

Whether cookies can be set and retrieved can be determined by the settings in your browser. For example, you can completely deactivate the storage of cookies in your browser, restrict it to certain web pages or configure your browser in such a way that it automatically notifies you, as soon as a cookie is to be set and asks you for a response. However, for technical reasons, it is necessary to allow session cookies on behalf of the functionality of our website.

​

We do not collect or store personal data in cookies in this context.

​

5. Data security

​

We also use technical and organizational security measures to protect the incoming or collected personal data, in particular against accidental or intentional manipulation, loss, destruction or against the attack of unauthorized persons. Our security measures are continuously improved in line with technological developments.
