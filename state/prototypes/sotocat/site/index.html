The week as it runs now

Your team is the switchboard. Every repair runs through somebody's phone.

The tenant calls, then sends a photo nobody can make out. Three plumbers get rung for a price. The landlord needs chasing for approval, and the visit moves twice because someone's out. Your managers weren't hired to do any of that, and it eats the week.

How a request moves

Every request moves through six stages. Your team steps in at one.

Captured Understood Routed Priced Tracked Resolved

01Captured

Tenants report it in a chat, with a photo, the moment it happens.

There's no portal to learn and no form to fill in. The tenant opens the SotoCat app, says what's wrong in their own words and adds a picture. It's logged against the right tenancy before anyone at the agency has opened their laptop.

Flat 3 reported a leak
Chat and one photo, 13.02
New

02Understood

SotoCat works out what's wrong, and whether it needs a visit at all.

Computer vision reads the photo and natural language processing reads the message. SotoCat walks the tenant through safe checks first, like resetting a boiler, and only raises a job when the problem needs a professional. When it does, the ticket already says what, where and how urgent.

03Routed

The job goes straight to verified contractors in the SotoCat marketplace.

Contractors near the property get the diagnosis and the photos in one message. They reply with a price and their earliest slot. Nobody at the agency rings round.
