About SotoCat

The best interface is no interface.

SotoCat is a London company building AI that runs repairs and maintenance for letting agents, so a tenant's problem gets fixed without anyone playing switchboard.

How SotoCat got here

SotoCat grew out of eighteen months of PropCat.

  1. June 2024PropCat starts

    A year and a half of building AI for property with the team that went on to back SotoCat.

  2. 3 March 2026SotoCat Limited is incorporated

    Registered in England and Wales, company number 17067432.

  3. 27 April 2026The new chapter

    PropCat closes and SotoCat grows out of it, with the former team behind the step.

  4. 1 May 2026Invited to Stripe Tour London

    Stripe already runs SotoCat's subscription billing.

  5. NowOpen to letting agents

    Ninety days free, then priced per unit. Reapit integration and a landlord version are on the way.

Dates from the Companies House record for SotoCat Limited and from posts on the SotoCat blog.

The team

Engineers and property people build it together.

SotoCat brings together specialists in machine learning, front end and cross platform app development, back end engineering and the property industry. The same people who built PropCat stood behind this next step.

“There were no random people in that team. Not one.”Sergey Shalunov, in I Am SotoCat. The New Chapter.
