03Underneath the blur

The product story's strong. The page doesn't lead with it.

Your seven step workflow, the public per unit prices and the savings calculator are the best things on the site. Here's what gets in their way.

The first thing on the page is a paragraph.

04What a careful agency checks

Agencies will hand SotoCat their tenants' data. Two things make that a harder yes.

Terms of use

The terms name PropCat Limited's company number.

They say the site belongs to the company registered at 86 to 90 Paul Street, number 15785231. That's PropCat Limited, which the register shows with an active proposal to strike off. SotoCat Limited's own number, 17067432, isn't on any page, and UK company rules ask for it on the website.

Cookies

Nobody can answer the cookie banner.

The survey layer covers Reject all, Accept all and Customise at every screen size we tried. The consent record says analytics is off, and Google Analytics and Contentsquare send a page view anyway. The FAQ says SotoCat complies with GDPR.

06The one problem

SotoCat is becoming two products. The site can only show one at a time.

Today it switches between them with a blur. When the survey's up, the agency product is out of reach. The new site gives each one its own front door.

£297.60

That's what each agency that can't get in is worth every month once its trial ends, using your own worked example of 124 units at £2.40.

Fixflo puts Get quote in its first screen, Lanten puts Try for free and Latch puts Start free trial. Latch also gives UK landlords free trackers for gas safety, deposit protection and the Renters' Rights Act, so that's the bar the landlord version has to clear.
