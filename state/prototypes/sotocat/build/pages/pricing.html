How the fee works

You pay one price per unit, set by how many you manage.

Your bracket sets the price for every unit

An agency managing 124 units pays £2.40 on all 124, so the month comes to 124 × £2.40, which is £297.60 before VAT.

It's worked out daily

The fee follows the units you actually manage through the month, so it moves when your portfolio does.

Start with 90 days free and cancel anytime

You add billing details when you sign up and nothing's charged for the first 90 days. There's no minimum term after that.
