Letting agents

What does SotoCat do for a letting agency?

It takes tenant repair requests off your team's plate. A tenant reports the problem in the SotoCat app with a photo. SotoCat works out what's wrong using computer vision and natural language processing, walks the tenant through safe checks and raises a job only when it needs a professional. Verified contractors quote, you approve one, and SotoCat books the visit, keeps an eye on it and asks the tenant to confirm the fix.

Do we need a CRM to use it?

No. SotoCat works on its own, whether you use a CRM or manage without one. Full integration with Reapit is coming, and we'll announce it when it launches.

How do we get set up?

You sign your agency up and add billing details. Then we set up your workspace with you, import your tenancies and send your tenants, landlords and contractors a welcome email with simple instructions for downloading the app and creating an account. The video guides walk your team through the platform a step at a time.

What support do we get?

Our team supports you through onboarding, including training for your staff. After that you get ongoing support and every update as it ships.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Every agency gets 90 days free, and you can cancel at any point. If you'd rather see it first, book a demo with Sergey.

Does SotoCat help with compliance?

SotoCat cross references tenancy agreements and special clauses with current regulation, such as the Landlord and Tenant Act in the UK, and flags gaps with a recommendation for what to do about them.

What happens to repairs that don't need a contractor?

SotoCat asks the questions a good property manager would and walks the tenant through safe checks first, like resetting a boiler. If that fixes it, the job closes in the chat and nobody's sent out.

Tenants

How do I report a repair?

If your letting agent uses SotoCat, ask them to invite you, then download the app and describe the problem in the chat. Adding a photo helps SotoCat understand it straight away.

My landlord manages the property themselves. Can I use SotoCat?

Ask them to visit the SotoCat website. Once they've joined, you'll get access to the app as well.

Can I report something at night or at the weekend?

Yes, SotoCat takes reports around the clock. If you smell gas, open the windows, don't touch any switches and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 straight away. If anyone's in danger, call 999.

Contractors

How do I get jobs through SotoCat?

Join the marketplace and we'll match you with relevant jobs from vetted property managers and landlords. You see the photos and SotoCat's diagnosis, then send a price and your earliest slot.

Who arranges access with the tenant?

SotoCat does. Once a quote's approved it agrees a time with the tenant and sends you both a calendar invite with the details.

Landlords

My property is managed by an agency. What do I get?

Ask your agency to join SotoCat and invite you. You'll see each repair as it moves, approve quotes and keep every job in one place, all from the app.

I manage my own properties. Is there a version for me?

It's being built now. Join the early access list, or take the landlord survey and tell us what costs you the most time and money.

Billing and data

How much does it cost?

You pay per unit, per month, and the price per unit falls as you manage more, from £2.50 for 1 to 99 units down to £1.65 at 1,000 or more. The fee's worked out daily from the units you actually manage. Prices exclude VAT. See every bracket.

Is there a minimum term?

No. After your 90 free days you can cancel whenever you like.

How do you look after our data?

SotoCat encrypts the data it holds and handles personal data under UK GDPR. The privacy policy sets out exactly what we collect and why. This site doesn't set any tracking cookies.
