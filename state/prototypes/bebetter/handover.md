## v2, the art layer (25 Oct, evening)

Raka on v1, "supperr basic ... really just a cheap website ... make it like something they havent ever seen before. but
still simple and tell the story and GUIDES THE AUDIENCE TO THE INTENDED ACTION". The direction is research/award-v2.md,
from about 24 award sites rendered that evening. Everything compounds, 1% better every day, and their teddy is the hero
object. The home page is one year, scrolled, with a DAY counter in the header running 001 to 365.

- **Home.** (1) The teddy traced from their own PNG into five tones plus his blue shorts, printed live on a black stage,
  one colour per squeegee pull with registration marks, then a foil sheen. Two stage lights sway, dust drifts in them,
  their wordmark sits behind him in outline. Click the teddy and it prints again. (2) The year as a dial of 365 bars,
  each 1.01 to the power of its day. Scroll is the days, the dial turns so today sits under the notch, the teddy grows
  with the curve, and at day 365 the view flies into the star on his shorts and floods copper. (3) The drop on brushed
  copper, a black hang tag on a string you can grab and swing, its foil date glinting as it moves, days to go counted on
  Amsterdam's calendar. (4) Their stand photos cut into tees on hangers along a chrome rail that swing when dragged or
  scrolled, each with a paper tag saying where and when. (5) A halftone map of Europe, the van driving Rotterdam, Köln,
  Breda, Praha and on to Gorinchem as you scroll, a polaroid dropping onto a pile at each stop, the month large in the
  corner. (6) The tee on a hanger that swings with every letter of the gym's name and prints it when they stop typing.
  (7) Their wordmark edge to edge across the footer.
- **For gyms.** Stage lights over the hero tee, which leans toward the pointer. Every pull drops an aluminium screen
  frame with mesh over the back, the squeegee crosses, the frame lifts and a wet sheen passes. The print now pulls on
  its own 650 ms after they stop typing. The sold out rail has morning light crossing the wall, warming toward the
  afternoon, tees lifting off blurred, and a red rubber stamp SOLD OUT slamming down at four hours. The Hungarian order
  is 200 copper tees filling the wall. The kits stand on a lit floor with reflections and turn as they pass the middle.
  The studio garment leans toward the pointer and turns in 3D when it flips.
- Every scene has a still state with no script and under reduced motion, nothing dims text, nothing lazy loads.
- The licence checked hand wrap film (item 12 below) is no longer on any page and no longer ships. Only media a page
  references is deployed now.

## What it is (v1, still the structure under v2)

The Saavedra v3 recipe (RULES section 5). The four lines and the flow table came first (research/four-lines.md,
research/flow.md), then the art direction (research/art-direction.md), and the 53 site teardown ran alongside
(research/teardown/TEARDOWN.md).

- **For gyms, one kit carried down the page.** The hero is the first input, the gym's name typed onto the back of a
  washed oversized tee drawn from the grammar of their own kits (Team Siem, American Gym Antwerp, Human Sports), pulled
  across the shoulders by a squeegee sweep. Then their first gym story as a rail that empties while a clock runs four
  hours, in their words. Then their kits on a rail you drag. Then the studio (pieces, garment colour, lettering, print
  colour, crest, our teddy or their own logo, motto, city, year, front and back). Then the size run as bars you drag,
  with a rail filling with hangers. Then the work order that fills itself, the FAQ, and the form with everything in it
  and the logo attached. Sending stamps the sheet RECEIVED and drops it into a black BeBetter bag. A bar at the bottom
  carries the kit after the first screen and leaves before the form.
- **Home.** Their teddy in the empty EVLS hall, the drop of 27.10 counting days (it turns into a shop button on the
  day, it never shows an hour they didn't give), the line, the teddy wall, the road as the back of a tour tee
  (Köln, Breda, Praha, next), a door for gyms that prints the name and hands it to /gyms/, and their creed as a curve
  you drag, 1.01 to the power of the days.
- **The plan.** Six steps with a tracker, sources under each. The proof, the gap (CBS, 2,310 gyms, 8% with a clothing
  shop, a dot wall you fill), the others (print shops, makers, YOKKAO), the page, the fair (Sport Ondernemers Expo
  4 Nov, a slider for their own weeks to delivery against the January rush), the 90 days and a pieces calculator.
- English first, Dutch switch on the site. The plan is English, Astra's voice.
