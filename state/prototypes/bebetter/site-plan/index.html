2

More than nine in ten Dutch gyms sell no clothing at all.

CBS counts 2,310 gyms in the Netherlands and only 8% of them have a clothing shop. A kit that's designed, made and delivered for them is the easy version of a shop. And you don't need many of them.

about 185 gyms with a clothing shop2,125 without onethe gyms you add

10 gyms is 0.4% of the gyms in the Netherlands.

Sources and the catch
  • 2,310 gyms, 3.24 million members and 8% with a clothing shop, CBS StatLine table 83822NED, 2023, provisional. The share was 7% in 2015, 2019 and 2021.
  • The catch. 8% can also mean most gyms don't want to stock clothing. That's why the kit has to be done for them, design, sizes and delivery.
  • Where to start. 242 CrossFit boxes in the Netherlands, 8 of them in Rotterdam, and 99 in Belgium, from the CrossFit map. More than 8,400 registered personal trainers, KVK figures via ANP. About 1,200 gym locations in NL Actief, the trade association. All read 25 Oct 2026.

3

Everyone else either prints or waits.

The cheap ones print whatever the gym designs. The ones that design a line, Eigen Label here and YOKKAO for fight gyms, work through a quote, and YOKKAO takes six to eight weeks. Nobody we found lets a gym build its own kit on the page and send it over as a brief.

  • Print shops, Bedrukt.nl, from one piece, no setup cost, production in 3 to 5 working days. Tshirtdeal, sport shirt from €6.00, from one piece.

  • The limits. Thirteen pages opened plus searches on 25 Oct 2026. It isn't a census, a Dutch or Belgian brand doing this may exist and not advertise it.

4

So the page does the selling.

“Send us a message” becomes a request you can answer with a design and a price. A gym owner types their gym's name and sees it on the back of your tee, builds the rest, sets the sizes and sends you the whole brief with their logo attached.

5

Your next stand is full of gym owners.

On 4 November the first Sport Ondernemers Expo opens in Gorinchem, a trade fair for gym owners, and its exhibitor list has BeBetter as the only apparel brand of 97. January is when gyms sign the most new members, so a gym that says yes at your stand will want its kit before New Year. Set your own weeks below.

Put the page live before the fair, a QR code on the stand that opens it, and a tablet where owners type their gym's name and see it on the back.

The next 90 days.

The page live. Your from price, minimum and usual time in the answers, the three things the page can't say yet. The QR code and the tablet on the stand.

November
