# BeBetter growth plan, verified facts

Researched 25 Oct 2026, 16:12 to 18:10 UTC. Everything below was opened in this session. Nothing comes from a search snippet. Search was only used to find pages, and every figure here was then read on the page itself.

**How each fact was checked**
- **C1, source.** The page was opened and the exact words copied.
- **C2, opposite.** I tried to prove the figure false with a newer number, a rival source, a recount or the page most likely to contradict it.
- **C3, independent.** I confirmed it a second way that doesn't share a failure mode with C1, such as a different organisation, a different tool or arithmetic.

**Verdicts**
- **VERIFIED** means all three checks passed.
- **PARTLY** means one check failed or couldn't be run, and the row names which one.
- **NOT VERIFIED** means the claim couldn't be confirmed.

**Walls met, and nothing was bypassed**
- **Trustpilot.** Plain curl got an AWS WAF "Verifying your connection" page with HTTP 403, and the Tesco control page got 403 too. The content was then read through the standard WebFetch tool. I didn't use impersonation or any evasion. I'm flagging this so you can decide whether it's acceptable.
- **decathlon.be.** It sits behind Cloudflare "Just a moment", so it's WALLED.
- **hyrox.com.** It returned HTTP 429, so it's WALLED. I didn't retry.
- **archive.org availability API.** It returned HTTP 429. I didn't retry.
- **CrossFit.** The map page is a JS shell. I counted affiliates from the public JSON endpoint the map page itself calls (`c3po.crossfit.com/api/affiliates/v2/affiliates`). That endpoint needs no login, no key and no challenge.
- **tools/fetch-walled.py.** I didn't use it, because it impersonates browser TLS fingerprints.

## The 8 strongest facts for the plan

1. **BeBetter is already pitching gyms face to face.** It's listed as the **only Apparel exhibitor of 97** at the Sport Ondernemers Expo, **4 Nov 2026**, Gorinchem. That's the first national B2B fair for Dutch gym owners, chains and boutique studios, run with NL Actief (B9a).
2. **The B2B proof is already on their own page.**
   - The first gym drop sold out in **four hours** (26 Mar 2026).
   - A Hungarian gym ordered **200 pieces**.
   - As of 29 Jun 2026 they'd worked with **10 gyms and 3 personal coaches across Europe** (D1, D2).
3. **The buyer pool is countable.** CBS gives **2,310 fitness centre establishments and 3.24M clients** in NL (2023), and NL Actief has about **1,200 member gyms**. Treat KVK's 13,998 as a growth signal, not a gym count (A1).
4. **Almost no Dutch gym sells clothing.** Only **8% of fitness centres have a sportswear shop** (CBS 2023, stable at 7 to 8% since 2015). Even the smallest independents are only at 11%. That's the gap a ready made gym collection fills (A1b).
5. **Fitness reach in NL is high.** **19% of Dutch people are gym members**, about 2x Europe's 9.3% (EHFMR via Basic-Fit, cross checked by CBS 18.2%) (A2, A1d).
6. **Community boxes are a natural first segment.** There are **242 CrossFit affiliates in NL and 99 in Belgium**, 8 of them in Rotterdam. These are community gyms where members wear the box name (A3). There are also **8,400+ registered personal trainers**, four times the 2015 figure (A4).
7. **January drives gym sign ups.** Basic-Fit's Q1 is its biggest net growth quarter, and operators report **30 to 40% more** new members in January. A gym's kit for the January intake has to be sold and produced in Oct to Dec (A5).
8. **The competition is either cheap or slow.**
   - **Cheap.** Dutch print shops print on blanks from 1 piece, €6 to €14, 3 to 5 days.
   - **Silent on terms.** Agencies ask for a quote by email, with no price or lead time shown.
   - **Slow and foreign.** The one premium brand designing gym collections (YOKKAO) is a Thai combat brand at 6 to 8 weeks.
   - **The gap.** A young Dutch gymwear brand that designs the collection was not found (C).

## What contradicts our assumptions

- **"Founded in 2024"** is LinkedIn's wording. KVK shows a VOF registered on **20 Jan 2025**, and the partners are Laurens van Dorp and Tiara Sint Annaland.
- **The "22 Oct" post is dated 21 Oct 2026, 16:18 UTC.** The id decode and JSON-LD agree on that.
- **The next event is probably SOE on 4 Nov**, which is a B2B gym owner fair, not a consumer expo. It's slightly after "end of this month". This matters because they're already working on the gym channel the plan proposes. The plan should build on that rather than present the gym channel as new.
- **The Trustpilot disagreement isn't a disagreement.** There are two profiles. bebetterofficial.nl has 4.1 on 5 reviews, and bebetter-officiall.com has 4.7 on **39**, up from 37.
- **"13,998 fitness centres" overstates gyms by about 6x.** CBS counts 2,310 and NL Actief has about 1,200 members.
- **The 8% gym shop figure cuts both ways.** It can mean unmet demand, or it can mean most gyms don't want to stock clothing. Present it as the opening, and say gyms with no shop are the ones a done for you drop helps.
- **"Their site never mentions it" can't be shown live today.** The whole shop is behind the password page until 27 Oct.
- **These are unverified.** The FIBO stand number 10.2C72, "youngest brand owners ever at FIBO", and EVLS's "80,000+ visitors".
- **The 10 gyms and 3 coaches figure is from 29 Jun 2026.** It may be higher now. Ask them.
