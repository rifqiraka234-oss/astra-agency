## Rejected on the frame check (Free licence, but a mark or text is in shot)

Method. One frame per second on a contact sheet for every clip, then full resolution frames and enlarged crops wherever something could be a mark.

### The warehouse gym series (8 clips, one shoot)

All eight were shot in the same concrete warehouse gym. A row of round black discs runs along the yellow wall band and the discs read **"FSC"** alternating with a **running figure X logo**. Confirmed on a 3x crop of 52094 at 3 s and a 2x crop of 52084 at 6 s. That is the gym's own brand mark, so every clip where the band is in frame is out.
