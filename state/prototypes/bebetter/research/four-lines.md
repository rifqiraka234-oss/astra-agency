# BeBetter, the four lines (25 Oct 2026)

RULES section 5, written before any design. Sources are their own words, LinkedIn company page posts read raw on
25 Oct 2026 (research/li.html), the shop's password page (research/www.bebetter-officiall.com_), Laurens's headline in
the lemlist record, and the 24 Oct opener's research gate (state/drafted_2026-10-23-batch1.md).

## The custom kit page (the page the opener promised, the primary action)

1. **Who lands.** The owner or manager of a gym, a CrossFit box or a studio, or a personal coach, in the Netherlands
   and nearby. Most likely they met BeBetter at an expo (FIBO, the EVLS PRO SHOW, SIGGI'S) or saw a member wearing it,
   and are now looking them up. Their own first gym client came exactly that way, "someone we connected with during one
   of our events. He had picked up one of our shirts that day" (post of 26 Mar 2026).
2. **What they think, in order.** Do they make kit for a gym like mine? What would mine look like? Is the quality
   right, will my members actually buy it? Who else have they done this for? How many do I need, is there a minimum?
   How does it work and how long does it take? What does it cost? Who do I talk to?
3. **Their goal.** Merch their members want to wear and buy, that looks like their gym and not like a catalogue
   shirt, without having to design it themselves. In BeBetter's words, "a collection that truly represents their
   community" (29 Jun 2026), and the first one "Four hours later… Everything was sold out" (26 Mar 2026).
4. **The one action.** Send BeBetter a kit request they can answer with a design and a price. Gym name, the look they
   built on the page, pieces, colours, sizes and numbers, a date, a contact, their logo attached. Secondary path, the
   individual buyer goes to the drop.

## The home page

1. **Who lands.** Someone who saw the teddy at an expo or online, mostly a young lifter in the Netherlands, Belgium or
   Germany. Second, the gym owner, who needs a door.
2. **What they think.** What is this brand? Where do I get the teddy? When is the next drop? Is it for me?
3. **Their goal.** Get the pieces, be part of it.
4. **The one action.** Join the community for early access to the drop of 27 Oct (their own Instagram channel link,
   from their own password page). Gym owners get one clear door to the custom kit page.
