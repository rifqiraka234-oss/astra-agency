# The bar, rendered 30 Oct 2026

Every site below was opened in Chrome today at 1440 and 390. Each one got a first screen and two scroll depths, and its
motion stack was read from what it actually loaded (`_research/bar2/`, `_research/astra/`, `stack.json` in each).

## Astra's own, from Raka's list

19 of 20 rendered at both sizes. The Garnish Collective answers 500 today. The two signed off as the level:

- **BWILD.** The mark cut into the sunrise, the sky showing through. Huge italic caps, a sunrise that moves through the
  colour script, then a night of stars that are their runs. Something new on every screen, and Reserve always one tap away.
- **Honey It's Handled.** Their own wedding film full bleed, edge to edge, the light changing as you scroll. The type
  calm and expensive, the film doing the talking.

What both have that our last three looks didn't: the picture or film is the whole screen on every screen, the colour
changes as you go, and every screen has one thing moving that you've not seen before.
