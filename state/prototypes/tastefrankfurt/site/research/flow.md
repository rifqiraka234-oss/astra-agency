# The flow, Taste Frankfurt prototype

Refined from the brief's draft (section 10) for direction A, as built on 30 Oct. Their photos carry every screen, at least 40 percent of each and 60 of the opening, as Raka's brief asks. One row per step, the visitor's question,
what they see and do, the moment that answers it. Every step moves the planner one step toward one enquiry that arrives
filled in. Every line of theirs used is in `offer.json`, reopened against their pages on 29 Oct.
