02 Why a range

Eight in ten NE3 semis sold for between £159,000 and £579,000 in the year to July.

Same postcode district, same kind of house, and £420,000 from one end of that spread to the other. A single figure hides it. A range shows where your home sits, and a visit settles the rest.

03 How it's worked out

Your range is read off homes that actually sold.

STEP 1

Every recent resale nearby

We take each sale HM Land Registry recorded in your postcode sector in the last year. New builds and sales it flags as non standard are left out.

STEP 2

Brought up to today

Each sale is moved to July 2026 prices with the UK House Price Index for your council area and your type of home.

STEP 3

Your answers pick the slice

Bedrooms, condition and extras move your range up or down within those sales. They never add a figure of their own.

STEP 4

Then we visit

One of our Gosforth team sees what the records can't, inside and out. It's free, with no obligation.

Fewer than 30 sales of your type in your sector? The range widens to your whole postcode district, then to your council area, and your result says which it used.
