Circular furnishing isn’t the future any more. It’s a necessity.

More and more organisations want to depend less on ownership, use resources more wisely and meet their sustainability goals. In practice that’s complicated. It takes stock, know how on reuse, a reliable supply chain and partners who think along.

That’s why ReShare Living Group exists. To bring those loose links together in one clear model, with one way of working.

Government, housing associations and institutions are encouraged to make at least 50% of their purchasing circular by 2030. KeyPro, 16 October 2025

Our mission.

  1. Give furniture more than one life by making reuse the standard.
  2. Help organisations switch without big investments, by putting use above ownership.
  3. Make circular furnishing simple, so anyone can use it.
  4. Make the impact measurable, so organisations know exactly what it delivers.
  5. Be ready for 100% circular procurement in 2030, with a clear model that scales.
“For years we’ve been building an alternative to buying and throwing away, to make that huge mountain of waste smaller.”
