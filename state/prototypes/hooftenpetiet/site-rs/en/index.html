An example. Follow one sofa through the kinds of home KeyPro and Hooft & Petiet furnish.

Life 1

  1. KeyPro & Hooft & Petiet

    In the warehouse

    A sofa waits in the warehouse. A large stock of our own and efficient logistics make fast delivery possible.

  2. Hooft & Petiet

    Life one, a guest home

    Life two, an expat home

    For a company arranging homes for international staff. Back again, refreshed again.

  4. Hooft & Petiet

One way of working

Circular furnishing, without the hassle.

Stock, know how on reuse, a reliable supply chain and partners who think along. ReShare brings those loose links together in one clear model.

  1. Getting to know you

    How many homes, when, and who will live there?

  2. Proposal

  5. Back and again

    Collect, clean, check and use again.

Our mission

Five things we stand for.

  1. Give furniture more than one life by making reuse the standard.
  2. Help organisations switch without big investments, by putting use above ownership.
  3. Make circular furnishing simple, so anyone can use it.
  4. Make the impact measurable, so organisations know exactly what it delivers.
  5. Be ready for 100% circular procurement in 2030, with a clear model that scales.
