# Hooft & Petiet and ReShare, v3 brief. 25 Oct 2026

Raka on v2, "it feels like it's so plain and basic. What happened to like using all the pictures and animations and
gradients and being like 10 times more extra creative? … Now you've done the flow, but not the artistic value. It needs
to have a wow effect … something that they've never seen before … animations, graphics, shapes, … gradients, colors.
Search it up." v3 keeps v2's flow (the visitor, their questions in order, the one action) and adds the art.

purple glow), shapes that mean something (a canopy, a window), photos used big and inside type, motion tied to scroll.

## H&P, "lichten aan"

Their promise is that a home is "direct bewoonbaar zodra de sleutel wordt overgedragen" and Brenda's line, residents
only have to unpack their suitcase. v3 tells it as one day in a new home.
- **The day.** A living WebGL gradient behind the whole page, their lime, warm oak and a lilac from their indigo, with
  film grain. It moves from dawn at the top, through midday, to a dusk indigo evening at the close. CSS fallback.
- **Hero.** A giant Newsreader headline with their photos inside the words, cut as arch windows and their house shape.
  A rotating ring of text, "Projectinrichting sinds 2010", around the lime house from their logo. A warm lamp follows
  the cursor. The availability bar stays on the first screen.
- **The room.** As you scroll, the arch window after "bewoners" grows into the full room and the light comes on
  (the photo goes from dusk to daylight, a warm sun sweeps through). "Direct bewoonbaar, zodra de sleutel omgaat."
- **The verdict.** The residents' quote fills in word by word as you read it.
- **What you hand over.** A sticky arch frame on the left, the seven rows on the right. As each row comes to the
  middle, its own photo wipes in.
- **Logeer, rust of wissel.** The three words run as a giant line with a photo inside each, moving with the scroll.
- **Every home.** A concave curved panorama of their 2026 photos that turns as you scroll, the projects below.
- **Ook voor.** Arch topped photos.
- **The close.** Dusk, a lit window drawn in light, "Vertel ons over uw project.", the bar again.

## ReShare, "meerdere levens"

- **The life cycle.** A particle sculpture. 2,400 points form a chair, dissolve and re-form as a sofa, a lamp, a table,
  each one a new life with its counter, Leven 1 to 4. The cursor pushes the points. Their colours, KeyPro teal to sand.
- **A teal day gradient** behind the page, deep green at the close.
- **The ring.** A 3D ring of furniture and home photos turning, the circle that KeyPro's mark already is.
- **Two doors** that open wider on hover, each on its own moving gradient, KeyPro and Hooft & Petiet.
- **The reasoning** fills in word by word, the four steps on a circle with a light travelling round, the mission, Willem.

## Rules kept
Square buttons, the bar as the one action, every fact from research.md, no dashes or colons in copy, NL and EN.
Reduced motion and no JavaScript get a complete static page. Everything that moves writes transforms or CSS
variables only, canvases pause when off screen, device pixel ratio capped.
