# Hooft & Petiet and ReShare, v2 brief. 25 Oct 2026

Raka on v1, the points in his order. Keep the person most likely to land in mind, what they're thinking, their goals,
and guide the whole experience to the one action. v1 reads as a typical Claude or ChatGPT site, same styling, same
positions, same buttons, same word placement, information without purpose. Reinvent the style, research real sites
online. More expensive, more chic, easier, simpler. Break out of the colours and gradients. It feels like a 500 euro
site. Sizing and shape of buttons and words matter. Exclusive, premium, and it works on the intended audience.

## 1. The visitor and the action

### Hooft & Petiet
1. **Who lands.** The project leader on a renovation. A sociaal projectleider or bewonersconsulent at a housing
   association (Portaal, Stadgenoot, HEF Wonen, Cazas are theirs), or the omgevingsmanager at the renovation contractor
   (BAM Wonen and Heijmans are on their partner row). They have households who must leave home for weeks, or want a quiet
   room in the day, on a fixed renovation schedule.
2. **What they're thinking, in order.**
   - Can they have my number of homes ready on the date the contractor needs them?
   - Will my residents be all right? Complaints and the corporation's own satisfaction survey land on my desk.
     (Corporations run outside satisfaction calls during renovations, e.g. Eigen Haard's Rivierenhuis newsletter,
     July 2024, a 7,5. Renovations make the news when residents get bounced between corporation and contractor,
     AT5 on Stadgenoot Noord.)
   - What do they take off my hands? Keys, contracts, check in and out, cleaning between residents, one contact.
   - Have they done this for a corporation like mine?
   - What will it cost and how fast do I get a proposal?
3. **Their goal.** The renovation stays on schedule, residents are looked after and say so, and the project team can
   focus on the work. Danny Mooren of Portaal said it best, "Dit gaf ons de ruimte om ons te focussen op de renovatie zelf."
4. **The action.** Ask for availability and a proposal for their project (type, number of homes, from when, how long),
   or call the planning team on 085 2734 872. Second audience, the estate agent, goes to the verkoopstyling package
   with its price. Third, expat employers and others, a quiet "ook voor" row.

### ReShare Living Group
1. **Who lands.** Someone checking the group behind KeyPro and Hooft & Petiet. A procurement or sustainability lead at a
   housing association or council who has to buy circular (KeyPro, "landelijke doelstelling om in 2030 50% circulair in
   te kopen"), a partner, or a candidate (the group's marketing team works for both brands).
2. **What they're thinking.** What is ReShare and who's in it? Can they furnish at scale and keep furniture in use, and
   show it? Which of the two do I call for my job?
3. **Their goal.** Meet the circular target without hassle, with one dependable partner.
4. **The action.** Go to the right company, KeyPro for furniture rental and furnishing, Hooft & Petiet for homes ready
   to move into with the service around them. Or write to contact@reshareliving.com.

## 2. What the premium sites actually do (rendered 25 Oct, research/v2/shots, 28 of 37 loaded)

Measured on the live pages.
- **Buttons are square.** border-radius 0 on Aman, The Modern House, Inigo, Toteme, Gubi, Frama, Le Collectionist,
  Kering, Apparatus, Carl Hansen. 12 to 14px text, often uppercase with tracking, 35 to 48px tall, solid black or a
  1px outline. Text links underlined.
- **Grounds are white or a barely warm white.** Aman #F3EEE7, Studiopepe #FAF8F3, Audo #F5F3EB, Van Duysen #FFFFFA.
  Colour comes from the photos. Where there is a colour it's one flat field, never a gradient (Inigo's muted plum panel
  beside a room photo, Pastoe's flat blue and terracotta fields).
- **Type is quiet.** A classic serif upright (Lyon at Aman, Tiempos at onefinestay, Caslon at Inigo, Century Old Style at
  Audo) with a neutral grotesk (Whitney, Akkurat, Söhne, Univers). Headlines in regular or light weight, often centred,
  rarely bigger than 72px. Nav in 11 to 12px.
- **The logo sits in the middle** on The Modern House, Inigo, Toteme, Kinfolk, Apparatus, Plum Guide.
- **Few words per screen.** One image, one line, one action. Toteme puts one small product in an ocean of white.
- **The action is a bar, not a button,** where the visitor books something. Plum Guide, The Hoxton, Ace and Le
  Collectionist put destination, dates and guests in one bar under the hero.
- **Listings are calm.** The Modern House, photo, then name left and price right in small serif, a hairline, an
  outlined "VIEW ALL" box.

## 4. The v2 language, one family

- **Ground** paper #FBFAF7 and white. **Ink** #191817. **Grey** #6E6A64 for second lines. **Hairline** #E4E0D9.
- **One flat field per site, drawn from the brand, never a gradient.** H&P, a muted dusk indigo #2F2B45, a quiet
  grown up version of their #382888, used for the hero panel and the closing panel only. Lime appears only inside their
  logo. ReShare, a deep muted green #26332F that nods to KeyPro's teal, used once.
- **Type.** Newsreader (OFL, Production Type) upright for headlines, quotes and captions on H&P. Albert Sans (OFL,
  Scandinavian grotesk) for all interface text and body, and as the display face on ReShare, light weight. No italics
  as decoration.
- **Buttons.** Square, 48px, 12px uppercase tracked .14em. Primary solid ink with white text, or white on the dusk
  field. Secondary 1px outline. Links underlined, 1px, offset 4px. No arrows.
- **Forms.** Bottom border inputs, labels in 11px uppercase grey, a square selected state.
- **Layout.** Logo centred, nav split left and right in 12px. A strict 12 column grid with wide margins, asymmetric
  placement, real white space. Photos never carry text on top. Text sits beside photos or on the flat field.
- **Media.** Only their 2026 shoot and their best styled rooms, large, uncaptioned as places or people. Portrait frames
  next to text. Small 800px pictures only at small sizes.
- **Motion.** A slow fade and 2% settle on images as they enter, nothing else. Reduced motion shows everything still.
- **Prototype notice.** A thin line above the header and a line in the footer, no floating pill.

## 5. The pages

### H&P home, seven sections, each answers the next question
1. **Hero.** Split, their room photo left, the dusk panel right. "Uw bewoners pakken alleen hun koffer nog uit." One
   line under it, who it's for. Under the split, the project bar, soort woning, aantal woningen, vanaf, hoe lang,
   and "Beschikbaarheid aanvragen". The phone number beside it.
2. **The residents' verdict.** One centred quote from a logee on their page, "We hebben er geen woorden voor, helemaal
   top!", and the line that an independent bureau measures it for their clients, 8 to 8,5 (their page, 30 Jan 2020).
   Then the partner logos small and grey, still.
3. **What you hand over.** A portrait photo of hands placing a welcome card, and a quiet list from key to check out.
   Danny Mooren's line under it.
4. **Logeer, rust of wissel.** Three rows, the way The Modern House lists homes. Name, when it's used, what residents
   bring, a "Aanvragen" link that prefills the type.
5. **Projects.** Four rows, corporation, place, what was done, the number. Two quotes.
6. **Ook voor.** Four small pictures, verkoopstyling, expatwoningen, modelwoningen, calamiteiten, each a link.
7. **Close.** The dusk field, "Vertel ons over uw project.", the request button and the phone.
Footer, quiet, with ReShare.

### Verkoopstyling, for the estate agent

gallery photos and three short reasons. Close.

6. **Willem's line** on the green field.
