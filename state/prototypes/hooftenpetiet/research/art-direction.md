# Art direction, Hooft & Petiet and ReShare as one family

## One idea each, taken from their own words

**Hooft & Petiet, "alleen de koffer nog uitpakken".** Brenda de Wilde's line on their Cazas page, and their promise
"direct bewoonbaar zodra de sleutel wordt overgedragen". The whole site runs from an empty home to the moment a tenant
arrives with only a suitcase. Colour tells it, night indigo (their wordmark) lightening to linen daylight, with their
lime as the light that comes on when a home is ready.

**ReShare, "meubels meerdere levens geven".** Their own mission line. The whole site is a loop. One piece of furniture
travels round the circle through the homes the two companies furnish, and the counter says which life it's on. Re,
Share and Living, their own explanation of the name, open the page.

**The family.** Same type, same grammar, same linen and indigo. H&P's colour is lime (the roof of its house mark),
ReShare takes KeyPro's teal (KeyPro's circle), because ReShare is the loop KeyPro started. H&P's house and KeyPro's circle
are the two shapes, and each site uses its own as the hero object.

## Four things changed at once (the Qualigraf v4 rule)

1. **Palette and type.** Linen #F3EEE4 and paper #FBF8F2, ink indigo #1B1546 (a deeper cut of their #382888), their
   lime #C8D020 with a darker text shade #6E7400, KeyPro teal #2FA597 with text shade #1E7368. Fraunces (soft optical
   serif) for headlines with one italic phrase per headline, Instrument Sans for everything else. Both OFL.
2. **Page grammar.** Chapters edge to edge, handing over through colour, never cards on a grey page. Numbers set huge in
   Fraunces. Glass only where a thing is held (the quote form, the configurator).
3. **Story spine.** H&P, the renovation that moves a tenant out, the empty home, four steps to ready, the welcome, one
   point of contact, the projects, every kind of home, the people who hire them. ReShare, Re, Share, Living, the loop,
   why now, the two companies, one way of working, the mission.
4. **Media.** Their own 2026 photo shoot (warehouse, van, hands placing cushions and plants, finished rooms) full bleed,
   a house mask that opens into a room, an SVG floor plan that furnishes itself in their four steps, a day and night
   bar for logeer, rust and wissel, a price configurator on their own verkoopstyling prices, a loop scene for ReShare.

## From the teardown (45 of 50 rendered, 5 walled, research/teardown)

Interior brands (Muuto, Norm, Montis) give full bleed rooms and oversized type. Furnished living brands (Blueground,
Sonder, Habyt) give warm cream pages and a serif headline with a coloured second half. Circular brands (Interface,
Fairphone) lead with a statement and a life cycle loop. 42 of 45 use sticky scenes, 24 use clip masks, 17 use film, 2
use WebGL. Their own clients' sites (Stadgenoot, Woonstad) are standard corporate, so a premium H&P site stands above the
people who buy from it.

## Rules carried from earlier builds

Every scene writes CSS variables only and has a static state for no JavaScript and reduced motion. No layout class named
after a state class. Small text on a brand fill uses the text shade. Screenshots at 320 to 1920. The quote form sends
nothing in the prototype and says so.
