Why temporary homes matter now

A renovation starts with a move.

Housing associations have to deal with every rented home on an E, F or G energy label by 2028 at the latest. In heavy renovations residents can’t live at home for a few weeks. When work goes on around them, they want somewhere quiet during the day.

We furnish homes for both. You provide the home, we furnish it.

180.000

housing association homes still had an E, F or G label at the end of 2022, down by about a quarter from 247,000. All of them have to be dealt with by 2028.

Source Volkshuisvesting Nederland, based on the Aedes benchmark 2023

A guest home

A fully furnished home for the weeks residents can’t live at home. They bring clothes, toiletries and their own bedding and towels, everything else is ready.

  • Fully furnished, from beds to crockery
  • TV and wifi sorted
  • A small gift on arrival
  • Clean and fresh for every new stay

A rest home

For renovations done while people stay at home. During the day residents can work there in peace, or sleep after a night shift.

  • A cosy living room
  • Places to work
  • Several seating corners
  • Somewhere to cook

A swap home

For renovations that take longer. We fit the floors and curtains in advance, and residents bring their own furniture with them.

  • Floors and window coverings
  • Painting and lighting
  • On the contractor’s schedule
