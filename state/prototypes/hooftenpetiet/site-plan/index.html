02The market

The work runs to 2034 at least.

Corporations tackle nearly 90,000 homes a year. They agreed to improve every home with an E, F or G label by the end of 2028, and the average heat demand of their homes has to fall from 108 kWh per m² to under 94 by 2030 and under 87 by the end of 2034. In every project somebody decides whether residents stay home, rest somewhere quiet in the day, or move out for weeks. That’s the decision the Hooft & Petiet site has to win.

04The allowance

A furnished home is also a legal answer.

Tenants who have to leave for a renovation are owed a minimum moving allowance, €7,926 for moves since 28 February 2026. On 1 April 2022 the Hoge Raad ruled that a tenant offered a fully furnished and fitted swap home is in principle not entitled to that minimum, also when they turn a reasonable offer down. Location and quality, the length of the stay, whether belongings have to move and the tenant’s circumstances decide whether the offer is reasonable, and costs like transport and storage remain. That belongs on the Hooft & Petiet site, in plain words, checked by a lawyer, right next to the request.

10HubSpot

Every request lands in HubSpot.

Your current site already loads HubSpot. When the new site goes live, the availability bar and the quote form send their answers there as a deal, the kind of home, how many, the start month and how long, so whoever picks it up can answer with dates. Each request carries the page it came from, which is how you’ll see which pages win projects.
