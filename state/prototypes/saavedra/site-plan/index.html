Sell empanadas twenty at a time.

Saavedra already has a brand people photograph and a wood oven in the middle of the 9e. Companies just can't order from it yet. This plan shows the site we've sketched for that, the offices it's for, who else sells to them, and the first 90 days.

What the best catering pages do.

We opened 17 catering and private hire pages in Paris, London and the US, from Bouillon and Popchef to Pizza Pilgrims and Chipotle. Most name office food and company events. Hardly any name afterwork, and none covers office boxes, team lunch, afterwork, private hire and events together. That's the door the sketch opens.

The good ones put terms next to the offer. Chipotle shows $8.75 a person and a 10 person minimum in the first screen, Popchef promises a reply in under ten minutes, and Pokawa shows an estimate before it asks for an email. The box builder does the same with your card.

4 Why now

December is the caterers' best month. It starts in five weeks.

French caterers take 19 to 29 % more than their monthly average every December, and about 25 % less every February. WeWork, two minutes away, is already selling year end event space to the same teams.

7 The new site

Your colours, used as a day.

The idea comes from your own poster, menos palabras, más empanadas. Big words instead of paragraphs, your photos instead of explanations, and a quote that takes one step. The page runs through a team's day inside your opening hours, lunch in celeste and crema, 18h30 in rosa and gold, then night and the glow of the oven for private hire.

Works on a phone, in two languages.

Every page works from 320 pixels up, in French with an English switch, and the form checks itself as it's filled in. If sending fails, it hands over a ready written email so no request gets lost.
