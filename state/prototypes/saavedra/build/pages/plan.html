1 Where you stand

A brand people would wear, and no door for companies.

We read every page of saavedraparis.fr on 25 October. It sends people to pickup and to Deliveroo, and it stops there.

Strong

The brand already sells

A drawn sun, a gaucho and a runner, posters in Spanish, tees and bags. None of the eight other Argentine places in Paris we opened has a drawn world like it.

Teardown of 9 Paris Argentine sites, rendered 25 Oct 2026

Missing

No word for companies

Traiteur, privatisation, entreprise, événement and afterwork appear nowhere in the visible text of the home, menu or click and collect pages. An office manager looking for a team lunch has nothing to land on.

saavedraparis.fr, home, /menu and /click-collect, text and HTML read 25 Oct 2026

Missing

The menu is three pictures

The card is published as images, so Google can't read a recipe or a price, and neither can a screen reader.

saavedraparis.fr/menu, 25 Oct 2026

Missing

No hours, no reviews, no legal notice

The site shows no opening hours, no reviews or press, and no link to mentions légales or a privacy page, which French law asks of every business site.

The newsletter may not save sign ups

The newsletter block's code still carries Squarespace's note asking for a storage option. That usually means sign ups aren't kept anywhere. It's a two minute check in the editor.

Clasico wins on scale. Saavedra can win the small order.

Clasico's own catering page sends anything under 120 empanadas back to its click and collect. At three a head that's a team of 40. Every team smaller than that, the 12 people at a launch or the 25 at a Friday lunch, has no caterer built for it on this street.

What the best catering pages do.

We opened 17 catering and private hire pages in Paris, London and the US, from Bouillon and Popchef to Pizza Pilgrims and Chipotle. Most name office food and company events. Hardly any name afterwork, and none covers office boxes, team lunch, afterwork, private hire and events together. That's the door the sketch opens.

The good ones put terms next to the offer. Chipotle shows $8.75 a person and a 10 person minimum in the first screen, Popchef promises a reply in under ten minutes, and Pokawa shows an estimate before it asks for an email. The box builder does the same with your card.

9 Offices next door

A WeWork two minutes from the oven.

OpenStreetMap lists 15 coworking spaces within 1.25 km of 48 rue Condorcet. The nearest is the WeWork at 37 avenue Trudaine, 150 metres on foot. Each one is a room full of teams who eat lunch and celebrate launches.
