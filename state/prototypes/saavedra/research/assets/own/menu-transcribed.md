## Notes for the build

- **Counts.** 9 empanadas (5 carne, 4 verduras), all at 4. Boxes of 6 at 20,90 and 12 at 40,90 (a box of 6 works out at 3.48 each, against 24 at the single price). Two lunch formulas. 2 desserts, 3 sauces, 7 softs, 5 beers and cider, 5 wines. Three items carry a "new" badge, Spianata, Hongos and Salsa Verde.
- **Printed slips, kept as printed.** "Fugazetta (oignons confits, mozzarella origan, ...)" has no comma between mozzarella and origan. "Criolla" has a double space before "vinaigre". "Coca - Coca-zero" uses hyphens. In anything we write, our own copy keeps the no dash rule. Their product names can be quoted as printed.
- **Box wording to quote.** "en box de 6 ou 12, à partager... ou pas" is their own sharing hook, and the nearest thing on the site to a group or office order.
- **Third party drink names** (La Parisienne, Quilmes, Coca, Perrier, Evian, Susana Balbo, Finca la Niña, Bellevue la Forêt) appear on their menu as products they sell. They're fine as menu text, but don't use their logos.
- **Opening hours (not on their site, from their own ordering system).** The Zelty API behind their Click & Collect link, `https://api.web-ordering.zelty.fr/restaurants?locale=fr`, read 2026-10-25, returned restaurant id 9786, name "SAAVEDRA", public_name "SAAVERDA" (sic), address "48 RUE CONDORCET, 75009 PARIS", takeaway_delay 15, and hours Monday to Saturday 12:00 to 14:30 and 18:30 to 22:00, Sunday 18:30 to 22:00. That's one source, read once. Reopen it before any hours go on a page.
