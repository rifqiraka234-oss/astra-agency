# Saavedra, the four lines (RULES section 5, the visitor and the action first)

Written 25 Oct after Raka's rule landed on the branch, checked against the built pages.

1. **Who lands here.** An office manager, a team lead or a founder at a company of 10 to 50 people in the 9e, often in
   one of the coworking spaces around rue Condorcet (WeWork Trudaine is 150 m away). Second visitor, someone planning a
   private evening, a birthday or a leaving do.
2. **What they're thinking, in order.** Is this good food a whole team will like? Can they handle 20 of us? What does it
   cost per head? Is there something without meat? Do they deliver or do we collect? How far ahead do I need to ask? Is
   asking a hassle?
3. **Their goal.** Lunch or drinks sorted in one go, a price they can put in front of whoever pays, and a team that's
   happy with it.
4. **The one action.** Ask for a quote. On Entreprises it's the box builder handing its headcount to the form, on the
   home page "Pour l'équipe", on Privatisation the same form preset to private hire. The secondary path is ordering for
   yourself (Commander, Zelty, Deliveroo, Uber Eats).

How the pages answer them, in that order. Entreprises opens on the promise and the action, then the box builder (how
many, what it costs at card prices), then the walk (how close, collect or delivery), team lunch (per head prices),
afterwork, private hire, the FAQ (meat free recipes, delivery, how far ahead, drinks), then the one step form. The home
page puts the team's day straight after the hero and the quote straight after the day. The team teaser and the three
step block were cut, they didn't move this visitor.
