4 · The problem, in one line

Your funnel starts with a sales call. The people you sell to won’t book one until somebody has already told them they have a problem.

Every route on wistree.de ends at the same place, a fifteen minute Calendly. That works for the manager who already knows his knowledge is walking out the door. He’s rare. The rest don’t book, because booking means admitting a problem they can’t see yet.

This is the thing you agreed with in your reply. Every pilot conversation starts by convincing a Mittelstand manager that he has a problem he can’t see. That job is currently being done by you, live, one call at a time.
