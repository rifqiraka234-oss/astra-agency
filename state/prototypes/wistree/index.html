4 · What sits behind it

The check is the easy half. The comparison needs a service behind it.

What you used above is one file with no server. Scoring a set of answers is a few days of work. Telling a Werksleiter where he sits against other firms his size means knowing what everyone else scored, and that’s a different build.

The part that compounds

Every completed check writes a row. The instrument that acquires the customer is the same instrument that builds the dataset, so the comparison gets more defensible the longer it runs. A competitor can copy the six questions in an afternoon. They cannot copy four thousand rows.

The part we’d be honest about on day one

On launch there’s nothing to compare against. Most industry and size cells will be too thin to mean anything for months. The honest build shows the score alone and switches the comparison on per cell as it fills, which is a rule in the code rather than a promise in a meeting.

5 · Where else this goes

You already have engineers. This is where an extra pair of hands takes the longest items off their list.

So this isn’t us telling an AI company what AI to build. It’s the five things we’d pick up first if we were working alongside you, all of them on ground your own site already stakes out. Ohne implizites Wissen scheitert KI is your line, and every one of these is downstream of it.

01

Erfassung, the expensive step

Turning what Herr Bauer knows into a Wissenselement is the part that costs real hours. Interview recordings, Teams calls, shift handover notes, the SOPs nobody updated since 2019, all of it into structured draft elements with a human review queue on the end. The reviewer corrects rather than authors, which is a different job and a much faster one.

02

Antworten mit Herkunft

A chatbot that answers a Werksleiter is only as good as his willingness to act on it. So every answer carries the Wissenselement it came from, the process step it belongs to and the person it was captured from. Provenance is what turns a plausible answer into one he’ll run the line on.

03

Die Wissenslandkarte, die sich selbst zeichnet

Cluster the captured elements onto the process map instead of drawing it by hand, and let the criticality scoring run over it continuously. The WKI stops being a number someone recalculates for a report and becomes a number that moves on its own. That is also what makes a renewal obvious.

04

Erosionswarnungen

Watch for the Wissenselemente that only ever trace back to one person, then fire when that person changes role, reduces hours or crosses into their retirement window. It is the single feature that makes the whole product feel alive to a plant manager, because it tells him something he did not ask about and could not have known.

05

And the unglamorous half, which is usually what actually blocks a pilot

Pilot onboarding that doesn’t eat a founder’s week. Routing from a completed Schnellcheck to the right conversation, with the scoring attached, so nobody reads a spreadsheet to decide who to call. Internal dashboards over pilot progress. The customer portal. The websites and apps around all of it. This is the ordinary work that sits between a good product and a repeatable sale, and it’s most of what we do.

None of this is on the table today. The Schnellcheck is. We’re naming it because the check is the first brick of the same wall, and it’s worth knowing what the wall looks like before you lay it.
