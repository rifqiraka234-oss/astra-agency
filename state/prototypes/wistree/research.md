## The thesis (this is what makes the deck worth building)
The obvious read is "your website needs work". That is not the argument.

WisTree's funnel currently starts with a 15 minute sales call. The scale Karim is
building is not a later product feature, it is the top of the funnel, and it belongs
on the website in front of the Calendly. A consultancy already gives the "first
indication" away free in five minutes with no signup, so the indication alone is
already commoditised. What nobody offers is the **peer benchmark**, which is the exact
half Karim described. A score on its own is an opinion a Mittelstand manager can
dismiss. "You sit in the bottom quartile for firms your size in your industry" is a
fact about him, and it is the thing that makes him book the call.

This also solves the problem we raised on 13 Sep and he agreed with: every pilot
conversation starts by convincing a manager he has a problem he cannot see. The
benchmark does that convincing before he ever speaks to anyone.
