The rest of the Hertling presence.

My message named three gaps. Your Google listing has no reviews yet, your LinkedIn page has one follower, and your homepage names no client. The site deals with the homepage by showing proof you can show from day one, the documentation, the self check and a way for clients to review you. This page sketches the other two.

Sketches only. Nothing here is live, and no review, follower or client is invented. The sample posts are in German, the switch at the top shows them in English.

01

Google is where a Hamburg firm compares you first.

Search Google Maps for an IT Systemhaus in Reinbek and every other firm on the list shows stars. Yours shows none yet, and stars can't be bought or borrowed. So the profile has to carry proof of its own until the reviews come, and every finished job has to ask for one.

  1. Photos of the work, not of servers. The documentation, the WLAN plan, the logo. Real things a client gets.
  2. The four services, word for word from the site, so the profile and the site say the same thing.
  3. A short update every few weeks, the same posts as on LinkedIn, so the profile looks looked after.
  4. A review request after every job, the card below.
02

A review card left behind after every job.

One card on the desk when a job's done. The code opens the review page on the site, which explains it in three steps and shows the Google button to everyone, happy or not. Nobody gets filtered out, which is also what Google's rules ask for.

Scan it with your phone, it works.
