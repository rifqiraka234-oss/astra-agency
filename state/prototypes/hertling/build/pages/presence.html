Our readingSo the work is rarely convincing a firm it needs IT help. Three in four already pay someone, and only one in six has an IT person of its own. Hiring one is hard too, with 79,000 IT jobs unfilled in Germany. The job is giving a firm a reason to switch, and switching takes trust a new firm has to show rather than claim.

Microsoft Learn, the lifecycle page of each product and its yearly end of support list, read 22 Oct 2026. Windows Server 2022 only left mainstream support in October and runs to 2031, so it isn't on this list.

Why nowEvery end of support is a reason for a firm to call someone, and a moment it's open to a new name. Each date is a short campaign you can run, a check for the firms still on it.
