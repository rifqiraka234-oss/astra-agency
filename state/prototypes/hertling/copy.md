Anything that isn't his is marked [CONFIRM] here and in the handover. No colons, no dashes, contractions in the English.
Hyphenated German compounds are rewritten ("Mail" for E-Mail, "vor Ort" for Vor-Ort) so the no-dash rule holds. His
company name keeps its hyphen, it's a proper noun.

## Governing idea

His logo is two blue pillars joined by one grey band. His promise is "IT-Betreuung ohne Blackbox", one fixed contact
and every change written down. So the site is built on the band. One person runs between a company and its technology
and writes down every connection he makes, so the IT stops being a black box.
