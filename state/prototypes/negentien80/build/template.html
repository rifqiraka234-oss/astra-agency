    In English, for Ramona and Raka

    Seven steps to a price, then the nearest of your eleven dealers.

    A shopper goes from fabric to their nearest dealer in one sitting

    They pick one of your four curtain collections, enter the width of their rail and the height to the floor, then choose the pleat, the lining, how full it hangs, the rail and the finish. The drawing of their window and the price change with every choice, so they can see a wave next to a double pleat, or what blackout lining does with the light.

    Then they type a postcode or a town. The tool ranks your 11 full service dealers by straight line distance, shows the nearest one with its address and website, and would send the shopper's maatbrief and contact details there once it's wired up. That follows your own route, advice at the dealer, then measuring once the prijsindicatie is agreed.

    Every euro traces back to one price table

    Fabric metres are the rail width times the plooifactor, plus 10 cm of side hem per panel, rounded up to the next 10 cm. That's room height fabric up to 290 cm finished length. Above that it switches to 140 cm wide widths (banen), each cut 25 cm longer than the curtain.

    The price is the fabric metres times the fabric, making, lining and hem prices per metre, plus the rail per metre of rail width, plus one fixed fee for measuring and fitting. Every price is incl. 21% btw, and the btw share is shown as the total times 21/121. Sums run in whole cents, so nothing drifts in the rounding.

    Five things change before it goes live

    • Every price on the page. Fabric per metre for each collection, making per pleat, lining, rail, hem and the measuring and fitting fee of €95. If your dealer rekentool already holds your prices, the same table can feed this one.
    • The 290 cm room height limit, the 140 cm width for banen, the 10 cm side hem and the 25 cm cut allowance.
    • The pleats, linings, rails and finishes on offer, and the plooifactor range of 1,5 to 3,0.
    • The appointment form doesn't send or store anything yet. It needs wiring to each dealer's inbox, plus your privacy wording.
    • The noindex tag comes off at launch.

    Shoppers are wary of big purchases, so the price comes first

    Curtains are a big purchase. Every month since January 2024, more Dutch consumers have told CBS it's an unfavourable time for big purchases than a favourable one. The gap was widest in May 2026, at 45 points, and it was still 35 in September.

    Someone in that mood wants a number at home before they invite anyone round. Your N80 page describes exactly that in its entry for 2022, a price in seven steps on your own website.

    Points more people answering "unfavourable" than "favourable" to whether now is a good time for big purchases.     Everything else comes from your own site and public data

    • The four collections, their descriptions and fabric photos, from negentien80.nl/collectie.
    • The 11 dealers, their addresses and websites, from negentien80.nl/full-service-dealers. Where a detail differs between that page and your N80 Full service page, the tool follows the dealer's own website.
    • The route from price to montage, in your homepage's own order.
    • The 21% btw rate, from the Belastingdienst, belastingdienst.nl, tarieven en vrijstellingen (read 9 October 2026).
    • Postcode and town lookups use PDOK, the government's open address service, with a built in table of CBS postcode areas as a fallback.
