Info
Healthy Secrets is a family-owned Austrian functional food company at the intersection of nutrition, medicine, and everyday health.
Founded by a physician, a dietitian, and a legal expert specialized in medical law, Healthy Secrets combines medical knowledge, nutritional science, and product innovation to develop functional foods designed for daily consumption.
What makes Healthy Secrets unique is our fully integrated approach. Every product is conceived, formulated, developed, and manufactured by our own team. From the first idea to formulation, production, design, and branding, we build every product in-house.
We strongly believe that only by knowing a product inside out can a company truly stand behind its quality, purpose, and long-term credibility.
Our mission is to make preventive health simple and accessible through food. We develop products that fit seamlessly into everyday life, allowing consumers to support their wellbeing without changing their routines.
For retailers, pharmacies, and distributors, Healthy Secrets represents a fast-growing category within functional foods, driven by increasing consumer demand for science-backed, trustworthy health products.
With clear positioning, strong medical credibility, and a premium brand concept, Healthy Secrets offers partners a differentiated product range within the expanding preventive health and functional nutrition market.
We are currently expanding partnerships with retailers, pharmacies, and distribution partners across the DACH region.

Healthy Secrets started as a family conversation around health, nutrition and everyday life.
Our co-founder Nikolaus Walkner shares how the idea behind the company came to life.
Nikolaus Walkner
7 Monate
Healthy Secrets didn’t start in a food lab.
It started in our own family.
Our father is a pilot and, like all pilots, he has to regularly pass his pilot medical.
In that profession, health isn’t optional, it’s a requirement.
Over the years, conversations about nutrition and health became a recurring topic in our family.
My sisters, one a medical doctor, the other a dietitian, often advised him on how to maintain long-term health through everyday habits.
Watching those discussions unfold, I realised something surprisingly simple.
Most people actually know what healthy nutrition looks like.
The real challenge is integrating it into everyday life consistently.
Busy schedules, travel, work, time-zones, all the things that make healthy choices harder than they should be, especially for pilots.
That insight eventually led the three of us to found Healthy Secrets .
My sisters bring medical and nutritional expertise.
My own background as an attorney specialising in corporate and medical law shapes how we approach building the company and the regulatory side of functional foods.
Our goal is simple:
to make preventative nutrition easier to integrate into everyday life.
No supplements.
No complicated routines.
Just functional foods designed with medical and nutritional expertise, but made for real life.
The photo was taken at the annual meeting of Austro Control, where our father was attending as a pilot.
My sisters joined him there (one behind the camera) in a moment that reflects how the idea behind Healthy Secrets grew out of real conversations within our family.
Founding Healthy Secrets never felt like starting “just another food brand”.
It feels like turning something we had talked about at our family table for years into something real.
And in many ways, that’s what drives us today.
