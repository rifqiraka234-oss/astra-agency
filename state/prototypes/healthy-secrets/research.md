## B, claims and red team

**Health claims.** The EU only allows nutrition and health claims from its register (Regulation 1924/2006; conditions
read on the Commission's page, `research/ec-nutrition-claims.html`, "HIGH FIBRE ... at least 6 g of fibre per 100 g",
"SOURCE OF FIBRE ... at least 3 g"). Their product pages carry no nutrition tables, so no claim can be checked against a
threshold. The site therefore states ingredients, sizes, preparation and their own warnings, and leaves out efficacy
claims ("nachweislich", "Cholesterin senken", "Diabetesprävention", "Darmkrebs"). The origin story is told without the
father's blood test result. This matters more than usual, Nikolaus is a medical law attorney. Flagged in the handover.

**Red team.** Right person and company, yes (Impressum names him). Pivot, no, the café opened 17 Oct. Commercially
important, yes, the café is their newest revenue and the site hides it. Intentional, no, the site predates the café.
Realistic, the café needs to be findable from Google and Instagram links, which point to the site.

## C, art direction

**Concept (C1).** "Ein ganz normaler Tag." The site is one Viennese day from 8 Uhr to 22 Uhr, told the way their own
About page tells it, granola in the morning, the Gut Health Club when it opens at 10, a matcha for the afternoon dip,
Moon Milk and tea at night, each moment showing where something three siblings made slips into a routine nobody has to
change. Only this company could own it, because it's their founding sentence and their café sits in the middle of it.

It controls: composition (the home page is a timeline of the day), colour (their palette mapped to the hours, blush
morning, sky late morning, pale tile blue at the café, sage afternoon, navy evening, gold keys at night), typography
(big times in Austrian form, "10 Uhr", "8.30"), imagery (real photos ordered by time of day), copy (is(s)t headlines),
interaction (day dial, quiz, booking), navigation (shop filter by Tageszeit), iconography (clock, keys), motion (the sun
moving along an arc as you scroll).

**Three adjectives.** Ärztlich (a surgeon and a dietitian decide the recipe, Gastro.News). Verspielt (mascots, Main
Character Energy, Instagram pills). Wienerisch (Wildpretmarkt, Schanigarten, Familienunternehmen).
**Anti.** Clinical, luxury spa, supplement shop.
**Metaphor.** The day, and the key (secrets you get to keep).
**Why not generic.** No serif display (the last three builds used Cormorant, Libre Caslon, Cormorant Garamond), no mono
labels, no cream and rust, no card grid as the default, arches instead of rounded rectangles.

**Type (C2).** Jost (OFL, Futura revival) for everything, light 300 for long copy because their body face is Futura LT
Light, 500 for UI, 800 for display. One family set with discipline instead of their seven. Hours set huge in Jost 200
with tabular figures. Loaded from local woff2 files.
**Colour (I9).** High chroma on purpose, their own. Text shades darkened for AA where needed.

**Collision test (C4) against the last three builds.** Garnish (Cormorant, Italiana, Pinyon script, cream and green),
Enrich Education (Libre Caslon, Poppins, IBM Plex Mono), Hire Quality Talent (Cormorant Garamond, Manrope, navy and gold).
Overlap: navy appears in HQT, here it's one hour of the day, not the brand base. Gold appears in HQT, here it's their logo
colour. No shared type, no shared layout grammar.
