## What v1 was missing, in one line each

- No light. Flat forest everywhere, nothing glows, nothing drifts.
- One colour. The romantasy world is jewel tones, v1 had green and gold only.
- The logo was a small image, never the hero.
- The book pages were plain ivory, no ornament, no gilt, no drop caps.
- The invitations were boxes with borders. Invitations in this world are foil, gilt and wax.
- The bars were rectangles.
- The finish was a QR and a paragraph.
