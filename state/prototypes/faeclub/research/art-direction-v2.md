# Fae Club v2, the art layer (25 Oct 2026, evening)

The flow in research/flow.md stays, every question in the same order, one ticket carried down the page. What changes is
everything the eye gets. References in research/award-v2.md.

## The idea, from their own mark

Their logo's C is a crescent moon with a fairy sitting on it, scattering stars from her open hand. So the page opens on
that moon, huge, in gold, in a living night sky, and her stars keep falling. "Who will you be?" is answered by her: tap a
character and her stardust gathers into a portrait at her fingertips. Scroll, and the moon sinks, the book rises, and
the portrait is on its cover. The mark is traced from their own 2048 px logo file into a vector (build/art), nothing
redrawn, nothing invented.

## The colour script, one night

Their forest and gold stay the base. The romantasy world their guests read is jewel tones (the Sarah J. Maas site,
FairyLoot), so the night moves through them as you scroll. One sky behind the whole page, a WebGL shader of slow aurora

## Signature moments

1. **The moon and her stars.** Their traced mark at the height of the screen, gold foil with a slow sheen, a warm halo.
   Stardust leaves her hand in a stream. A tap on a character sends a burst from the word to her hand, and the portrait
   forms in the dust. Scrolling sinks the moon and raises the book with the same portrait on its cover.
2. **An illuminated book.** Parchment pages with gilt edges, gold corner flourishes, a foil drop cap on every page, the
   photos inside Moorish arches like the Spanish Hall's own. The pages still turn with the scroll.
3. **Light in the hall.** The last page opens into their hall photo with beams falling from the skylights and motes in
   them, the line in gold foil.
4. **The circle.** Coming on your own lights places in a ring of their guests, like a dance about to start.
5. **Foil invitations.** The two tiers as velvet invitations with gilt borders, a holographic sheen that follows the
   pointer and a slight tilt. Royal is closed with a wax seal that says Sold out.
6. **Three arches of gold.** Pay in 3 as three Moorish arches, each filled a third with liquid gold. Pay in full fills
   one arch to the top. The retreat's months use the same arches.
7. **The seal.** Paying presses a wax seal onto the ticket, the QR gathers from gold dust as before, and gold leaf bursts
   out.
8. **Light everywhere.** Gold dust drifting at three depths behind the page, a fairy light at the cursor on desktop,
   fine film grain, foil on every headline, ornament dividers between chapters that draw themselves.

## Rules kept

- No italic second halves, no dash kickers, no pill buttons, no rounded soft shadow cards, no count up numbers, no
  marquee. Buttons stay square.
- Gradients are light in a night sky, foil on gold and liquid in an arch. Never a purple to blue hero, never a dark
  overlay on a photo except a fade from the side the words sit on.
- Every animation has a still state. Reduced motion draws the sky once, stops the dust, keeps the book flat. Without
  script the sky is a CSS gradient, the moon is still there, every word and price is on the page.
- Their photos only. The moon and fairy are their own mark. No stock, no film.
