/ AI cooling in Germany

AI data centre capacity in Germany is set to quadruple by 2030

Bitkom put AI data centres in Germany at 530 MW of connected load in 2025 and expects 2,020 MW by 2030. Nearly all of that power leaves the servers as heat, and the loops that carry it away run on manifolds, pressure vessels and heat exchangers like ours.

/ The efficiency law

From July 2026, new German data centres have to reach a PUE of 1.2

Under the Energy Efficiency Act, a data centre that starts operating from 1 July 2026 has to reach a PUE of 1.2 within two years and reuse at least 10 percent of its energy. Warmer return water is easier to reuse, so the cooling form asks for supply and return temperatures up front.

  1. 1 Jul 20261.2

    PUE for new data centres, plus 10 percent reused energy

  2. 1 Jul 20271.5

    PUE for data centres that opened before July 2026. Those opening from this date plan at least 15 percent reuse

  3. 1 Jul 202820%

    Planned reused energy for data centres opening from this date

  4. 1 Jul 20301.3

    PUE for data centres that opened before July 2026

Source. Energieeffizienzgesetz (EnEfG) § 11, as published on gesetze-im-internet.de, read 9 October 2026. PUE is power usage effectiveness, annual average.
