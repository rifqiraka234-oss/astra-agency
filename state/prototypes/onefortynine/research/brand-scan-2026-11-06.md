# Brand scan, 104 homepages, 6 Nov 2026

Raka 6 Nov, "Check out their websites online. I love like things like Apple ... Nike ... scrape like 100 like top 100 pages".
104 brand homepages loaded in Chromium at 1440 x 900 (UK URLs), first screen screenshotted, the first heading and first
filled button measured, and first screen lightness measured from the pixels. Script and raw data in the session scratchpad
(brands.js, brands/data.json), not committed.

**What loaded.** 65 real pages. 32 behind a bot wall (Cloudflare and similar, titles like "Access Denied", not bypassed), 6
failed to load, 1 blank. Patagonia served an outage page, so it counts as loaded but tells us nothing.

**What they do.**
- **The page is white or near white on 60 of 65.** The colour comes from photography and film, not painted backgrounds. First
  screens split 20 light, 19 mid, 26 dark, and almost every dark one is a dark photo or film on a white page (Apple's first
  screen was a MacBook on black that day).
- **Film above the fold on 21 of 65.**
- **No italic headings on any of the 65.**
- **Buttons.** Of 59 measured, 33 square or nearly (4px or less), 10 slightly rounded (5 to 19px), 16 pills.
- **Headings.** Brand grotesks almost everywhere (SF Pro, Söhne, Helvetica Now, Airbnb Cereal, Geist, Graphik, Aktiv
  Grotesk, Unica, Apercu, Lausanne). Weights split, 400 (19), 700 (17), 500 (8). Median first heading 40px, the heroes
  that lead with type run 64 to 104px.
- **Where there's a gradient it's one vivid signature on white** (Stripe's ribbon), not a dark wash.

**The relevant ones, looked at.** Apple, Nike, Airbnb, Center Parcs, Hoseasons, Sonos, Headspace, Hay, Glossier, Pret,
Innocent, Oatly, On, Stripe, Tiffany. Hospitality and holiday brands (Center Parcs, Hoseasons, Pret) lead with bright
real-life photography on white.

**Applied in v3.** White page, near black Mona Sans headlines, black buttons at 10 to 12px (soft, not pills), the film as a
large rounded tile beside the headline (Apple and Nike product page layout), his box on a light studio sweep, and one
signature gradient from his own orange and red, used for the place word, the markers, the money and one colour band.
