# OneFortyNine Games, the host page. Visitor, action and flow (1 Nov 2026)

Promised in the thread, Owen said yes 1 Nov 09.09 Amsterdam to "a sketch of the host page", the host page that "shows
what a box earns per stay and takes the order online" (our opener, 1 Nov 07.54 UTC).

## The four lines (RULES section 5)

1. **Who lands.** A holiday let or glamping host, one to a handful of cottages, pods or lodges, often the owner who also
   does the changeovers. Second, the manager of a holiday park or small hotel looking at several boxes. Third, someone
   booking a team day for a leadership team (Owen's own tagline, "Helping holiday parks & leadership teams get people off
   screens and working together"). They arrive from Owen's LinkedIn, an outreach message, or his stand at the Land
   Leisure & Tourism Show, NEC Birmingham, 4 to 5 Nov 2026 (banner on his home page, read 1 Nov).
2. **What they're thinking, in order.** Their own words where Owen's page already asks them, his four questions on
   /breakoutbox, "What's in the box?", "How does it work for our business?", "What do we get?", "How much does it cost?".
   Before those, "will my guests actually play it", and after them "what would it earn me" and "how do I get one".
3. **Their goal.** Something that makes a stay memorable and gets reviewed, that earns its keep, and that costs them no
   time on changeover day.
4. **The one action.** Order a box, rent from £60 a month or buy from £350, online, the form arriving filled in with what
   they set on the way down. Secondary, book Owen's 15 minute call (the park manager, anyone unsure). Teams, book a game
   day through the same call.

## The flow, one row per step

| Step | Visitor's question | What they see and do | The moment that answers it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is this? | Their box, full bleed, their wordmark, "Think escape room meets unboxing." The first input, how many stays a month | The locks click open as they set it |
| 2 | Will my guests play it? | Their photos of the box being played, their own line "that yes we did it moment as each lock clicks open" | The lid opens on scroll, light comes out |
| 3 | What's in the box? | The box opening in layers, each layer a lock and a prop holding snacks or drinks, their words | Unboxing, prop by prop |
| 4 | How does it work for our business? | Guests book with the host, the host sets the price, the box goes back in the cupboard | A stay told in three beats, arrival, the rainy evening, the review |
| 5 | What do we get? | Exactly what offer.json says is included | Each item checked into the box |
| 6 | What would it earn me? | Stays a month (from step 1), share of stays that play, price per game. Sliders that are the graph | A row of stays, the ones that pay the box off turn into keys |
| 7 | How much does it cost? | Rent or buy, read off the same state, payback for each | The cheaper route for their numbers marked |
| 8 | How do I get one? | The order, already filled in, rent or buy, boxes, property, postcode | Sending it locks the box and labels it for dispatch |
| 2nd | I run a park, or I'm booking a team day | Several boxes, or a game day | Book the 15 minute call, filled in |

## The one state

{ stays a month, share that play, price per game, rent or buy, boxes, property type, postcode, start month }. Every
control writes it, the hero number, the key row, the cost panel, the bar and the order form read it.
