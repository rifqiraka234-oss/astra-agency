      What's the catch?

      The questions every venue asks first.

      How much space does it take?

      It's a 70 litre cool box, under a metre long. It doesn't need a permanent spot, and it fits in the boot of a car.

      Who has to run it?

      Nobody. Set up takes under 10 minutes, then your guests play on their own for 60 to 90 minutes. If they get stuck, the online hints help them.

      What does it cost, and what's your cut?

      From £60 a month to rent, or £350 to own outright with no ongoing cost or subscription. Guests book with you, you set the price and the contents, and there's no revenue share.

      Will people come back to play again?

      Every box comes with two sets of the latest game, and new games are on the way, so your regulars get something new to crack.

      What if something breaks or goes missing?

      Extra supplies are there to order whenever you need them, and you get product support from the people who make it.

      Why not a digital game or VR?

      No screens, no headsets and no tech to break. Your guests talk to each other, and the reaction is what they tell their friends about.
