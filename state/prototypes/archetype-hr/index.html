Before you meet with Alex

  • Lead with specific recognition. Recognition is Alex's strongest motivator at 88 percent, and none has been logged this period.
  • Keep feedback direct. Alex's profile shows a preference for specific, promptly delivered feedback over saved-up formal review comments.
  • Offer visible ownership. A small initiative Alex can lead publicly will land better than a private thank you.
  • Reference the trend, not just the moment. Alex is already improving. Naming that keeps the conversation forward looking.
