## What the go to market system is, in his words (Kyson 7 Oct, Acquitas 9 Oct, the 16 Oct batch)

- "a modern go-to-market ... system so he can outreach, autom- automize and reach ... I reach out to like, more than 30
  people, 50 people a day" (16 Oct, on James, and "Aaron ... maybe also go to market as well").
- "our go to market architecture. Which is basically setting up Claude, setting up Lemlist ... getting the messaging
  right ... getting the lead hooks right" (7 Oct).
- "the hot list of ... founders that you can already outreach ... based on the ICP ... an assistant to ... what the
  message should be and also the researching ... research helper ... LinkedIn and email automation ... signals" and
  "integration with ... Lemlist ... to identify who the website visitors are" (9 Oct).

So the desk carries all of them. The hot list (today's founders from the ideal customer profile), the signals (the
eight dot fit check and the sources behind it), the research helper (why each founder fits), the message drafting
(every first message written for that founder and that call), LinkedIn and email sending with follow ups, and the
guide readers turning into warm founders on the list, which is where the content half and the outreach half meet.

## Writing (all sessions)

- No AI sound. "this sounds like ai built this, so like make it more flowing" (9 Oct). "yeah bro lol its still sounds
  like AI" (22 Sep). Contractions, plain words, no colons, no dashes, no "not X but Y", no triads for rhythm.
- The messages inside the desk follow the same rules, since Aaron will read them as the messages his company sends.
- "So it doesnt look like we're an ai scraper" (15 Oct). The drafted first messages in the desk say what the founder
  is building in a human way and never list scraped facts.
- English only. No country of the partner. Raka is the contact.
