## The whole thing in four lines

BidX writes Innovate UK grant applications for startups, £7,500 for one, plus 2.5% of the grant if it's won.
Their channels have gone quiet, the Meta ads stopped in December 2025, the blog is empty, and both founders now run
an AI app builder called Panda. We built them a desk that finds founders who are likely to win and writes Simon's
first message to each one. The pitch is that we run that engine for them, not that we build them a website.

## Lead with the growth engine, never with "we build sites"

Both founders sell against agencies right now. Panda's homepage says "Panda replaces $50K+ developers and marketers
so non-technical founders can get to revenue faster" (panda.new). Panda's LinkedIn About says "They charged you
£15,000 for a weekend's work." Aaron has built AI products for years (Combine.AI, now Panda). So "we build
websites and apps" walks straight into the thing they mock.

What they can't do with Panda is the ongoing work. Pulling UKRI's winners file, watching Companies House for new
share filings, keeping 28 open calls and their dates current, judging fit per call, writing in Simon's voice every
morning, and tuning it from the replies. That's the sale. A growth engine run for them, with the desk as its
cockpit.

Don't mention Panda unless they do. Knowing it is for framing, not for saying.
