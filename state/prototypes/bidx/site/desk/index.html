Who’s never picked

  • Already a BidX client, or in a live proposal
  • Contacted in the last 90 days, by anyone at BidX
  • Asked us not to get in touch
  • Registered outside the UK, or dissolved
  • A call that closes inside three weeks
  • No technical people on the team

The eight dots

  1. UK companyRegistered and active at Companies House
  2. Small enoughAn SME, so the call is open to it
  3. In scopeWhat it builds sits inside the call
  4. Something newReal development work, not resale
  5. Team to deliverTechnical people to run the project
  6. Can fund its shareA sign it can cover the match
  7. Time to applyMore than three weeks before the deadline
  8. Free to contactNot a client, not contacted in 90 days

From lemlist to a booked funding call.

lemlist finds the founders and sends the messages. Claude checks every founder against the calls that are open and writes each message. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

Every working day

  2. 8.30amToday is ready to read

  4. As they landReplies are sorted and an answer drafted
  1. 1lemlist

    Find

    Two lists come first. Past Innovate UK winners whose project ends in the next six months, from UKRI’s published list, and companies that have just filed new shares at Companies House, the public trace of a raise. Both go into lemlist as signals, the People Database finds the founder, and watch lists add hiring and guide readers.

    UKRI winners listCompanies House filingsPeople DatabaseWatch lists
  2. 2Claude

    Match

    Every morning Claude runs the eight checks against every call open on the Innovation Funding Service and the EIC. It starts founders in the week a call opens, around its briefing, and never on a call that closes inside three weeks. It reads their site, Companies House record and LinkedIn, and holds anyone a rule stops, with the reason.

    Eight checksLive call listNever picked rules
  3. 3Claude

    Write

    For each founder who fits, Claude writes the connection note, the first message and one follow up in Simon’s voice, with the call’s own figures. They’re saved on the lead in lemlist as custom fields the campaign reads.

    lemlist custom fields
  4. 4BidX team

    Approve

    You read them on Today, edit what you like, and approve or skip. Approved founders go into the lemlist campaign. A skip keeps its reason, so the rules get sharper.

    Today view
  5. 5lemlist

    Send

    lemlist sends up to 20 connection requests a day from Simon’s LinkedIn, which keeps inside LinkedIn’s limit of about 100 a week. The rest of the day’s founders get the same message by email from Simon’s address, to a work email lemlist finds. Weekdays, 9.40am to 4.30pm. One follow up three days later, then nothing.

    Replies land in lemlist’s inbox, LinkedIn and email together, with lemlist’s own read on interest. A webhook hands each one to Claude, which sorts it into wants a call, a question, later or not for us, and drafts the answer.
