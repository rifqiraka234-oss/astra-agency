Smart grants are closed. Here’s what to apply for instead

Innovate UK has stopped taking Smart applications. The calls open now are narrower, most close before Christmas, and some put the odds at 2%.

What happened

Smart grants have closed for new applications, and UKRI’s guidance page says why in one line.

“Smart grants are now closed for new applications as funding is being aligned to Innovate UK’s new strategic direction.”UKRI, guidance for specific funds

So the question changes. It used to be “is my idea good enough for Smart?” Now it’s “which of the calls that are open is written for what I’m building?”

How Smart went

Innovate UK’s own evaluation of Smart, published in July 2026, counted 1,688 projects awarded £531m of public funding between 2019 to 2020 and 2024 to 2025. The businesses it funded raised £3.6bn of equity after their award, by the end of 2024.

It also shows how hard it was. Of 21,782 applications matched in the evaluation, 1,295 were funded. 6,427 scored above 70, which is typically a passing score on other Innovate UK programmes, and weren’t funded. 1,439 of those scored 80 or more.

Sources, Smart grants impact evaluation, final report and technical annex, Ipsos for Innovate UK, July 2026.
What that means for the calls open now

A strong application isn’t the bar. The bar is the marks, question by question, from three assessors who only know what’s written down.

If none of them fit

Growth Catalyst

The investor partnership programme for seed to Series A startups has no round open. UKRI’s page lists two competitions in 2025 to 2026 and says “Further competitions subject to confirmation.” The investor partnership calls that are open are in our Investor Partnerships guide.

Innovation Loans

Loans of £100,000 to £5 million are paused. The latest brief says “Applications for Innovation Loans will pause from 11am 9th October 2026 until January 2027.”

EIC Accelerator

UK companies can apply for the grant only, up to €2.5 million. Short proposals can go in at any time and are batched on the first Tuesday of each month, so the next is 3 November.

Knowledge Transfer Partnerships

Round 4 closes on 27 January 2027 and is led by a university, research organisation or Catapult, with a business as the partner.
