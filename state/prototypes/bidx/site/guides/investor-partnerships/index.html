How it works

An investor partnership pairs an Innovate UK grant with equity from one of Innovate UK’s selected investor partners. The grant pays for the R&D, the investment backs the company, and the grant depends on the investment.

Across its investor partnership programmes from 2017 to November 2025, Innovate UK says it awarded £168 million to 417 SMEs, 105 investor partners made or led £448 million of aligned equity, and those SMEs raised £1.36 billion more afterwards.

Source, UKRI, investor partnerships, updated 22 September 2026.

Battery Innovation Programme

A single UK SME, invited to apply by an investor from Innovate UK’s pool of selected investor partners. Project costs depend on the kind of research, £50,000 to £300,000 for a feasibility study, £100,000 to £1 million for industrial research and £250,000 to £2 million for experimental development. Grant rates are up to 70% for a micro or small company on feasibility and industrial research, and up to 45% on experimental development.

Engineering Biology, round 1

A single UK SME developing engineering biology products or services, seeking private financing of at least twice the grant it asks for. The grant request is £250,000 to £2 million, the project runs 24 months, and the aligned investment led by the investor partner must always be at least twice the grant.

What decides it

In the last Growth Catalyst round, which runs on the same investor partnership model, the brief named three things a decision rests on. Innovation, assessed by independent assessors from your application. Additionality, how much the grant speeds up your innovation and draws in the investment. Traction, how likely the investment is to complete in reasonable time.

Our advice

Start with the investor. On the battery call you can’t apply without their invitation, and on engineering biology the investment has to be led by one of the selected partners.

And Growth Catalyst?

Growth Catalyst is the same model for seed to Series A startups. Its last round, for later stage startups, closed on 3 February 2026, and UKRI says further competitions are subject to confirmation.

Innovate UK’s own worked example for that round shows the scale. A £2 million experimental development project, a £900,000 grant at 45%, and £1.8 million of equity into the business before the project starts.

Sources, Growth Catalyst round 2 brief, applicant briefing slides and Innovate UK Business Connect.
