BridgeAI Deployment Catalyst, the three strands

A grant of £100K to £200K for an AI SME with a working product and a larger organisation that will put it to use. Opens 21 October and closes 8 December at 11am.

Who can apply

BridgeAI Deployment Catalyst is for AI that already works and is ready to be put to use by a larger organisation. The rules are the same across all three strands.

  • The lead is a UK registered AI SME with an existing AI or ML solution at technology readiness level 5 or 6, with a functioning minimum viable product.
  • Each application includes one deployer, a UK registered medium or large business or a UK based public sector organisation.
  • You may add one more partner, so the consortium is two or three organisations.
  • The lead accounts for between 40% and 70% of the project’s total eligible costs.

Which strand is yours

Pick the strand by what your system does once it’s deployed. Each has up to £5 million and the same dates.

Operational systems

“UK registered SMEs can apply for a share of up to £5 million to deploy Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning solutions that perform, control or coordinate operational activity.”

For example scheduling, routing, robotics on a line or a farm, energy and network control.

The operational systems brief on the Innovation Funding Service

Decision critical systems

“UK registered SMEs can apply for a share of up to £5 million to deploy Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning solutions that support consequential judgements, recommendations or decisions.”

For example clinical triage, credit and risk calls, safety decisions, planning in health or transport.

The decision critical systems brief on the Innovation Funding Service

Assurance and compliance

“UK registered SMEs can apply for a share of up to £5 million to deploy Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning solutions that monitor, test, verify or evidence compliance with requirements, standards or controls.”

For example checking documents against rules, testing other AI systems, evidencing controls for regulators.

The assurance and compliance brief on the Innovation Funding Service

The deployer carries half the story

In our reading, the question behind every scored answer is whether this AI will really run inside a larger organisation within three to five months. The deployer is how you answer it. A named medium or large organisation, the part of its operation the system goes into, and what it will measure.

Our advice

Get the deployer’s commitment in writing before you start the application. Without one, the application can’t go in at all.

The money, worked through

Grant rates are up to 70% for a micro or small organisation, 60% for medium and 50% for large. Put in your own numbers to see your company’s share.

How it’s scored

The application has 19 questions. The first nine aren’t scored, and the other ten are, each one by three independent assessors with expertise relevant to the project. In Innovate UK’s assessor guidance for Frontier AI SME Champions, another AI call open now, each scored question is marked from 1 to 10.

The brief doesn’t state the odds for BridgeAI. Across the AI calls that do, Frontier AI SME Champions says 2% and Efficient, Structured and Controllable AI Systems says 4%.

Five checks before you start

  1. Your product is at TRL 5 or 6, with a working MVP you can show.
  2. A medium or large UK business, or a UK public sector body, will deploy it and will say so in writing.
  3. You know which strand describes what the system does.
  4. Your share of the costs sits between 40% and 70%.
  5. You can fund your company’s share, at least 30% for a micro or small company.
