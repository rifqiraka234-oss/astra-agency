The rules, in Innovate UK’s words

Opens
Monday 21 September 2026
Closes
Wednesday 21 October 2026 11am UK time
Funding type
Thirdparty
Project size

Your Discovery Phase Project must have a total requested funding of no more than £200,000 exclusive of VAT.

Length

The Beta Phase of a Project focuses on the large scale demonstration of the solution to the problem, and the duration of the Beta Phase will depend on the scale and complexity of the solution demonstrated. Beta Phases will range between six months and five years and represent the largest scale phase of the Project. The Deployment phase must have a proven solution to be deployed into business as usual.

Who can apply

This competition is open to collaborations only.

To lead a Discovery Phase Project, you must be an Ofgem licensed electricity or gas distribution network, transmission operator, or National Energy System Operator (NESO).

How it’s scored

The Expert Assessors will review your answers for each question. The Expert Assessors will score your responses against the SIF Eligibility Criteria.

Your application will be reviewed by three independent assessors based on the content of your application and their skills or expertise relevant to your project. All of the scores awarded will count towards the total score used to make the funding decision unless you are notified otherwise.
