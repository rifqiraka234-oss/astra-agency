Good applications lose too.

Innovate UK’s own evaluation of Smart grants matched 21,782 applications from February 2019 to April 2024. 1,295 were funded. 6,427 scored above 70, which counts as a pass on other Innovate UK programmes, and still missed out.

Three independent assessors read each application and mark every scored question from 1 to 10. The gap between a good application and a funded one is in those marks, and that’s the part we write for.

1,439scored 80 or more, not funded

14,060the rest, not funded
Each dot is 100 applications. Source, Smart grants impact evaluation, technical annex, Ipsos for Innovate UK, July 2026. Assessor marking from the Frontier AI assessor guidance on the Innovation Funding Service.

Fewer small companies are winning, so every mark counts more.

UKRI supported 2,393 small and medium businesses in 2025 to 2026, down from 3,563 two years before. The award rate across UKRI fell from 27% to 19% over the same two years.
