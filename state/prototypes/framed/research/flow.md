| Step | Kian's question | What he sees and does | The moment that answers it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Home, first screen | What is this? | His December film, his brackets drawing round "This December, Framed asks first." Run this week's search, Open the desk | The brackets close round his own question turned round |
| 2 Home, the booth | Is it real? | His booth counts down, flashes, prints three strips, real companies with a dated reason and a source link | A strip with a company he knows, from Rotterdam |
