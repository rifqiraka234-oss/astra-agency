What we build

The desk in your colours, the two Claude runs, the lemlist campaign on your LinkedIn, and the list of sources for the Randstad.

What you bring

Your LinkedIn, connected by you. A lemlist plan in your name. Ten minutes on a Monday and a tap when someone answers.

What stays safe

Public business news only. Nothing goes out without your keep. A stop is final. Email, when it comes, goes from a separate domain so your own mail stays clean.

Before a single message goes out, we work out who books you.

The desk is the part you see. Underneath it sits the strategy we build with you in the first week, and keep sharpening on what actually replies and books.

01

Who fits, your ideal customer

The companies most likely to book a €299 booth, written down so the search only brings you those.

  • Near you first, the Randstad, then the rest of the country
  • Teams of about 30 to 1,000 people
  • A reason to celebrate in the next twelve weeks
  • One person who runs the events

Built from your past events, SS Rotterdam, Erasmus, Modefabriek and the rest, then tuned on who books

02

The signals, how we spot them

The public reasons that mean a company is planning something, where each one is found, and how fresh it has to be.

  • Job ads with events in them
  • Anniversaries and jubilees
  • New offices, moves and openings
  • Funding rounds and hiring waves
  • Galas and drinks already in an agenda

Every signal dated and linked, nothing older than four months

03

The pitch, one angle per reason

What to say depends on why they're celebrating, and when to say it.

  • An anniversary, capture the year
  • A new office, the housewarming
  • A new events person, their first party
  • A gala in the agenda, is there a photobooth yet
  • Funding, the first party with everyone

LinkedIn first, one short bump, then quiet. A reply playbook for yes, a question, not now and stop
