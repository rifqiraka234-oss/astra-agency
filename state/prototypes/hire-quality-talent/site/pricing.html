Ask a question
Do you work on a no win, no fee basis?

Yes, no win, no fee options are available. Tell us about the role and we'll say which arrangement fits it.

How long does a search take?

A typical search takes 4 to 8 weeks, depending on seniority and market conditions. You get milestones and updates throughout.

Why are your fees lower than a traditional agency?

Our lean, independent model means senior, hands on recruitment support without the traditional agency fee structure.

Can you work alongside our own talent acquisition team?

Yes. Whether you have an established team that needs more capacity or no recruitment resource at all, we step in wherever we're needed, for one difficult hire or a whole project.

Which roles and markets do you cover?

Our roots are in PPE and safety, workwear, tooling and power tool accessories, manufacturing and engineering. We recruit across sales, commercial, marketing, operations, HR, technical, leadership and C suite roles, in the UK, Europe and internationally.
