02 The market right now

48k manufacturing vacancies

Half the roles of 2022, and more candidates on the market.

UK manufacturing vacancies fell from 95,000 in spring 2022 to 48,000 this summer. In their September report, recruiters said candidate supply was rising at its fastest pace in three months, and engineering was one of only two sectors where permanent vacancies rose.

So an advert gets more CVs than it used to. That isn't the same as getting the right one.

03 Why the pile doesn't help

In manufacturing, more than a third of vacancies are hard to fill for skills.

The people applying often don't have the skills, qualifications or experience. The ones who do are usually busy doing the job somewhere else.
