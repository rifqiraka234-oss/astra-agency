## Method, so this can be re-tested

**What was pulled.** `get_campaigns` and `get_campaigns_stats` for all ten campaigns. Four
pages of `linkedinInviteAccepted` for v0.1, one for v0.2, two for Luna. Twelve pages of
`linkedinSent` unfiltered by campaign, 1,115 records. Then **116 individual
`get_inbox_conversation` calls**, which is every single candidate plus controls.

**The error caught on the way, and it would have been a bad one.** The first pass
classified a contact as messaged if any send record carried a message body. That produced
54 candidates. Pulling the first six showed all six had real openers in their threads,
including Gerard Ouattara who had a full prototype delivery and a decline. **The activities
endpoint omits the body for campaign attributed sends and only returns it for manual inbox
sends**, so a body was never a valid test. Rebuilt on record counts instead, which were
verified to match the thread exactly on five threads read independently.

**The falsification pass.** Filtering sends by `campaignId` drops every manual inbox send,
since those carry `campaignId: null`, and that is the real cause of the old 298 record
under report. The unfiltered pull covers 12 Feb to 22 Sep and contains the four 2 August
messages a previous pull was missing.

**The independent confirmation.** For the 242 v0.1 contacts with two or more sends, a check
for anyone whose every send landed within 60 seconds of acceptance returned **zero**, so
none of them is a stack of connect notes with no real message hiding inside it.
