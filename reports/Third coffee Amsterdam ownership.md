# Tracing Third's owners stops short of El Encanto

"Third." (styled with a full stop, sold as "Modern Bakery and Coffee") is a specialty coffee and pastry bakery at **Wielingenstraat 22, 1078 KK Amsterdam**. Its website is **third.amsterdam**, not third.nl. It is operated by **Third Grounds B.V. (KvK 97420980, registered 2 July 2025)**. Its registered director is **Deap Bouwvisie Holding B.V.**, whose sole director is building engineer **Jürgen Codfried**. The business's public founder and operator is Amsterdam hospitality entrepreneur **Andrew El Safoury**. A post reposted on Third's own LinkedIn page names El Safoury as "founder" and says Codfried and the post's author, **Robert Guerain**, are investors "behind the scenes". Codfried and El Safoury have worked together since at least 2019, so Third is best read as their latest joint venture. The shareholders are not public, and one further company officer is hidden behind a paywall. On the second question, **no evidence links Third's owners, company, contractors, contact details, websites or suppliers to Restaurant El Encanto** (KvK 91130379), a steakhouse partnership at Scheldeplein 18 whose only named partner is "Ayad, E.". The two businesses are neighbours 59 m apart in the same 1931 perimeter block. They probably share a housing-corporation landlord (de Alliantie) and one façade-renovation permit. That makes them co-tenants, not related businesses. The only remaining uncertainty is one unnamed officer at each company, and two KvK extracts costing a few euros each would settle it. For outreach, contact Third through Andrew El Safoury, treat El Encanto as a separate account that has probably not started trading, and never imply a link between the two.

An earlier reading that the two units have "separate owners" should be dropped. What remains is two commercial tenants of one large corporation, not a business relationship.

One hypothesis needs to be named so it can be set aside. Adhoc described the 2023 buyer of Djago as "an experienced hospitality entrepreneur from central Amsterdam", and El Safoury fits that description. **The idea is weak and unverified** for five reasons. First, the description fits hundreds of operators. Second, the one named El Encanto partner, Ayad, has been a partner since the VOF was founded in 2023, which matches the buyer's timing without needing anyone else. Third, El Safoury's own venue list and Deap's project list leave out El Encanto and Scheldeplein. Fourth, El Encanto's domains sit with a different registrar and share no contact handle with the Mijndomein accounts behind El Safoury's and Codfried's domains ([SIDN RDAP](https://rdap.sidn.nl/domain/el-encanto.nl); [SIDN RDAP](https://rdap.sidn.nl/domain/adamgroup.nl)). Fifth, every search pairing El Safoury's name with El Encanto, Ayad or Scheldeplein came back empty. The idea that Deap was El Encanto's permit adviser is in the same position. It is plausible because Deap sells exactly that service, but no document supports it, since Amsterdam's notices never name applicants.

### Two KvK extracts and one Kadaster check would close the case

The residual doubt comes down to two unnamed people, and you can resolve it yourself. First, buy a **KvK uittreksel for 91130379** (Restaurant El Encanto) at kvk.nl. It names both VOF partners and their entry dates; the historical version also shows who joined or left on 29 January 2026. Second, buy one for **97420980** (Third Grounds B.V.). It names the second officer and, if there is one, a sole shareholder; a Dutch B.V. with several shareholders does not publish them in the register. The research put each extract at a few euros. If neither hidden person is Codfried, El Safoury, Guerain, Ayad or one of their companies, the "no connection" verdict becomes close to certain. Third, a **Kadaster eigendomsinformatie** lookup for parcels Amsterdam **V 6780** and **V 6707** (about €3 each) settles whether de Alliantie owns both units. For secondary checks, the permit files name applicants and advisers. You can request them from vth.administratie.sdz@amsterdam.nl (cases Z2025-054524, Z2024-042092, Z2025-021370, Z2025-041168) and horecavergunningen.sdz@amsterdam.nl (Z/26/3157323, Z/26/3157598), as the notices themselves say ([Gemeenteblad](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/gmb-2026-75521.html); [Gemeenteblad](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/gmb-2026-420280.html)). The most useful change is in who counts as the owner. The register shows an engineering holding with €1 of assets, which on its own reads like a passive shell.
